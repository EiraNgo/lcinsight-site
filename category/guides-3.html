SEC vs. CFTC: who regulates crypto in the US, and where the line now falls

The SEC oversees crypto that counts as a security, and the CFTC oversees commodities like bitcoin and ether plus derivatives on them. Since March 2026, the two agencies share an interpretation that says most crypto assets are not securities.

A TGE, or token generation event, is the moment a crypto project creates its token and the first slice starts to circulate. Airdrop claims and trading usually begin then, while team and investor tokens stay locked for months or years.

Bitcoin halving explained: what it is, when the next one is and why it matters

The bitcoin halving is a rule in bitcoin's code that cuts the reward for mining a new block in half every 210,000 blocks, about every four years. The last one happened on April 20, 2024, at block 840,000. The next is due at block 1,050,000, expected around spring 2028.

How crypto exchange hacks happen: the weak spot is rarely the key itself

Most exchange hacks start in hot wallets, the online wallets that pay out withdrawals. Attackers rarely break the cryptography. They get into the systems and people that approve transactions, then rush to swap stablecoins before issuers can freeze them.
