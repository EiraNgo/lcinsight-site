Compound's fourth retreat in a month: a vote to pull 90 ETH and 42,000 USDC off Unichain

A Compound DAO vote closing November 3, with no votes against so far, would send reserves from its deprecated Unichain markets back to Ethereum. A bigger 534,530 USDT withdrawal from Polygon was filed and canceled within an hour.

Aave V3.8 vote: 381,439 AAVE for, zero against, a new lock on 15 networks

Aave Labs wants a reentrancy guard on every active Aave V3 market, built so flash loans and liquidations still work. AAVE holders backed it with zero AAVE against in a Snapshot vote that closed October 31; audits come before the final onchain step.

November 1, 2026

Uniswap opens the tool behind its UNI burns to any app. Robinhood takes USDC instead

TJAR, the system that turns Uniswap's protocol fees into UNI and burns it, is now open to everyone. It handled more than $15.3 million in a month, and its first outside user wants stablecoins, not UNI.

NYSE owner ICE wants Hyperliquid perps in America. What stands in the way?

Intercontinental Exchange plans to run Hyperliquid-based perpetual futures markets for US retail traders with Trade[XYZ], and is taking a minority stake. HYPE jumped almost 20% on the day, but nothing goes live until US regulators approve it.

October 29, 2026

Ethena Pay adds Earn+, a leveraged USDe strategy on Aave V4, without publishing a rate

Two months after launching a money app on Avalanche, Ethena added Earn+, which supplies USDe and borrows USDT through a vault on Aave V4. There is no stated rate, and deposits are capped at $20,000 to $200,000 by tier.

October 28, 2026

RLUSD's big week: a federal trust bank on Monday, an A2 Moody's rating on Tuesday

Ripple's $2.5 billion stablecoin won final OCC approval for a national trust bank to hold its reserves, then got an investment-grade A2 rating from Moody's a day later. It still trails PayPal's PYUSD.

October 28, 2026

Aave weighs a 60 million cap for USAT on Avalanche, where its supply is zero

Aave Labs wants USAT, the Anchorage-issued dollar stablecoin built with Tether's technology, on Aave V4's Avalanche hub. Risk firm LlamaRisk backs it as borrow-only at a fixed $1, but flags an uncapped mint key and a bridge that is not live yet.

Circle earned $1.4 billion and kept about 7 cents per dollar. Where did the rest go?

Circle says 59% of its first-half 2026 revenue went to the exchanges and apps that distribute USDC. A September filing shows how one of those deals, with Binance, works.

Native bitcoin as Aave collateral? Babylon's plan starts with a 300 BTC cap

Babylon Labs wants Aave V4 to accept bitcoin that never leaves the Bitcoin chain, with no wrapper or custodian. Aave Labs says the integration works and LlamaRisk has set a 300 BTC starting cap, but a Snapshot vote is still to come.

Sentora, not Aave's usual risk providers, now picks the collateral and rules for three Ethereum lending markets on Aave V4. Users can borrow RLUSD, OUSD and PYUSD, and the DAO keeps half the revenue under Sentora's proposal.
