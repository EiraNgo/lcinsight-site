FinCEN and the Fed drop the 2020 plan to cut the travel rule threshold to $250

The 2020 proposal would have lowered the cross-border threshold from $3,000 to $250 and written crypto into the definition of "money." FinCEN pulled it on November 6, 2026.

November 6, 2026

Greece puts a 10% crypto gains tax to a final vote. Staking income would pay 15%

Greece's finance minister brought a bill with dedicated crypto tax rules to a final vote in parliament on November 4. Gains up to 500 euros a year would be tax free.

November 6, 2026

The CFTC cleared perpetual futures on US stocks. Cboe and Citadel filed legal action that day

On November 3, CFTC Chairman Michael Selig said perpetual futures on single US stocks can trade on CFTC-registered exchanges for the first time. The same day, Cboe, Citadel Securities and Healthy Markets Association took legal action to stop it.

November 4, 2026

A7A5 stablecoin network moved $179 billion. FinCEN wants US banks and crypto firms out

A Treasury proposal would bar about 347,926 US financial institutions, crypto exchanges included, from moving money for the Russia-linked A7 network's front companies. Public comments close November 4, 2026.

November 2, 2026

Kenya's crypto grace period ends November 4: KSh 25 million fines for unlicensed firms

Crypto firms already serving Kenya had one year to get licensed under the new VASP Act, and that year ends Wednesday. From November 4, 2026, a company operating without a license risks a fine of up to KSh 25 million.

November 2, 2026

Brazil's crypto license deadline has passed. From November 6, banks must drop unlicensed firms

Crypto firms already operating in Brazil had until October 30, 2026, to file for central bank authorization. From November 6, banks and payment firms may no longer deal with unauthorized providers, and late filers get 30 days at most.

November 2, 2026

Code is not a shield: the FCA's draft crypto map covers DeFi, comments due November 18

The UK regulator wants views by November 18, 2026, on draft guidance about when DeFi, stablecoin payments, market making and tech services need a license. Using smart contracts does not settle the question on its own.

November 1, 2026

Tokenized gilts as margin? The Bank of England says yes; stablecoins must wait

The Bank of England says clearinghouses can already accept tokenized versions of collateral they take today, and set out what they must check first. Stablecoins, tokenized money funds and tokenized gold get questions, not approval.

October 29, 2026

One year in the High Court: HTX settles with the FCA and promises to stop UK ads

HTX, formerly Huobi, has given court-backed undertakings not to unlawfully promote its crypto services to UK consumers, the UK regulator says. The case began in October 2025 and ended with a confidential settlement, no admission of wrongdoing and no announced fine.

October 29, 2026

Dubai's VARA reviewed every 2025 reserve report. Now auditors face seven checks

Two VARA circulars in October tell licensed crypto firms what a real reserve audit must prove, and warn that vague website claims like "protected" and "segregated" are not enough.

October 29, 2026

US sanctions North Korean IT workers in Russia and lists 8 crypto addresses, 5 on Tron

Treasury designated 10 people and a Russia-based center on October 28, 2026, saying they moved crypto for North Korea's IT worker schemes. One Ethereum address appears under two different men.

October 28, 2026

Washington sues Utah: the CFTC's 10th state fight over prediction markets

The US derivatives regulator went to court to stop Utah from shutting down federally registered prediction markets under state law. It is already fighting nine other states, and the outcome decides whether these markets are one national product or 50 local ones.

October 28, 2026

Hong Kong's SFC calls it a world first: tokenized money market funds now trade 24/7

The SFC let two ChinaAMC tokenized funds trade around the clock on a licensed crypto platform. A week later the HKMA moved its own tokenized settlement rail to 24/7 and said regulated stablecoins are next.

October 27, 2026

No interest, par redemption, stress tests: Singapore drafts its stablecoin law

MAS wants to ban interest on MAS-regulated stablecoins and recognize a few foreign ones. Seven weeks later, the central banks of Malaysia and Thailand joined its BLOOM project for cross-border digital money.

October 24, 2026

Stablecoin issuers need OCC approval by January 18, 2027. Applications are not open yet

The US bank regulator finalized its GENIUS Act rule on October 21, 2026. Issuing a US payment stablecoin without approval becomes a crime in January, and existing issuers will lean on waivers.

October 22, 2026

Japan's FSA wants an on-chain finance roadmap by early 2027. Who is at the table?

Japan's Financial Services Agency has opened two new tracks in three weeks: a cross-ministry forum on on-chain finance in the AI era, and a study group on digital and decentralized finance that heard from Nomura and Securitize first.

October 21, 2026

USDC is the first of 45 EU stablecoins called 'significant.' What changes on November 12?

The European Banking Authority has classified Circle's USDC as a significant e-money token under MiCA, the first in the EU. From November 12, 2026, the EU banking watchdog takes direct charge of the reserves that back it.

October 14, 2026

Thailand's SEC: 11 crypto ETF rules, then a block on Vcex.io from November 7

In three October days, Thailand's Securities and Exchange Commission set rules for local Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs, filed a criminal complaint over the unlicensed Vcex.io exchange and said access to it will be blocked from November 7. The message: use the licensed route or be shut out.

Out by January 8? ESMA tells EU crypto firms to drop non-MiCA stablecoins

The EU's markets watchdog says licensed crypto firms should stop offering stablecoins that break MiCA rules and clear old client positions within three months. Warning customers is not enough.

Could Americans trade crypto with leverage at home? The CFTC opens a 60-day consultation

With the CLARITY Act stuck in the Senate, the CFTC on October 5 asked the public how to regulate retail crypto trading on borrowed money at federally registered exchanges. Comments are due around December 12, and nothing is binding yet.

October 6, 2026

The SEC would let advisers hold client crypto keys, as 'Crypto Mom' Peirce bows out

A new SEC proposal would let investment advisers hold client crypto themselves when no custodian will, and let state trust companies act as custodians. The same day, the agency announced Commissioner Hester Peirce's departure.

UK crypto firms have until February 28 to apply. The FCA says no license is automatic

Every crypto firm that wants to keep serving UK customers after October 25, 2027, now needs full FCA authorization. The window opened on September 30, 2026, and firms that miss its February 28 close risk losing the right to sign up new customers.

Can a token buyback make a coin a security? SEC staff say it hinges on one word

New SEC staff FAQs say a buyback on a working, "functional" network is not a promise of managerial effort. Projects that are not yet functional are warned off marketing buybacks as yield.

The SEC will let tokenized stocks trade in DeFi-style pools for five years. The catch?

A new SEC exemption lets approved onchain venues trade tokenized US stocks through permissioned liquidity pools without registering as exchanges. Companies can veto their own listings, volumes are capped, and only vetted users get in.

The Digital Asset Tax Certainty Act would end tax on small network fees, simplify the tax on dollar stablecoins and bring wash-sale rules to crypto. Congress's own scorekeeper says it raises about $1.5 billion over seven years.
