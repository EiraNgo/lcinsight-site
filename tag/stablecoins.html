Stablecoins news and analysis from LC Insight: 15 stories, newest first.

Exchanges

Asian exchanges lost $11.95 billion in three weeks, most of it USDT leaving Binance

DeepSea's data shows a steady stablecoin exodus from Asian exchanges starting September 28, weeks before Binance was hacked. Western exchanges barely moved, while smaller dollar tokens began to swing.

Security

Binance lost a record $6.3 billion. Who froze $2.7 billion of it?

Attackers emptied Binance's hot wallets on October 29 in the biggest crypto theft on record. Stablecoin issuers froze about $2.7 billion, the FBI blames North Korea, and Binance says users will not pay.

October 30, 2026

Infrastructure

BNB Chain's Jenner hard fork walls off 5% of every block for payments. Who gets in?

Jenner went live on the BSC testnet on October 28, 2026. It reserves block space for payments, adds a native token standard with freeze and seizure powers, and gives smart contracts millisecond time. Mainnet has no date yet.

October 29, 2026

DeFi

RLUSD's big week: a federal trust bank on Monday, an A2 Moody's rating on Tuesday

Ripple's $2.5 billion stablecoin won final OCC approval for a national trust bank to hold its reserves, then got an investment-grade A2 rating from Moody's a day later. It still trails PayPal's PYUSD.

October 28, 2026

DeFi

Circle earned $1.4 billion and kept about 7 cents per dollar. Where did the rest go?

Circle says 59% of its first-half 2026 revenue went to the exchanges and apps that distribute USDC. A September filing shows how one of those deals, with Binance, works.

October 27, 2026

Policy

No interest, par redemption, stress tests: Singapore drafts its stablecoin law

MAS wants to ban interest on MAS-regulated stablecoins and recognize a few foreign ones. Seven weeks later, the central banks of Malaysia and Thailand joined its BLOOM project for cross-border digital money.

October 24, 2026

AI & RWA

4.40% on a token: SoFi launches the first national bank-issued tokenized deposit for consumers

SoFi Earn (SOFIE) turns a dollar on deposit at SoFi Bank into a token that pays 4.40% and keeps FDIC coverage. Swap it for SoFi's stablecoin to spend or send, and that coverage ends.

October 22, 2026

Policy

Stablecoin issuers need OCC approval by January 18, 2027. Applications are not open yet

The US bank regulator finalized its GENIUS Act rule on October 21, 2026. Issuing a US payment stablecoin without approval becomes a crime in January, and existing issuers will lean on waivers.

October 22, 2026

AI & RWA

Mastercard lets AI agents pay their own way: nine payments, one budget, no invoices

Mastercard opened early access to Agent Pay for Machines, which lets AI agents buy services on their own within a spending limit set by a human. It runs on the x402 standard and can settle in cash or stablecoins on Base.

October 20, 2026

Policy

USDC is the first of 45 EU stablecoins called 'significant.' What changes on November 12?

The European Banking Authority has classified Circle's USDC as a significant e-money token under MiCA, the first in the EU. From November 12, 2026, the EU banking watchdog takes direct charge of the reserves that back it.

October 14, 2026

Policy

Out by January 8? ESMA tells EU crypto firms to drop non-MiCA stablecoins

The EU's markets watchdog says licensed crypto firms should stop offering stablecoins that break MiCA rules and clear old client positions within three months. Warning customers is not enough.

October 8, 2026

DeFi

Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, Coinbase and Shopify launch OUSD, and hand partners the interest

Open Standard's dollar stablecoin went live on September 30 with more than $1 billion in launch liquidity and over 200 partner companies. Partners, not the issuer, keep most of the reserve income. Circle is not on the list.

September 30, 2026

Infrastructure

Polygon claims 11 million payments per second. Almost none of them touch the chain

Polygon Labs says its new agent pay channels processed more than 11 million payment updates per second in tests. The number is real, but it measures offchain hubs on a devnet, not Polygon's blockchain.

September 25, 2026

Policy

Crypto fees of $10 or less could go tax-free as House panel backs bill 38 to 5

The Digital Asset Tax Certainty Act would end tax on small network fees, simplify the tax on dollar stablecoins and bring wash-sale rules to crypto. Congress's own scorekeeper says it raises about $1.5 billion over seven years.

September 17, 2026

Kraken's parent plugs into SoFi: 24/7 dollar settlement and a bank-issued stablecoin

SoFi will route some crypto trades for its 15.8 million members through Kraken Prime, and Kraken will list SoFiUSD, a stablecoin issued by SoFi Bank. Kraken's institutional clients also get dollars that settle at any hour, weekends included.
