What is Chainlink? How DeFi gets its prices, plus CCIP and LINK staking

Smart contracts cannot see the outside world, so lending apps rely on oracles like Chainlink to tell them what ether is worth. Chainlink also moves tokens between chains through CCIP, and LINK pays and backs the node operators who do the work.

What is a layer 2? Rollups, proofs and why no big L2 is Stage 2 yet

A layer 2 is a separate blockchain that runs transactions cheaply and settles them on Ethereum, using fraud proofs or validity proofs to show its state is correct. L2BEAT tracks about $30 billion in L2s, and its Stages show how much control each team still holds.

Lisk shuts its chain October 31 and burns a quarter of LSK. Tokens left behind are lost

Lisk set October 31, 2026, as the last day of its own blockchain and its DAO. It is burning 100 million LSK, a quarter of supply, and is turning the token into a loyalty reward on Ethereum. Anything not bridged out in time is lost, the project says.

Four years after the Nomad Bridge hack, a man is extradited to the US over $2.89 million

Prosecutors say Alexander Gurevich ran four exploit transactions against the Nomad Bridge on August 1, 2022, taking 103 WBTC and 300 WETH. It is the second old DeFi exploit case to reach a US courtroom this month, three weeks after the Uranium Finance hacker's conviction was announced.
