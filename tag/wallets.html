Seed phrase and hardware wallet safety: the words that guard your crypto

Your seed phrase is the master key to your wallet: anyone who sees it can take everything, and if you lose it, nobody can restore it. A hardware wallet keeps keys offline, but only if the phrase stays offline too and the device is genuine.

What is address poisoning? The copy-paste scam hiding in your wallet history

Address poisoning is a scam where an attacker plants a lookalike address in your transaction history and waits for you to copy it. No hacking is needed, only a tiny or fake transfer and a hurried user, and the payment cannot be reversed.

Look up your wallet address in an approval checker such as Etherscan's Token Approvals page or Revoke.cash, find spenders you no longer use or do not recognize, and revoke them. Each revoke is an on-chain transaction, so it costs a small network fee.

CEX vs DEX explained: who holds your crypto, and what can go wrong

A centralized exchange (CEX) is a company that holds your coins and matches orders in an order book; a decentralized exchange (DEX) is blockchain software that lets you swap from your own wallet against a pool of tokens. The real trade-off is custody: convenience and support on one side, control and responsibility on the other.
