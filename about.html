About LC Insight

Read the chain, not the rumors.

LC Insight covers crypto markets, companies, policy and on-chain activity in plain English.

What makes LC Insight different

  • Receipts, not hearsay. We never cite other news outlets. Every story shows its receipts: the official documents, the on-chain data and the market data behind each number.
  • The chain first. We read wallets, exchange flows and smart-money moves directly (DeepSea, Arkham, Nansen, Dune, DefiLlama, block explorers) and show what the money did, not what people said.
  • Said vs did. We put what crypto influencers post next to what their wallets actually do.
  • Rumor vs record. When a claim goes viral, we check it line by line against the official record and mark each part true, wrong or unverified.
  • Plain English, drawn as a story. Short pieces a beginner can follow, with art that shows who moved what to whom, and one-minute videos.
  • Asia and the world. We watch regulators and exchanges in the U.S. and in the countries where most crypto users live.

How we report

  • We only publish information that is public. Every figure, date and quote is linked to its source.
  • We go to the source first: the company, the project, the regulator, the court or the blockchain itself.
  • Facts and views are kept apart. Our own reading of events appears only in the section marked "LC Insight analysis".
  • We do not name private individuals or repeat accusations that officials or the companies involved have not made.
  • Illustrations are drawn by LC Insight. They are cartoons, not photographs, and do not depict real people.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and show the update time on the article. Send corrections to us on X.

Privacy

We use Microsoft Clarity to see which stories readers find useful (pages read, time spent, how far down the page people scroll). Clarity collects usage data through cookies and similar technology; it does not give us your name or email, and we never sell data. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
