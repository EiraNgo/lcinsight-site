infiniFi airdropped 8.3% of INFI on Tuesday. The token is 88% below its first trade

The yield protocol sent INFI straight into eligible wallets on Ethereum and Base on November 17, 2026. Trading opened near $0.92 and slid to about $0.10 by midnight ET, which puts all 100 million tokens at roughly $10.6 million.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • infiniFi said 8.3% of INFI's supply went to early users from its first two rewards seasons, and registered users got tokens directly in their wallets on November 17, 2026. There is no claim page to visit.
  • CoinGecko's five-minute data show INFI's first real trades at about $0.92 at 10:35 a.m. ET. By 12:40 a.m. ET on November 18 it was about $0.106, down 88%.
  • CoinGecko lists a total supply of 100 million INFI, so the whole supply is valued at about $10.6 million. The 8.3% airdrop is worth about $880,000 at that price, against about $7.6 million at the first trade.

How the INFI launch worked

infiniFi, a decentralized finance (DeFi) protocol that pays yield on dollar deposits, launched its INFI token on Tuesday, November 17, 2026. In a press release issued at 10:35 a.m. ET, the company said INFI went live on Ethereum and Base at the same time.

This was an airdrop with no claim step. infiniFi said registered users would receive their allocations directly into their wallets on November 17. The tokens could not be moved at first and unlocked together one hour after distribution, the release said.

Founder and CEO Rob Montgomery said: "INFI marks a new chapter for infiniFi."

Who got INFI and how much

infiniFi said early users from its first two rewards seasons could get 8.3% of INFI's total supply. CoinGecko lists a total and maximum supply of 100 million INFI, which makes the airdrop about 8.3 million tokens.

A separate 600,000 INFI is set aside for eligible users who lock their INFI, paid out over the first year, the company said. The release did not give the team or investor share or a vesting schedule, and we could not open a tokenomics page on infiniFi's site, so we leave those out. infiniFi said its funding tops $6 million, with Electric Capital leading a $3.1 million seed round.

For scale, infiniFi's dollar token iUSD had a market value of about $73.6 million on CoinGecko at 12:45 a.m. ET on November 18.

The first 14 hours of trading

CoinGecko's five-minute data show a placeholder price of about $1.16 with almost no volume before launch. Real trading started at 10:35 a.m. ET near $0.92. Within 30 minutes INFI was at $0.49, and it kept falling through the day. It hit a low of about $0.1027 at 11:20 p.m. ET.

That $1.16 figure is listed as INFI's record high on CoinGecko, so a drop measured from it looks like 91%. Measured from the first real trade, the fall is 88%.

About $500,000 of INFI changed hands in 24 hours, CoinGecko showed. Gate carried about half of it. The rest ran through Aero pools on Base (about 33%) and Ethereum (about 17%), as CoinGecko labels them.

Where the real token lives

infiniFi's official site is infinifi.xyz. CoinGecko lists the INFI contracts as 0xf40c07d7cd45f291b93ca674db66a15c8a98957e on Ethereum and 0x9f4c76f421f47b3c234f841f477897f1d895177d on Base. Since tokens went straight to wallets, any site asking you to "claim" INFI should be treated as suspect. Fake claim pages copy every airdrop, so never connect a wallet from a link in a DM or a reply.

The risks

  • Thin trading. About $500,000 a day, split across one exchange and a few pools, so single sellers can move the price a lot.
  • Unknown unlocks. The release does not say when team or investor tokens unlock. CoinGecko does not yet show a circulating supply.
  • More tokens ahead. The 600,000 INFI for token lockers will be paid out over a year.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the slide shows how an airdrop with no lockup works in practice: most recipients could sell one hour after the drop, and many did. A small daily volume means a few large sellers were enough.
  • The likely next test is whether infiniFi publishes a full unlock schedule. Without it, buyers cannot tell how many more tokens could reach the market.
  • We think 88% is the fair measure of the fall. CoinGecko's 91% starts from a $1.16 placeholder price that had almost no trading behind it.

What's next

  • A tokenomics page from infiniFi with team and investor unlock dates
  • Whether CoinGecko starts reporting a circulating supply for INFI
  • Whether INFI holds above its $0.1027 low
