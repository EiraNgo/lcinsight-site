An unnamed KOL wallet holds a $49 million bitcoin short at 40x as KOL 66ca tops the week

DeepSea's KOL board puts KOL 66ca first over seven days at +$2.76 million. On the bitcoin futures list, an unnamed influencer wallet shorted near $62,678, just above Friday's price.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • DeepSea's KOL board at 12:36 a.m. ET on Friday: KOL 66ca +$2.76 million in 7 days, 2 freres 2 fauves +$2.42 million, mk4 +$2.36 million. All three trade on Hyperliquid.
  • On DeepSea's BTC-PERP page, a wallet labeled only KOL holds a $49.28 million bitcoin short at 40x, entered at $62,678, about $858,000 up on paper.
  • mk4 holds a $6.10 million ether short at 15x from $1,817.50. DeepSea's said-versus-did file, last updated November 11, lists no cases.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea tracks the on-chain wallets of crypto influencers, or KOLs (key opinion leaders). Its KOLs and sharks board, read at 12:36 a.m. ET on Friday, ranks them by profit over seven days:

  • KOL 66ca: +$2.76 million
  • 2 freres 2 fauves: +$2.42 million
  • mk4: +$2.36 million
  • Frank: +$332,500
  • BoobsAreFantastic: +$315,200

The top three all carry DeepSea's Hyperliquid badge, meaning they trade futures on that exchange. DeepSea's dashboard put KOL 66ca at +$4.04 million over 30 days. On the smart money board, a Hyperliquid wallet labeled SM66ca shows the same +$2.76 million for the week and $7.91 million in total assets.

The BTC-PERP page at 12:39 a.m. ET listed a position under the label KOL, with no name: a $49.28 million short at 40x, entered at $62,678, with a liquidation price of $71,647 and a paper gain of $857,800. It was the sixth-largest bet among 94 tracked wallets.

On the ETH-PERP page, mk4 held a $6.10 million ether short at 15x from $1,817.50, $323,000 up on paper.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 20, 2026, 12:36 a.m. ET.

Why the KOL short stands out

Most big bitcoin shorts on DeepSea's list were opened far higher, between $69,000 and $78,000. The KOL wallet's entry of $62,678 sits just above bitcoin's price of $61,568 at 12:35 a.m. ET on Friday, according to CoinGecko. That suggests the bet was built during the slide of the past few days, when bitcoin traded near that level. An average entry can mix several fills, so the page does not show exactly when.

At 40x, the trader puts up about $1 for every $40 of the bet. The liquidation price of $71,647 is about 16% above Friday's price, so the bet has room before it would be closed.

What the weekly board shows

The first 100 names on DeepSea's 7-day KOL board all show a profit. No. 100 is at +$3,300. The board is sorted from the top, so it does not show how many tracked KOLs lost money.

mk4 led the same board on November 16 at +$4.66 million, with $6.38 million of ether shorts. Its ether short today is a similar size. 2 freres 2 fauves, No. 2 this week, shows $31.60 million in total assets on DeepSea's smart money board, far more than the other two leaders.

DeepSea's said-versus-did file, which matches what KOLs post with what their wallets do, was last updated November 11 and lists no cases, so we cannot compare these trades with public calls.

The wider market

CoinGecko's Hyperliquid data at 12:58 a.m. ET put open bets on Hyperliquid's BTC-USD contract at $1.24 billion, counted one side only, with funding at 0.001%. A $49 million position is about 4% of that.

DeepSea does not say who runs the KOL wallet. We could not open Hyperliquid's data feed or a block explorer this morning to see its trade history.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the KOL short is the most interesting bet on the board because it was opened near the bottom of the move. It is in profit only as long as bitcoin stays below about $62,700.
  • Three Hyperliquid traders took most of the week's KOL profit. That fits a falling market in which futures shorts paid off better than buying tokens.
  • A KOL's trades are not a signal to follow. They show what one wallet did, not what it will do next.

What's next

  • Whether the KOL short grows or is closed if bitcoin moves back above $62,678.
  • Whether KOL 66ca keeps the top spot as older days drop out of the 7-day window.
  • New said-versus-did cases on DeepSea's KOL pages.
