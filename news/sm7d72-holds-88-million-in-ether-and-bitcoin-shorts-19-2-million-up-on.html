SM7d72 holds $88 million in Ether and bitcoin shorts, $19.2 million up on paper

The No. 3 trader on DeepSea's 7-day board has a $47.6 million Ether short at 25x and a $40.4 million bitcoin short at 40x, plus $55 million in USDC.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • SM7d72 ranks No. 3 on DeepSea's smart money board: +$16.15 million in 7 days, $64.55 million in assets.
  • Its Ether short is $47.59 million at 25x (+$10.78 million on paper). Its bitcoin short is $40.41 million at 40x (+$8.45 million).
  • Of 20 listed positions, 19 are shorts. Only a $307,800 ADA long is not.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's smart money board ranks wallets by 7-day profit. SM7d72 is No. 3, a Hyperliquid futures trader (a bet on price moves with borrowed money). Its owner is unknown. It has appeared in our earlier smart money board stories.

Its DeepSea page shows $64.55 million in total assets, including $54.96 million of USDC. Profit: +$16.15 million in 7 days, +$32.27 million in 30 days, +$26.66 million in 90 days. Volume over 30 days: $272.17 million.

Largest open positions, all shorts (bets on a price fall):

  • Ether: $47.59 million, 25x, entry near $2,200, paper gain $10.78 million
  • Bitcoin: $40.41 million, 40x, entry near $78,400, paper gain $8.45 million
  • HYPE: $2.42 million, 10x, paper gain $461,400
  • Seventeen more, $259,000 to $931,100 each

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 17, 2026, 6:53 a.m. ET.

Checking the numbers

CoinGecko showed bitcoin at $64,803 and Ether at $1,816.58 at 6:54 a.m. ET. The $40.41 million short implies about 624 BTC. DeepSea's $8.45 million gain then implies an entry near $78,350, matching the page's 78.4K. The $47.59 million Ether short implies about 26,200 ETH; a $10.78 million gain implies an entry near $2,230, matching 2.2K.

Adding the 20 listed paper figures gives about $21.1 million. The two biggest shorts are 92% of the $88.0 million of Ether and bitcoin. Data from DeepSea and CoinGecko.

New to this?

A short is a bet that a price will fall. Leverage (25x and 40x here) multiplies gains and losses: at 40x, a price rise of a few percent would normally wipe out a position unless extra collateral backs it. This wallet holds $54.96 million of USDC, which may serve that purpose, but DeepSea does not say. "Paper gain" means the profit is not locked in until the position closes. Smart money is DeepSea's label for wallets with a profit record, not a promise of future results.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think this is a one-way bet on falling prices: almost every position is a short, and bitcoin and Ether are 92% of the book.
  • A rebound in bitcoin to the $78,400 entry would erase the $8.45 million gain, and that is a rise of about 21% from today's price.
  • This is a data view of one wallet, not a forecast.

What's next

  • Whether the Ether and bitcoin shorts are cut or kept
  • Bitcoin's distance from the $78,400 entry
  • Whether other top-10 wallets add to shorts
