Monad's MON hits a second record low in a day, $0.00914, as trading tops its market value

MON fell 8.5% below Tuesday morning's record low, bounced, then slipped back under one cent. Its 24-hour volume more than tripled, led by a Uniswap pool on Monad itself.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko records a new all-time low for MON of $0.00914 at 4:06 p.m. ET on November 24, 8.5% below the $0.00999 low set that morning.
  • MON's 24-hour volume peaked at $130.7 million at 6 a.m. ET on November 25 in CoinGecko's hourly data and was $120.4 million at 6:35 a.m., above its market value of $117 million and more than triple the $36.4 million of a day earlier.
  • The biggest single market was a MON/WETH pool on Uniswap v4 on the Monad chain, with $32.2 million traded, about a quarter of the total, per CoinGecko's ticker list.

A second record low in one day

MON, the native token of the Monad blockchain, first fell under one cent on the morning of Tuesday, November 24, when we reported a record low of $0.00999. It kept falling. CoinGecko now records an all-time low of $0.00914 at 4:06 p.m. ET that afternoon, another 8.5% lower.

Overnight, MON bounced to about $0.0107 at 4 a.m. ET on November 25, according to CoinGecko's hourly prices, then gave it back. At 6:38 a.m. ET it was $0.00989, down 22.2% in 24 hours and 33.5% in seven days. Bitcoin fell 2.3% in the same 24 hours.

A day's trading topped the market value

Trading kept climbing as the price fell. CoinGecko's rolling 24-hour volume peaked at $130.7 million at 6 a.m. ET in its hourly data and was $120.4 million at 6:35 a.m. ET, against a market value of about $117 million. So more MON changed hands in a day than all circulating MON is worth at today's price. A day earlier, at 7 a.m. ET on November 24, the volume was $36.4 million.

The mix of venues changed too. CoinGecko's ticker list puts a MON/WETH pool on Uniswap v4, running on the Monad chain itself, at the top with $32.2 million, or about a quarter of the volume. Coinbase followed with $17.4 million, then Bybit ($12.3 million), OKX ($11.7 million) and Upbit's Korean won market ($10.4 million).

Futures bets stayed small

On Hyperliquid, an on-chain futures exchange, the MON-USD perpetual held $12.5 million of open bets at the latest reading, CoinGecko's Hyperliquid listing shows. About $22 million traded there in 24 hours.

Most of the heavy trading happened in the spot market, where tokens change hands outright. Leveraged bets on Hyperliquid are about a tenth of MON's market value. We found no statement from Monad on the price.

One year after the record high

MON set its record of $0.0488 at 11:21 p.m. ET on November 25, 2025, according to CoinGecko. A year on, it is about 80% below that peak. CoinGecko counts 11.8 billion MON in circulation out of about 100.7 billion in total, so the fully diluted value, counting every token, is close to $1 billion.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the record volume matters more than the extra 8.5% drop. A day's trading above the whole market value shows a lot of MON changing hands at these prices, both sellers getting out and buyers stepping in near one cent.
  • The jump in on-chain trading on Monad's own Uniswap pool, while open bets on Hyperliquid stayed near $12.5 million, points to spot selling and buying on the chain rather than a large build-up of leveraged shorts.
  • Only 11.8 billion of about 100.7 billion MON circulate. We think the gap between a $117 million market value and a fully diluted value near $1 billion is the number to keep in view.

What's next

  • Whether MON holds above the $0.00914 record low.
  • Whether 24-hour volume falls back toward $50 million or stays above the market value.
  • Any token schedule news from Monad, a year after MON's $0.0488 record.
