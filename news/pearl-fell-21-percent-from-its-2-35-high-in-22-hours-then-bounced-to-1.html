Pearl fell 21% from its $2.35 high in 22 hours, then bounced to $1.98 as bitcoin slid

The AI mining coin hit $2.35 on Thursday morning, then dropped to about $1.85 by 8 a.m. ET Friday, with a 10.5% fall in a single hour. It is still up about 38% on the week.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows PEARL at about $1.98 at 9:04 a.m. ET on November 20, 2026, down 11.9% in 24 hours. It ranks 77th by market value, at about $761 million.
  • PEARL peaked near $2.35 at 10 a.m. ET on November 19, then fell 21.4% to about $1.85 at 8 a.m. ET on November 20. The drop from 7 a.m. to 8 a.m. alone was 10.5%. It has since bounced about 7%.
  • Bitcoin fell 3.3% in 24 hours, to about $61,845, and ether 3.2%, to about $1,752. Over seven days PEARL is still up about 38%, while bitcoin is down 10.6%.

How the drop unfolded

After a week of gains, PEARL reached about $2.35 at 10 a.m. ET on Thursday, November 19, according to hourly prices on CoinGecko. That was its highest level since its $2.46 record on October 21.

The coin held above $2.19 for most of Thursday, then slid in the evening, to about $1.93 by midnight ET. It recovered to about $2.07 by 7 a.m. ET on Friday, then fell to about $1.85 an hour later, a 10.5% drop in one hour. At 9:04 a.m. ET it traded at about $1.98.

The market around it

The whole market was lower on Friday morning. Bitcoin traded near $61,845, down 3.3% in 24 hours and 10.6% in seven days, and ether near $1,752, down 3.2% in a day, CoinGecko data show. PEARL had risen through most of that slide, as our November 18 story showed, so Friday's fall gave back part of a gain that ran against the market.

PEARL trading also cooled. CoinGecko's rolling 24-hour volume was about $60 million early on Thursday and about $35 million at 9 a.m. ET on Friday.

The supply picture

Pearl is a proof-of-useful-work blockchain that pays GPU miners for the matrix math used in AI, its project description on CoinGecko says. CoinGecko counts about 385.1 million PEARL in circulation out of a maximum of 2.1 billion, about 18%. At $1.98 the fully diluted value, all 2.1 billion coins, is about $4.15 billion. Even after the drop, PEARL is about 20% below its October record.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think this looks like profit-taking after a fast run: PEARL had climbed more than 50% in a week while bitcoin fell, and a 10.5% hourly drop is typical of thin order books giving way.
  • The 7% bounce does not settle anything yet. The price is back where it was on Wednesday, before the last leg up.
  • Supply stays the long-term question: with about 82% of coins still to be mined, new coins keep reaching miners, who often sell some to cover costs.

What's next

  • Whether PEARL holds above Friday's low near $1.85
  • Whether 24-hour volume keeps falling from Thursday's $60 million
  • How PEARL moves if bitcoin keeps sliding below $62,000
