A $101 million shark lost $1.21 million in a week, then sold 11 altcoins in two minutes

Hold91cc, an Ethereum wallet we have followed since November 8, now trades about $1.24 billion a month. Its 30-day result has turned to a $1.14 million loss.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • DeepSea's wallet page for Hold91cc showed $101.45 million in assets, minus $1.21 million over 7 days and minus $1.14 million over 30 days at 6:46 a.m. ET on November 13, 2026.
  • Between 6:32 and 6:35 a.m. ET, DeepSea's live feed logged the wallet selling 11 tokens for $62,885, led by ALT ($17,706) and HOT ($12,630), while buying $2,544 of others.
  • The wallet's 30-day count reached 53,069 trades and $1.24 billion of volume, up from 28,847 trades and $634.78 million on November 10.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea labels Hold91cc (Ethereum, address ending in 91cc) a Shark and a Holder, meaning a very large token holder. Its wallet page at 6:46 a.m. ET on November 13 showed $101.45 million in total assets across 5 tokens, minus $1.21 million over 7 days and minus $1.14 million over 30 days, a 36% win rate, 53,069 trades and $1.24 billion of volume in 30 days.

DeepSea's realized profit table (closed trades, first in, first out) built at 6:35 a.m. ET gives minus $1,205,235 on 2,921 closed trades over 7 days, 1,062 of them winners. That is the third-worst shark row of the week.

In DeepSea's live feed, the wallet sold 11 tokens between 6:32:59 and 6:34:59 a.m. ET for $62,885: 3.39 million ALT ($17,706), 32.6 million HOT ($12,630), 51,228 BARD ($8,315), 3,874 CVX ($6,245), 50,858 IMX ($5,645), 28,736 GTC ($4,414), 53,277 ERA ($4,360), 8,646 ONDO ($2,993), 381 PENDLE ($572) and two sales under $5. In the same minutes it bought 26,043 ZBT ($1,502), 100 LINK ($996) and small amounts of LPT and GTC.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 13, 2026, 6:46 a.m. ET.

How the wallet changed

New to the tools? Read what DeepSea is.

We first wrote about Hold91cc on November 8, when it showed $100.07 million in assets, 12,787 trades and +$26,100 over 30 days. On November 10 it showed $93.26 million, 28,847 trades and minus $162,000. In the three days since, its 30-day trade count nearly doubled and the loss grew about sevenfold.

The market

The sales came with bitcoin under $70,000: CoinGecko showed $69,314 at 6:48 a.m. ET, down 4.9% in 24 hours. Ether was $1,958, down 3.2% on the day and 12.8% on the week.

Why it matters

This analysis uses data from DeepSea and CoinGecko; we could not open Etherscan for this run, so the trades rest on DeepSea's records, which link each transaction. Each sale was small next to the wallet's $101 million, so the pattern, many tokens sold within seconds, says more than any single trade. Who runs the wallet and why it trades this way is unknown.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think Hold91cc looks like an automated, high-frequency trader: tens of thousands of trades a month, mostly small, across dozens of tokens.
  • Its size has stayed near $100 million while its trading result slid from a small gain to a $1.14 million loss in five days.
  • Trade count is not skill. 53,069 trades in 30 days still added up to a loss.

What's next

  • Whether the 30-day loss keeps growing as the crash runs on
  • Whether its assets stay near $101 million
  • Which tokens it buys back after the selling
