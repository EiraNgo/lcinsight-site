Hyperliquid passes Bybit to rank third in open bets, even as its crypto bets shrank 14%

Hyperliquid now ranks third for open futures bets, even though bets on its crypto markets fell about $590 million from Friday to early Sunday as bitcoin slid to $64,800. Bets on stocks, indexes and commodities held near $2.16 billion, CoinGecko data show.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Hyperliquid's 179 crypto markets held $3.45 billion in open bets at 12:43 a.m. ET on November 15, 2026, down from $4.04 billion on November 13, per CoinGecko. Even so, it now ranks third by open interest among the venues CoinGecko tracks, just ahead of Bybit.
  • Its 168 stock, index and commodity markets held $2.16 billion, about the same as Friday's $2.20 billion, and now make up 38.5% of all open bets.
  • HYPE's own market held $667 million in open bets, more than ether's $576 million.

What moved

Hyperliquid passed Bybit to rank third among derivatives venues by open interest early Sunday, November 15, CoinGecko data show. It got there even as open bets on its 179 crypto markets fell about $590 million, or 14%, in under two days, while its stock, index and commodity markets held near $2.16 billion.

On Hyperliquid, traders take perpetual futures: price bets that never expire, settled onchain (our guide explains how it works). Open interest, or open bets, counts the money still sitting in positions that are not yet closed.

CoinGecko's Hyperliquid tickers, read at 12:43 a.m. ET on Sunday, November 15, list 347 markets with $5.62 billion in open bets. The 179 crypto markets held $3.45 billion. When we last checked, at 6:38 a.m. ET on Friday, they held $4.04 billion. That is a drop of about $590 million, or 14%, in under two days.

Over the same stretch CoinGecko shows bitcoin falling to $64,813, down 3% in 24 hours and 14% in a week. Ether traded at $1,807, down 19% in a week.

The side that held

The 168 builder-run markets, nearly all tracking stocks, indexes and commodities, held $2.16 billion in open bets, against $2.20 billion on Friday, a dip of under 2%. They now make up 38.5% of the exchange's open bets, up from about 35%.

The biggest were an S&P 500 market at $249.6 million, Nvidia at $187.3 million, Brent oil at $148.3 million and gold at $144.3 million. Trading in them was slow, at $1.21 billion in 24 hours against $6.00 billion on Friday, since US stock markets are closed on weekends.

Crypto markets went the other way: they traded $13.16 billion in 24 hours, a third more than Friday's $9.89 billion, while their open bets shrank. More trading with fewer open positions usually means traders were closing bets, by choice or by force. The public data do not show which.

HYPE bets beat ether's

Bitcoin's market held the most open bets, $1.23 billion. Second was HYPE, Hyperliquid's own token, at $667 million, ahead of ether at $576 million. HYPE traded at about $65.8 on the exchange, down 4.4% in 24 hours. Its funding, the small fee that keeps a perpetual near the spot price, was slightly negative, so traders betting on a fall were paying those betting on a rise.

Across the exchange, 79 of 347 markets had negative funding, up from 47 on Friday.

Where Hyperliquid ranks

CoinGecko's ranking of derivatives venues puts Hyperliquid third by open interest, at 87,686 bitcoin worth of bets. That is behind Binance (415,013) and OKX (108,123) and just ahead of Bybit (87,341). On Friday it ranked fifth. Among other onchain venues, Aster held 17,867 bitcoin worth and Lighter 6,302.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the split shows that this week's selling was a crypto story. Bets on stocks and oil barely changed while crypto bets were cut by a seventh.
  • The rise in negative funding suggests more traders are now leaning toward further falls, which can turn into fast short covering if prices bounce.
  • A third place ranking reached in a falling market partly reflects others shrinking faster, not only Hyperliquid growing.
