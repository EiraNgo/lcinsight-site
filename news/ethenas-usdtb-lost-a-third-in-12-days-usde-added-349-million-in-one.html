Ethena's USDtb lost a third in 12 days. USDe added $349 million in one day

Two steps took USDtb from $484 million to $318 million on CoinGecko. Its sister token USDe jumped $349 million in a single reading, then went flat.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • USDtb, the Treasury-backed dollar token in the Ethena family, fell from $484.4 million in CoinGecko's November 4 reading to $317.9 million at 6:45 a.m. ET on November 16, 2026, down $166.5 million or 34%.
  • Almost all of it went in two one-day steps: $67.3 million in the November 5 reading and $98.7 million in the November 13 reading.
  • USDe, Ethena's main dollar token, rose $349.2 million in the November 9 reading to $4.93 billion. Etherscan showed 4.87 billion USDe on Ethereum at 6:55 a.m. ET.

Two big drops, no slow leak

USDtb is a dollar token backed by cash and short-term U.S. Treasury bills, with part of the reserve held in BlackRock's BUIDL fund, according to rwa.xyz, which lists Anchorage Digital Bank N.A. as the legal issuer. Because each token is meant to be worth $1, its market value tracks its supply.

On CoinGecko, USDtb sat near $475 million to $484 million for most of October. Then it fell in two clean steps. The November 5 reading was $67.3 million lower than the day before. The November 13 reading was $98.7 million lower. Between those days it barely moved. At 6:45 a.m. ET on November 16, 2026, it stood at $317.9 million, down 34% from $484.4 million on November 4.

That pattern points to a few large redemptions rather than many small holders leaving, though the data do not show who redeemed. The price held at $0.999.

A long slide from $1.5 billion

The recent drops continue a longer decline. CoinGecko's one-year data put USDtb at $1.53 billion on November 17, 2025, its highest reading in that span. It is now about 79% smaller. rwa.xyz, which also counts about 30.1 million tokens on Solana, put the total at $348.2 million on November 15, down 32.7% from 30 days earlier, spread across 294,072 holders.

USDe went the other way

USDe, the synthetic dollar Ethena backs mainly with hedged crypto positions, moved the opposite way. After a slide from $4.98 billion on October 7 to $4.50 billion on November 1, it rose $349.2 million in the November 9 reading to $4.93 billion, the biggest one-day gain in CoinGecko's 60-day data. It then stayed within $4 million of that level for a week.

The live reading at 6:42 a.m. ET on Monday was $4.85 billion, $78.8 million below the November 16 daily reading. Etherscan showed a total supply of 4.872 billion USDe on Ethereum at 6:55 a.m. ET, held by 47,151 addresses. Staked USDe (sUSDe), which earns the yield, was worth $1.19 billion on CoinGecko.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the step pattern in USDtb looks like one or two large holders cashing out, not a run. The price never left $0.999.
  • USDe's jump came in a falling market. Ethena's yield comes from hedged positions, so new mints often follow traders chasing that yield, but the data cannot show why this mint happened.
  • The two tokens now serve different buyers: USDe for yield seekers in crypto, USDtb for holders who want Treasury-style backing. Their split paths fit that.

What's next

  • Whether USDtb's next move is another large step down.
  • Whether USDe holds near $4.85 billion after Monday's $78.8 million dip.
  • Any Ethena or Anchorage statement on USDtb redemptions.
