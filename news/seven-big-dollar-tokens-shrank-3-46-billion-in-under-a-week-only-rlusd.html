Seven big dollar tokens shrank $3.46 billion in under a week. Only RLUSD grew

USDT and USDC lost $2.82 billion between them since Sunday, November 8, CoinGecko data show, as bitcoin fell 13.6% in seven days. Ripple's RLUSD added $107 million.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • From CoinGecko's 7 p.m. ET reading on November 8 to 6:42 a.m. ET on November 15, 2026, seven of eight large dollar tokens we checked shrank by a combined $3.46 billion.
  • USDT fell $1.56 billion to $175.31 billion and USDC fell $1.26 billion to $69.09 billion. World Liberty's USD1 lost 9.4% of its value, the most in percentage terms.
  • Ripple's RLUSD was the only one to grow, up $106.8 million or 4.0% to $2.80 billion. Net, the eight lost $3.36 billion.

What moved

For a dollar token, market value is close to supply, because each token is meant to be worth $1. A falling number means tokens were redeemed and destroyed. We compared CoinGecko's daily reading taken at 7 p.m. ET on Sunday, November 8, 2026, with its live figures at 6:42 a.m. ET on Sunday, November 15, a little under seven days.

Over that time, seven big dollar tokens shrank by $3.46 billion. USDT lost $1.56 billion (0.9%) and USDC lost $1.26 billion (1.8%). Bitcoin fell 13.6% over seven days on CoinGecko.

Who shrank the most

In percentage terms, USD1 fell hardest: down $322 million, or 9.4%, to $3.09 billion, trading at $0.998. PayPal's PYUSD lost $151 million (5.4%). Sky's USDS lost $84 million, Paxos's USDG $62 million and DAI $26 million.

Ripple's RLUSD went the other way, up $106.8 million (4.0%) to $2.80 billion. All eight traded between $0.998 and $1.

We left out Ethena's USDe because CoinGecko listed its circulating supply above its total supply, so we could not trust its change. The data show totals only, not who redeemed or why.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the pattern fits traders cashing out of crypto during the selloff, but redemptions can have other causes and no issuer has said why.
  • USDT's $1.56 billion is the biggest dollar figure, but at 0.9% it is a small share of its supply. USD1's 9.4% is the sharper move.
  • RLUSD growing through a crash week stands out. One week is too short to call it a trend.

What's next

  • Whether USDT and USDC keep shrinking next week.
  • USD1's price against $1 as its supply falls.
  • Whether RLUSD keeps growing above $2.8 billion.
