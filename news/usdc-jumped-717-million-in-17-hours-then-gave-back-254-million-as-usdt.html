USDC jumped $717 million in 17 hours, then gave back $254 million as USDT sat flat

Circle's USDC grew fast on Monday, then shrank as bitcoin slid under $60,000. Tether's USDT stayed inside a $141 million band for 72 hours.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • USDC's market value on CoinGecko rose $717 million in 17 hours, from $68.47 billion at 7 a.m. ET on November 23 to $69.19 billion at midnight ET.
  • By 6:39 a.m. ET on November 25 it was back to $68.94 billion, $254 million below that peak.
  • USDT stayed between $174.58 billion and $174.72 billion for 72 hours, ending at $174.66 billion, just $19 million above where it started.

USDC rose, then partly fell back

CoinGecko's hourly data show Circle's USDC at $68.47 billion at 7 a.m. ET on Monday, November 23. The biggest single hour, 10 a.m. to 11 a.m. ET, added $332 million. By midnight it reached $69.19 billion, up $717 million. We reported part of that rise on November 24.

Since then it has swung up and down hour by hour and drifted lower. At 6:39 a.m. ET on Wednesday, November 25, it stood at $68.94 billion, $254 million below the peak and $87 million lower than 24 hours earlier. USDC's price held between $0.9992 and $0.9997 in the same data.

Stablecoins are tokens built to stay at $1. Their supply grows when the issuer mints new tokens for dollars deposited, and shrinks when holders cash out and tokens are burned. That makes them a rough gauge of dollars entering or leaving crypto.

USDT held flat

Tether's USDT barely moved. In CoinGecko's hourly data its market value ranged from $174.58 billion (5 p.m. ET, November 22) to $174.72 billion (2 p.m. ET, November 23) over the 72 hours to 6:38 a.m. ET on November 25. It ended at $174.66 billion, $19 million above the start.

CoinGecko lists USDT's total supply at $180.31 billion.

USDT's price was $0.9990, with a 24-hour low of $0.9980 on CoinGecko, a hair below its $1 target.

Where the tokens sit

Tronscan's data show 94.21 billion USDT issued on the Tron network, about 52% of the total supply on CoinGecko. On Ethereum, Etherscan lists 44.38 billion USDC at 4:18 a.m. ET on November 25, about 64% of USDC's 68.97 billion circulating tokens.

Bitcoin fell from $63,697 at 9 a.m. ET on November 23 to $58,469 at 6:37 a.m. ET on November 25 in CoinGecko's data. Stablecoin supply did not fall with it. The two largest coins together are about $455 million larger than at 7 a.m. ET on Monday, and all of the gain came from USDC.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think USDC's quick jump and partial retreat look like large holders moving dollars in and then pulling some back out as prices kept falling. The data show the flows, not who made them.
  • USDT's flat line is notable after its market value fell about $439 million in one hour on the evening of November 20, in CoinGecko's data. A pause in redemptions suggests the cash-out wave of the crash has slowed, though no fresh dollars are arriving either.
  • Stable supply during a falling market means money is waiting on the sidelines in dollar tokens rather than leaving crypto entirely. It does not tell us when or whether it will be spent.

What's next

  • Whether USDC keeps shrinking toward its November 22 level of $68.37 billion.
  • Any new USDT landing in Tether's Ethereum treasury wallet, a sign of fresh minting.
  • USDT's price, which touched $0.9980 in the 24 hours to Wednesday morning.
