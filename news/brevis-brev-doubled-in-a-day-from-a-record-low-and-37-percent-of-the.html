Brevis (BREV) doubled in a day from a record low, and 37% of the trading ran through Upbit

BREV hit an all-time low near $0.0554 on Monday night, then ran to about $0.132 by Tuesday morning. It is still about 80% below its January record.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows BREV at $0.1121 at 7:01 p.m. ET on November 17, 2026, up 99.6% in 24 hours. It was No. 3 on CoinGecko's trending list earlier in the day.
  • The token set an all-time low of $0.05535 at 8:04 p.m. ET on Monday, then climbed to about $0.132 at 10:20 a.m. ET on Tuesday, a gain of about 139%.
  • About $104 million traded in 24 hours against a market value near $28 million. Upbit's BREV/KRW market carried about 37% of the trading CoinGecko lists by venue.

The move

Brevis's token BREV drifted lower through Monday evening, November 16, 2026, according to CoinGecko. At 8:04 p.m. ET it printed $0.05535, the lowest price CoinGecko has ever recorded for it. It then traded sideways near $0.057 overnight.

The climb started around 7 a.m. ET on Tuesday and was fast: about $0.059 at 7 a.m., $0.069 at 8 a.m., $0.093 at 9 a.m. and a peak of about $0.132 at 10:20 a.m. ET. It slipped to about $0.100 by early afternoon, then recovered to $0.1121 at 7:01 p.m. ET, up 99.6% in 24 hours.

Over the same 24 hours CoinGecko shows bitcoin up 0.1% and ether up 0.3%, so the jump was BREV's own, not the market's. Even after doubling, BREV is about 80% below its all-time high of $0.5601, set on January 7, 2026, the day after the token launched.

Where it traded

CoinGecko shows about $104 million of BREV volume in 24 hours. Of the trading it lists by market, Upbit's BREV/KRW pair handled about $61.5 million (36.9%), Binance's BREV/TRY pair about $27.0 million (16.2%) and Binance's BREV/USDT pair about $26.1 million (15.7%). Bithumb's won pair added about $7.9 million (4.7%), so the two big South Korean exchanges together carried about 42%.

Daily volume of nearly four times the token's market value means the float changed hands several times in one day. Moves on that kind of turnover can reverse just as fast.

What Brevis is, and the supply

Brevis builds zero-knowledge proving infrastructure, software that lets apps prove a computation was done correctly without redoing it. BREV went live on January 6, 2026, alongside its ProverNet mainnet, Brevis said. The token uses the same contract address (0x086f...8bffb) on Ethereum, BNB Chain and Base.

CoinGecko counts 250 million BREV in circulation out of a 1 billion maximum, or 25%. At $0.1121 that is a market value of about $28 million (No. 621 on CoinGecko) and a fully diluted value of about $112 million. Brevis has not given a reason for the jump in any source we checked, and this story does not suggest one. Our earlier look at the token's launch is here.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a doubling that starts from a fresh all-time low, with most of the trading on a few exchange order books, says more about short-term traders than about a change in the project. Only a quarter of the supply circulates, and the 75% still locked is a long-term overhang. This is not investment advice.

What's next

  • Whether BREV holds above about $0.100, where it bottomed after the morning peak
  • Whether trading stays concentrated on Upbit and Binance
  • Any official statement from Brevis about the move
