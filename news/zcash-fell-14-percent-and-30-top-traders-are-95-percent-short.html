Zcash fell 14% and 30 top traders are 95% short. Influencer mk4 is short again

DeepSea counts $76.82 million of Zcash shorts on Hyperliquid against one $4.11 million long. Influencer mk4, whose $13.4 million Zcash short was gone on November 16, now holds a $4.60 million short.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Zcash traded at $818.53 at 6:48 a.m. ET on Friday, down 14.5% in a day from a 24-hour high of $954.20, CoinGecko shows.
  • DeepSea's ZEC-PERP page at 6:48 a.m. ET counted 30 top Hyperliquid traders 95% short: $76.82 million against $4.11 million long.
  • KOL mk4 holds a $4.60 million short at 10x from $888.85, up $370,000. One short opened at $330.91 is still $4.14 million down.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's ZEC-PERP page follows the Zcash futures bets of the top traders it tracks on Hyperliquid. At 6:48 a.m. ET on Friday it counted 30 wallets, 95% short: $76.82 million at an average entry of $872.50. The only long, a wallet labeled PURRINA Pro Plan, held $4.11 million at 10x from $940.69, $597,700 down on paper.

The page showed Zcash at $819.10, down 14.42% in a day, with $278.26 million of open Zcash futures on Hyperliquid.

The biggest bets were shorts:

  • Smart trader #214: $8.94 million from $888.84, up $720,300
  • Smart trader #49: $7.39 million from $917.09, up $854,800
  • Smart trader #82: $6.93 million from $330.91, down $4.14 million
  • BobbyBigSize: $5.27 million from $911.23, up $598,400
  • mk4: $4.60 million from $888.85, up $370,000

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 20, 2026, 6:48 a.m. ET.

mk4 is back

mk4 is a crypto influencer, or KOL, whose Hyperliquid wallet DeepSea tracks. On November 16 LC Insight reported that mk4's $13.4 million Zcash short was gone from its DeepSea page. Friday's page shows a new, smaller short of $4.60 million at 10x, entered at $888.85, with a liquidation price of $4,494.70. DeepSea's KOL board ranks mk4 third for the week, up $2.36 million.

A short is a bet that the price will fall. About half of the shorts were entered between $830 and $920, so the group's paper gains are modest next to the size of the bets.

Our 6:53 a.m. ET screenshot showed $76.73 million short and $4.11 million long, still 95% short.

The short from $330

Smart trader #82 is the outlier. Its short was entered at $330.91, when Zcash cost well under half of today's price, and it is still $4.14 million down even after Friday's drop. Its liquidation price is $2,199.40.

Hyperliquid's ZEC-USD contract had $140.29 million in open positions and $252.35 million of 24-hour volume on CoinGecko at 6:49 a.m. ET. DeepSea's $278.26 million is about twice as large; the two sites measure open bets differently.

Hyperliquid's perps as a whole held $7.712 billion in open interest on DefiLlama, which also logged $13.709 billion of trading over 24 hours.

Zcash is 74.4% below its October 2016 record of $3,191.93, according to CoinGecko.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think mk4's return to a Zcash short, at a third of the old size, shows the influencer still leans the same way but with less at stake.
  • With one long against 29 shorts, the board is crowded on one side. New shorts entered near $890 have far less room than the older ones.
  • Smart trader #82's position shows that a big drop can still leave an early short deep in the red.

What's next

  • Whether mk4 adds to or closes the $4.60 million short.
  • Whether the PURRINA Pro Plan long nears its $560.41 liquidation price.
  • Whether Zcash recovers toward the shorts' $872.50 average entry.
