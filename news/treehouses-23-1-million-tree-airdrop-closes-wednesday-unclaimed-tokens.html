Treehouse's 23.1 million TREE airdrop closes Wednesday; unclaimed tokens go to its treasury

Season 2 of Treehouse's airdrop set aside 23.1 million TREE, 2.31% of supply, and the claim window ends at 2:00 a.m. ET on November 25. The pot is worth about $667,000 at Sunday's price, with TREE 98% below its record.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Treehouse's Season 2 airdrop holds 23.1 million TREE, 2.31% of the 1 billion supply. Claims close Wednesday, November 25, 2026, at 2:00 a.m. ET (7:00 UTC), per Treehouse's docs.
  • All of it has now vested: the 80% locked portion finished vesting on October 26. Unclaimed allocations return to the Treehouse treasury when the window closes.
  • At 12:43 a.m. ET on November 22, CoinGecko put TREE at $0.0289, 97.9% below its July 2025 record of $1.36. The whole Season 2 pot is worth about $667,000.

The airdrop, and what Treehouse is

Treehouse builds fixed-income tools for DeFi, according to its CoinGecko profile: a benchmark rate called DOR (Decentralized Offered Rates) and tETH, a wrapper around staked ether. TREE is its token.

On April 29, 2026, Treehouse announced its Season 2 rewards: 23.1 million TREE, taken from its future airdrops allocation, or 2.31% of total supply. Its docs list the claim window as April 29 to November 25, 2026, 7:00 UTC, which is 2:00 a.m. ET on Wednesday. The claim is open and closes in about three days.

Who qualifies

The snapshot was taken on April 25, 2026, at 2:00 UTC. Treehouse's rules give a wallet a share if it had any of these:

  • At least 500 Nuts (Treehouse points) at the snapshot: a minimum of 100 TREE plus a share in proportion to Nuts.
  • A vote on at least one Treehouse Improvement Proposal during Season 2: 10,000 TREE.
  • Each Treehouse Squirrel Council NFT held at the snapshot: 10,000 TREE, on top of other shares.

Treehouse says it applied sybil filtering, which removes or cuts allocations for wallets it judged to be fake users. It has not published the exact criteria.

Supply and unlocks

Season 2 opened with 20% of each share claimable. The other 80% vested in a straight line over 180 days, ending on October 26, 2026. A wallet can claim only once, and claiming ends its vesting, so anyone claiming now receives the full amount.

Per CoinGecko, 156.1 million TREE are in circulation, 15.6% of the 1 billion supply. That gives a market value of $4.51 million and a fully diluted value of $28.87 million. TREE fell 24.3% in 30 days and hit a record low of $0.0269 on November 14, before rising 5.9% in the last 24 hours.

Where to claim, and the risks

Use only the claim link in Treehouse's official docs, and check the airdrop contract address listed there before signing anything. Treehouse says it will never send claim links by direct message, reports a rise in fake claim links, and tells users to go to the site directly rather than through links they receive.

The other risk is supply: 84% of TREE is not yet circulating, so more tokens can reach the market over time.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • Treehouse has not published how much of the pot was claimed. Wallets that claimed before October 26 gave up the part not yet vested, and that part also returns to the treasury.
  • At $0.0289, the minimum 100 TREE share is worth under $3, while a vote or one NFT is worth about $289. For most wallets, the size of the payout now depends far more on TREE's price than on the token count.
  • Unclaimed tokens returning to the treasury do not leave the supply. They stay with Treehouse and can be used later.

What's next

  • The window closing at 2:00 a.m. ET on Wednesday, November 25.
  • Whether Treehouse publishes how much of the 23.1 million TREE was claimed and returned.
  • TREE's price after its November 14 record low.
