Solana ETFs drew $4.7 million in crash week while ether funds lost $1.24 billion in four weeks

Of the U.S. spot bitcoin, ether, solana and XRP fund groups, only solana's had a weekly inflow, thanks to one Monday. Ether funds have had just two inflow days since late October, and XRP funds lost $11.1 million on the week.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • U.S. spot solana ETFs took in a net $4.7 million in the week of November 16 to 20, 2026, CoinMarketCap's flow data show: $11.7 million on Monday, then $1.1 million and $5.9 million out on Thursday and Friday.
  • Spot XRP funds lost $11.1 million on the week, with $6.1 million out on Tuesday and $5.0 million out on Friday. They have not had an inflow day since October 29.
  • Spot ether funds lost $1.24 billion over the 20 sessions from October 26 to November 20. Only November 4 ($75.3 million) and November 13 ($2.3 million) brought money in.

The week for solana and XRP funds

Spot solana ETFs ended the week of November 16 to 20, 2026, with a net inflow of $4.7 million, according to CoinMarketCap's solana fund series read at 6:45 a.m. ET on Saturday. All of it came from Monday's $11.7 million. CoinMarketCap shows no flow on Tuesday or Wednesday, then $1.1 million out on Thursday and $5.9 million out on Friday.

Spot XRP funds lost $11.1 million on the week in CoinMarketCap's XRP fund data: $6.1 million on Tuesday and $5.0 million on Friday, with no flow shown on the other days. Their last inflow day was October 29, at $8.4 million.

A spot ETF holds the coin itself and its shares trade like a stock. A day with no flow means no new shares were created or redeemed. Friday figures are first readings and are often revised.

Ether funds: a month of selling

Ether funds lost $13.2 million on Friday and $211.7 million on the week, in CoinMarketCap's ether fund series, as we reported with the bitcoin figures overnight. The longer view is starker. Over the 20 trading sessions from October 26 to November 20, ether funds lost $1.24 billion, and money came in on only two days: $75.3 million on November 4 and $2.3 million on November 13.

The biggest single day in that run was November 9, at $163.4 million out. November alone stands at minus $844.1 million over 15 sessions.

Prices over the week

Solana traded near $70.77 on Monday morning and about $67.38 at Friday's 4 p.m. ET close, and was at $68.72 at 6:46 a.m. ET Saturday, CoinGecko's chart shows. XRP went from about $0.950 to $0.887 at Friday's close and $0.902 on Saturday morning, in CoinGecko's XRP data.

Ether fell further: from about $1,802 on Monday to $1,599 at Friday's close, then $1,652 on Saturday morning, per CoinGecko. Bitcoin funds, for comparison, lost $126.8 million on Friday and $1.30 billion on the week, CoinMarketCap's series shows.

November so far

So far in November, solana funds are at minus $42.2 million and XRP funds at minus $30.2 million, by our sum of CoinMarketCap's daily figures. Both are small next to ether's $844.1 million. We could not open Farside's or the issuers' per-fund tables, so we cannot say which funds drove the weekly numbers.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the solana number says more about one Monday than about a trend. Without the $11.7 million on November 16, solana funds would have ended the week down too.
  • Ether funds are the clearest case of steady selling outside bitcoin: money in on only 2 of 20 sessions, while ether dropped about 11% from Monday to Friday's close.
  • Funds are closed on weekends, so the drop under $1,600 for ether and the bounce early Saturday happened without ETF buyers or sellers.

What's next

  • Revisions to Friday's first readings on Monday.
  • Monday, November 23 flows, the first since Saturday's crash low.
  • Whether ether funds' 20-session outflow passes $1.5 billion.
