AKE rose 82% more after our Friday report as $583 million traded, twice its value

Akedo's AKE token rose another 82% from Friday morning to early Saturday and is up almost fourfold from its 24-hour low, CoinGecko data show. It is still 92% below its September record.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • AKE traded at $0.01201 at 12:42 a.m. ET on November 14, 2026, up 306% in 24 hours, CoinGecko data show. Its 24-hour low was $0.00321 and its high $0.01369.
  • Our November 13 report had AKE at $0.00658 at 6:45 a.m. ET. The price has risen another 82% since then, most of it between 10 a.m. and 1 p.m. ET.
  • CoinGecko counted $582.8 million of AKE traded in 24 hours, more than twice its $273.7 million market value. AKE is still 91.8% below its record of $0.1467 from September 20.

What moved

Akedo's AKE token rose another 82% after our Friday morning report. At 12:42 a.m. ET on November 14, 2026, it traded at $0.01201, up 306% in 24 hours, CoinGecko data show. Over that window it ranged from $0.00321 to $0.01369, so the top was more than four times the bottom.

The rise came while the wider market slipped. At 12:50 a.m. ET, bitcoin traded near $66,886, down 2.4% in 24 hours, and ether near $1,896, down 1.2%, CoinGecko data show.

Volume, supply and the record

Trading was far bigger than the token itself. CoinGecko counted $582.8 million traded in 24 hours, while the whole token was valued at $273.7 million. On Friday morning the volume was $158.8 million. When a day's trading is twice a token's whole value, the same coins change hands many times.

Most of the supply is not trading yet. Of the 100 billion AKE that will ever exist, CoinGecko counts 22.8 billion as circulating. Priced at today's level, all 100 billion would come to about $1.20 billion, 4.4 times the market value. The other 77.2 billion are not counted as circulating yet. If they reach the market, the supply that trades grows.

The longer view has not changed much. AKE is up 150% in seven days but down 56% in 30 days, and still 91.8% below the $0.1467 peak CoinGecko records for September 20, 2026.

Where it traded

Two venues carried most of the volume CoinGecko lists. PancakeSwap V3, the main decentralized exchange on BNB Chain, showed about $247.9 million in its AKE pair with wrapped BNB. The Gate exchange showed about $186.1 million against USDT. Together that is about 74% of the 24-hour total.

Smaller amounts traded on LBank (about $40.5 million), KuCoin (about $38.6 million) and Kraken (about $13.3 million), CoinGecko's market list shows.

What we could not check

We found no official reason for the move in the sources we could open. Akedo's website and the BscScan token page, which shows the largest holders, could not be reached during this check. So we do not say who bought, or how concentrated the supply is. According to its whitepaper, Akedo lets users type a text prompt and have AI agents help build a game from it.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think a move this fast on volume twice the market value shows heavy short-term trading, not a settled new price. Tokens that run like this often swing hard both ways in the following days.
  • AKE climbed while bitcoin and ether slipped, so this was a move in one token, not a market rally lifting everything.
  • The 4.4 times gap between full-supply value and market value matters. If more of the supply comes to market, the amount trading could grow well beyond today's circulating figure.

What's next

  • Whether 24-hour volume falls back toward the token's market value.
  • Any statement from Akedo explaining the move.
  • The holder list on BscScan, to see if a few wallets own most of the supply.
