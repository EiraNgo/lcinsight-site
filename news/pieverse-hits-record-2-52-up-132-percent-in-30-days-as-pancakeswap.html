Pieverse hits record $2.52, up 132% in 30 days, as PancakeSwap pool takes 37.4% of trading

PIEVERSE beat its November 9 high on Wednesday morning while bitcoin slipped. This time a PancakeSwap pool on BNB Chain, not South Korea's Upbit, handled the largest share of the volume.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko recorded a new all-time high of $2.52 for PIEVERSE at 6:36 a.m. ET on November 18, 2026, above its November 9 peak of about $2.22 in CoinGecko's hourly prices.
  • At 6:44 a.m. ET the token was at $2.47, up about 24% in 24 hours and 132% in 30 days, with a market value of about $682 million (No. 88 on CoinGecko).
  • A PancakeSwap Infinity pool on BNB Chain carried about 37% of the trading CoinGecko lists by venue, ahead of Upbit at about 33%.

The move, from one record to the next

Pieverse's token PIEVERSE climbed to a record $2.52 at 6:36 a.m. ET on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, according to CoinGecko, and is up 132% in 30 days. On November 9 it set a record of $2.13 in the morning, as we reported then, and CoinGecko's hourly prices show it kept rising to about $2.22 by 2 p.m. ET that day.

The path between the two highs was not straight. CoinGecko's hourly prices show PIEVERSE near $2.07 at 7 a.m. ET on November 10, then about $1.63 at the same hour on November 12. It recovered to about $2.00 by Tuesday morning, November 17. The 24-hour low was $1.93 at about 8:30 a.m. ET Tuesday, and the last leg came quickly: about $2.23 at 4:40 a.m. ET Wednesday and $2.50 two hours later.

At 6:44 a.m. ET PIEVERSE was at $2.47, up about 24% in 24 hours, while bitcoin fell 0.9% and ether 1.1%. A month ago, on October 19, it traded near $1.06.

Where it traded

CoinGecko's ticker list sums to about $49.2 million of PIEVERSE trading in 24 hours. The biggest single venue was PancakeSwap Infinity's PIEVERSE/USDT liquidity pool on BNB Chain, with about $18.4 million (37.4%). Upbit's PIEVERSE/KRW market followed with about $16.2 million (32.9%), then Gate at $3.7 million (7.5%), Bybit at $3.5 million (7.1%) and Bithumb at $3.2 million (6.5%).

That is a change from November 9, when Upbit handled about 43% and the PancakeSwap pool about 25%. The two South Korean won markets together carried about 39% this time, down from about 52%. Daily volume is also lower than on November 9 (about $49 million against $58.7 million), even though the price is higher.

Supply to keep in mind

Of a 1 billion maximum supply, 274.75 million tokens (about 27.5%) circulate, per CoinGecko. At $2.47 that is a market value of about $682 million and a fully diluted value, the value if every token existed, of about $2.47 billion (our guide to FDV vs. market cap). CoinGecko files the token under AI applications and the BNB Chain and Ethereum ecosystems. It uses the same contract address (0x0e63...25a9) on both chains. Its all-time low was $0.1194 on November 14, 2025, so the token is up roughly 20 times in a year.

We found no official explanation for Wednesday's move in the sources we could check, and we do not assign one.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, the shift of trading from Upbit toward an on-chain PancakeSwap pool is the new detail in this run. The buying is no longer led by one Korean order book.
  • Lower volume at a higher price can mean fewer sellers, or a thinner market that moves more easily both ways.
  • With 72.5% of supply not yet circulating, any unlock schedule matters. This is not investment advice.

  • Whether the PancakeSwap pool keeps the largest share of trading
