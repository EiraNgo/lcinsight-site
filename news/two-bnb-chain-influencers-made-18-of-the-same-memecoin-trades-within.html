Two BNB Chain influencers made 18 of the same memecoin trades within hours. Both are up this week

DeepSea's KOL wallets for kurtz and tech bought and sold the same tokens, in the same direction and within about an hour, over two days. DeepSea counts every closed trade of theirs this week as a win.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Among the 30 latest trades DeepSea lists for each wallet, 18 tokens appear in both, traded the same way within about an hour of each other: GEN.G, GM BINANCE, CORLA, WHALE, MSN, Billy, RELAY, FMINING and others.
  • DeepSea shows kurtz (@kurtzxx, 16,096 followers) at +$22.6K over 7 days and tech (@technoviking46, 10,265 followers) at +$28.5K, each with six closed trades this week, all winners.
  • At 12:34 a.m. ET on November 8, DeepSea logged kurtz and a second KOL wallet, 0xAA, each buying one STARLINK token in the same transaction.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea tags both wallets as KOLs (key opinion leaders, meaning crypto influencers) on BNB Chain. The kurtz wallet links to the X handle @kurtzxx with 16,096 followers. The tech wallet links to @technoviking46 with 10,265 followers.

Each page lists its latest 30 trades. 18 tokens appear on both lists, on the same side, 17 of them in the same hour as DeepSea rounds it and the 18th an hour apart. Both bought 比特币 (Chinese for "bitcoin," here a memecoin's name) about 14 hours before our check. Both bought GEN.G, GM BINANCE and CORLA and sold SHIB about 16 hours before, then bought WHALE and 红温CZ, another memecoin with a Chinese name, around 16 to 17 hours before. Both bought MSN, YYDS and four tokens with Chinese names, including 币安人生 ("Binance Life"), about 22 hours before, and both sold DONKEY an hour earlier. Both bought Billy and RELAY a day before, bought FMINING two days before, and sold MAMESUKE two days before.

DeepSea's trade panel shows kurtz +$22.6K over 7 days and +$37.0K over 30 days, 522 trades and $1.68 million of volume, and tech +$28.5K over both periods, 681 trades and $1.09 million. Both show a 100% win rate. DeepSea's realized PnL table (12:30 a.m. ET) agrees: six closed trades each this week, six winners each, +$22,623 and +$28,537. They are the two best KOL results in that table this week.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 8, 2026, 12:35 to 12:58 a.m. ET.

Two scorecards on one page

Each wallet page carries a second, profile scorecard that tells a different story. It puts kurtz at +$45.1K over 30 days with a 74% win rate and a 0% win rate over 7 days, and $114,100 of 30-day volume. Tech shows +$7.0K over 30 days, a 68% win rate and minus $22 over 7 days. The "DeepSea trades" panel counts many more trades, so the two panels do not measure the same thing. Readers should treat the 100% figure as six closed trades, not a perfect record.

Same name, maybe a different token

Memecoins on BNB Chain often share names. DeepSea's rows price the two wallets' 币安人生 buys at about $0.33 a coin. CoinGecko's token of that name, with a $499 million market value, traded at $0.4989 at 12:54 a.m. ET, down 6.6% in a week. The gap could mean an earlier price or a different token with the same name. We could not open BscScan, the BNB Chain explorer, in this run to check the contracts.

What it does and does not show

Matching trades are common among memecoin traders and have ordinary explanations: two traders reading the same public calls or alert channels, a copy trading tool, or one trader following the other. DeepSea's data does not show which. We make no claim about who controls these wallets, we see no sign of wrongdoing, and neither influencer is accused of anything. A one-token STARLINK "buy" logged for two KOL wallets in a single transaction is unusual; we could not check on an explorer whether either wallet asked for it.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • The overlap is large: 18 shared tokens out of 30 recent trades, same side, mostly the same hour. Whatever the reason, followers who copy either wallet are in practice getting much the same trades.
  • Six winning trades in a week is a small sample. The profile panel's 7-day win rate of 0% for kurtz shows how much depends on which DeepSea panel you read.
  • Tiny memecoins with copycat names are easy to mix up. Anyone checking a KOL's calls should match the contract address, not the ticker.

What's next

  • Whether the two wallets keep trading the same tokens
  • Whether their 30-day results stay positive
  • More STARLINK or similar transfers to KOL wallets
