Solana ETFs took in $11.7 million on Monday, the most since September, as SOL fell 11%

U.S. spot Solana funds broke a month of selling on November 16, CoinMarketCap data show. XRP funds saw no net flow, and XRP slipped below $1.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • U.S. spot Solana ETFs had net inflows of $11.7 million on Monday, November 16, 2026, CoinMarketCap data show. It is the largest daily inflow in CoinMarketCap's 90-day chart since September 25 ($80.7 million).
  • The funds had lost $136.7 million over the 20 trading days to November 13, with one small inflow day ($1.7 million on November 12). Monday's inflow cuts that to about $125.0 million over 21 days.
  • CoinMarketCap shows SOL at about $72.69 beside Monday's flow, down about 11% from $81.66 beside Friday's. XRP funds had no net flow on Monday and XRP was at about $0.97.

Buyers show up as the price drops

Spot Solana ETFs are listed funds, bought like shares on U.S. exchanges, that hold actual SOL. An inflow means more money came in than went out that day.

CoinMarketCap's flow data show $11.7 million flowing into U.S. spot Solana funds on Monday, November 16. The previous Friday had $3.1 million in net outflows, and the only other inflow in the past three weeks was $1.7 million on November 12.

CoinMarketCap's 90-day chart covers 58 trading days. The last time it shows a bigger one-day inflow is September 25, with $80.7 million.

Price fell while money came in

On the same CoinMarketCap series, SOL was about $81.66 beside Friday's figure and about $72.69 beside Monday's, a drop of about 11%. So the one big inflow day came as the price was falling, not after a rally.

At 6:44 a.m. ET on Tuesday, CoinGecko showed SOL at $73.29, up 2.5% in 24 hours.

A daily flow is net: it does not tell us who bought. It could be one large order or many small ones, and CoinMarketCap's total does not show which fund received the money.

XRP funds stay quiet

U.S. spot XRP funds had $1.1 million in net outflows on Friday and no net flow on Monday, CoinMarketCap shows. In the week of November 9 to 13 they lost about $19.1 million, with outflows of $10.4 million and $7.6 million on the first two days.

CoinMarketCap lists XRP at about $0.97 beside Monday, down from about $1.04 beside Friday. CoinGecko had it at $0.9677 on Tuesday morning, so XRP is trading below one dollar.

What we could not confirm

We could not open fund-by-fund tables, so we cannot say which Solana fund took the $11.7 million. We also cannot say whether Monday's buyers were new investors or existing holders shifting money between products.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think one inflow day does not change the trend. Solana funds are still down about $125 million since October 19, and Monday's figure is small next to September's days of $25 million to $80 million.
  • The timing is the interesting part. Money came in on the day SOL fell about 11%, which suggests some buyers saw the drop as a chance to buy, but the data cannot show their reasons.
  • XRP funds show the opposite mood: little buying and little selling, even with XRP under $1.

What's next

  • Tuesday, November 17: whether Solana funds post a second inflow day.
  • Whether XRP funds see any flow while XRP stays below $1.
  • Fund-by-fund Solana numbers from issuer pages.
