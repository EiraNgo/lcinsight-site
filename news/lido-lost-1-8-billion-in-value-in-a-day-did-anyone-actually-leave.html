Lido lost $1.8 billion in value in a day. Did anyone actually leave?

DefiLlama shows DeFi's biggest protocols shrank 4% to 8% in 24 hours. Compared with CoinGecko prices, most of that looks like falling prices, not withdrawals.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Lido's total value locked fell 7.6% in 24 hours to $21.73 billion, DefiLlama shows. Ether fell 8.3% over about the same time on CoinGecko.
  • Aave V3 fell 5.9% to $15.10 billion and Morpho Blue 4.7% to $10.92 billion.
  • Over seven days, Lido, Aave V3 and Morpho Blue are still up 0.7%, 2.3% and 3.2%.

What TVL means

TVL (total value locked) is the dollar value of all the coins deposited in a protocol. It drops when people withdraw, but it also drops when the price of the deposited coins falls, even if nobody moves anything. So on a crash day, the key question is which of the two happened.

What moved

DefiLlama's protocol list, read at 12:50 a.m. ET on November 6, shows red across DeFi's largest names over 24 hours:

  • Lido (ether staking): minus 7.6% to $21.73 billion.
  • SSV Network (staking): minus 7.9% to $11.69 billion.
  • Aave V3 (lending): minus 5.9% to $15.10 billion.
  • Morpho Blue (lending): minus 4.7% to $10.92 billion.
  • WBTC (bitcoin on Ethereum): minus 5.9% to $8.82 billion.

By our math, Lido's drop is about $1.8 billion.

Price, not panic

CoinGecko showed ether down 8.3% and bitcoin down 6.2% in 24 hours at 12:38 a.m. ET. Lido holds almost only ether, and its value fell a little less than ether itself. WBTC fell a little less than bitcoin. That points to deposits staying roughly flat, with the price doing the damage. The two data sets use slightly different time windows, so this is a rough comparison.

Lending protocols such as Aave and Morpho fell less because they also hold stablecoins, which kept their $1 value.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the TVL drop mostly reflects lower prices: these numbers show no sign of a rush to withdraw, though daily TVL cannot rule out smaller outflows.
  • The seven-day figures, still positive for Lido, Aave V3 and Morpho Blue, support that reading.
  • The real stress test for lenders is liquidations, which happen if prices keep falling, so the next moves in ether matter most.

What's next

  • Whether Lido's ether deposits change over the week
  • Aave and Morpho liquidations if ether drops further
  • Ether price near $2,200
