EVAA is up 142% in two days, and $60 million traded in a token worth $18 million

The lending token on TON and BNB Chain climbed from $1.10 to $2.67 since early Saturday, with 24-hour trading 3.4 times its market value. Only 13% of its supply circulates, and it is still 80% below its 2025 record.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko showed EVAA at $2.67 at 12:37 a.m. ET on November 16, 2026, up 63.5% in 24 hours, 131% in seven days and 349% in 30 days. It was $1.10 at 1 a.m. ET on November 14.
  • About $60.3 million of EVAA traded in 24 hours, 3.4 times its $17.7 million market value. Gate's EVAA/USDT market carried about half of that.
  • Only 6.62 million of 50 million tokens circulate (13.2%). The fully diluted value is $133.5 million, and the price is still 80.4% below its $13.62 record.

What moved

EVAA more than doubled over the weekend, from $1.10 at 1 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 14, to $2.67 at 12:37 a.m. ET on Monday, November 16, per CoinGecko. It is the token of EVAA Protocol, a lending app where users deposit crypto and borrow against it. CoinGecko lists it on TON and on BNB Chain, and tags it as a Binance Alpha Spotlight token and an Animoca Brands portfolio project.

The run came in two legs. CoinGecko's hourly data shows EVAA at $1.12 at 7 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 14, then $1.95 by 10 a.m. ET, a 74% jump in three hours. It gave back part of that and sat near $1.43 at 7 p.m. ET. The second leg started overnight: $2.18 at 4 a.m. ET Sunday and $2.80 at 10 a.m. ET.

At 12:37 a.m. ET on Monday, November 16, it traded at $2.67, with a 24-hour range of $1.70 to $2.85, per CoinGecko's market data. Bitcoin moved 0.1% in the same 24 hours.

Where it traded

Trading grew far faster than the price. CoinGecko's rolling 24-hour volume was about $1.2 million early on November 14. It peaked near $91.6 million at 10 a.m. ET on November 15 and stood at $60.3 million at 12:37 a.m. ET, 3.4 times the token's market value.

In CoinGecko's per-exchange list, Gate's EVAA/USDT pair carried about $30.0 million, half of the total. PancakeSwap v3 on BNB Chain followed with $8.4 million and Bitget with $7.8 million. When one venue holds half the trading, its order book can steer the price.

Supply and value

CoinGecko counts 6,617,972 EVAA in circulation out of 50 million in total, or 13.2%. That gives a market value of $17.7 million (No. 829 on CoinGecko) and a fully diluted value, the price times every token, of $133.5 million.

A small float means a little buying moves the price a lot, and it also means many tokens are still to come. The CoinGecko page lists a record of $13.62 on October 26, 2025, so EVAA is still 80.4% below it. Its low was $0.2818 on June 5, 2026; it now trades about 9.5 times higher. What we could not check

We did not find a project statement in the market data we read that explains the move. Who bought, and why, is unknown from these records.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the volume tells more than the price: $60 million of trading on an $18 million float means the same tokens changed hands several times a day, which points to fast traders more than long-term holders.
  • The likely risk is the supply gap. With 87% of tokens not yet circulating, the market value readers see is a small part of the fully diluted value.
  • For readers, the record matters as much as the run. Even at $2.67, EVAA sits 80% below its October 2025 high, so this weekend's gain wins back only a small part of earlier losses.

What's next

  • Whether 24-hour volume falls back toward its $1 million to $2 million level of last week
  • Any official EVAA Protocol post on token unlocks or listings
  • Gate's share of trading
