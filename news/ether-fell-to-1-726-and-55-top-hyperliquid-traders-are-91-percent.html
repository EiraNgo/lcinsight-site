Ether fell to $1,726, and 55 top Hyperliquid traders are 91% short with $366 million

DeepSea counts $365.62 million of ether shorts against $34.53 million of longs. The shorts' average entry is $2,143.90, so as a group they are well in profit. The biggest long is $6.89 million down.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Ether traded at $1,726.39 at 12:35 a.m. ET on Friday, down 4.0% in 24 hours and 10.8% in a week, CoinGecko shows.
  • DeepSea's ETH-PERP page counts 55 top Hyperliquid traders 91% short: $365.62 million short versus $34.53 million long.
  • The largest short, $51.80 million from $2,283.20, shows a $16.69 million paper gain. The largest long, $16.96 million from $2,427.30, shows a $6.89 million paper loss.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's ETH-PERP page follows the ether futures of top traders on Hyperliquid, an on-chain futures exchange. At 12:39 a.m. ET on Friday it counted 55 tracked wallets. They were 91% short (betting on a fall): $365.62 million at an average entry of $2,143.90, against $34.53 million long at an average of $2,223.10.

The page put ether at $1,727.70, down 3.95% in a day, with $1.06 billion of open ether futures on Hyperliquid and a funding rate of 0.0007%.

The four largest positions were all shorts:

  • Smart trader #25: $51.80 million at 20x, entry $2,283.20, paper gain $16.69 million
  • Smart trader #8: $45.94 million at 25x, entry $2,212, paper gain $12.90 million
  • Smart trader #89: $24.63 million at 25x, entry $1,817.10, paper gain $1.30 million
  • Smart trader #7: $24.11 million at 20x, entry $2,443.60, paper gain $9.98 million

The biggest long, Smart trader #170, held $16.96 million at 25x from $2,427.30, with a $6.89 million paper loss and a liquidation price of $1,261.40. The same label shows a $16.87 million short on DeepSea's HYPE-PERP page, $1.99 million in profit.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 20, 2026, 12:39 a.m. ET.

The price

Ether fell to a 24-hour low of $1,717.73 overnight, according to CoinGecko. At 12:35 a.m. ET on Friday it traded at $1,726.39, down 4.0% in a day and 10.8% in a week, and about 65% below its August 2025 record of $4,946.05.

A short is a bet that the price will fall. On Hyperliquid, traders use perpetual futures, contracts with no end date, often with borrowed money (leverage).

Old shorts and new shorts

With ether near $1,727, the price is about 19% below the short side's average entry of $2,143.90. That leaves most of these traders sitting on paper gains. In our screenshot at 1:00 a.m. ET the totals had barely moved: $368.65 million short and $34.92 million long, still 91% short.

Several big shorts, though, were opened much closer to the current price. Smart trader #89's $24.63 million bet started at $1,817.10 and would be closed near $1,912.60, about 11% above the page's price. Smart trader #10 holds $15.65 million from $1,770.90, and Smart trader #92 $10.25 million from $1,732.80.

The wider market

CoinGecko's Hyperliquid data, read at 12:58 a.m. ET, showed $532 million of open bets on Hyperliquid's ETH-USD contract, counted one side only, and $912 million traded in 24 hours. Doubled, that matches the $1.06 billion on DeepSea's page.

On CoinGecko's derivatives list at the same time, funding was positive on all 15 of the largest ether perpetual contracts, including 0.0034% on Binance's ETHUSDT, the biggest at $4.32 billion. Positive funding means longs pay shorts a small fee, so outside Hyperliquid's top traders the market is not leaning short.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the board shows a crowded trade. When nine in ten tracked dollars bet the same way, a quick bounce can force many shorts to buy back at once.
  • The newest shorts are the most exposed. Bets opened between $1,732 and $1,817 have far less room than the older ones from above $2,200.
  • The gap between Hyperliquid's top traders and positive funding on other exchanges suggests these big accounts are more bearish than the wider market.

What's next

  • Whether ether holds above Friday's $1,717.73 low.
  • Whether Smart trader #89's short nears its $1,912.60 liquidation price.
  • Whether funding on Binance's ETHUSDT turns negative.
