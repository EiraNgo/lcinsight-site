Lighter's LIT fell 15% in a day to $2.31, its lowest price since August

LIT lost 5.8% in the same hour bitcoin broke below $60,000 on Friday and kept sliding into the evening. It is down 29% in a week, CoinGecko data show.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • Lighter's LIT token traded at $2.31 at 8:03 p.m. ET on November 20, 2026, down 14.5% in 24 hours, CoinGecko data show. Its 24-hour range was $2.30 to $2.69.
  • The sharpest drop came between 10 a.m. and 11 a.m. ET, when LIT fell from about $2.55 to about $2.40, or 5.8%. That is the same hour bitcoin fell below $60,000.
  • LIT is down 28.9% in seven days and 40.2% in 30 days. CoinGecko's daily prices last showed it this low on August 17, 2026, at about $2.28. It is 70.7% below its $7.86 record from late December 2025.

A slow slide, then one hard hour

LIT drifted lower all of Thursday night, from about $2.70 at 8 p.m. ET to about $2.60 by midnight, according to hourly prices on CoinGecko. On Friday morning it was still near $2.55 at 10 a.m. ET. Then it fell to about $2.40 by 11 a.m. ET, a 5.8% drop in one hour.

That hour matches the move in bitcoin, which fell from about $61,679 to about $59,402 at the same time, as our story on bitcoin's break below $60,000 showed. LIT kept sliding to about $2.31 by 4 p.m. ET and was at $2.31 at 8:03 p.m. ET.

How far LIT has fallen

A week ago, on November 13, LIT's daily price on CoinGecko was about $3.22. It is now 28.9% lower over seven days and 40.2% lower over 30 days. CoinGecko's daily prices last went this low on August 17, 2026, at about $2.28, which makes Friday's price LIT's lowest in about three months. The token's record is $7.86, set in late December 2025, so it now trades 70.7% below that peak.

About 250 million LIT are in circulation, for a market value of about $577 million, rank #90 on CoinGecko. Valued at the full supply, the figure is about $2.27 billion. About $52 million of LIT traded in the past 24 hours.

A hard week for Lighter

The drop caps a rough week for the perpetual futures exchange. On November 16, LIT fell 13% in a day, as we reported. The same evening Lighter paused withdrawals after a trader lost about $10.4 million through a leaked API key, covered in our story on the loss. Friday's fall came with the whole market: CoinGecko shows bitcoin down 5.1% and ether down 7.8% in 24 hours.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think Friday's move was mostly the market, not Lighter: the steepest LIT hour lined up with bitcoin's break below $60,000.
  • Falling 15% while bitcoin fell 5% fits how smaller tokens usually move in a sell-off: harder in both directions.
  • The August low near $2.28 is the next level the price data shows. LIT was a few cents above it at the latest check.

What's next

  • Whether LIT holds above its August 17 low near $2.28
  • Whether bitcoin gets back above $60,000
  • Any update from Lighter on the API key loss and withdrawals
