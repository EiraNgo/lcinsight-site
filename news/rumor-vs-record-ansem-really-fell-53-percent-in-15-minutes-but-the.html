Rumor vs record: ANSEM really fell 53% in 15 minutes, but the hack story is unproven

We checked seven claims that spread on Tuesday, November 24, against CoinGecko, company releases and the Justice Department. Three hold up, two are close but off, and two cannot be confirmed from the record we could reach.

LC Insight Editorial Team4 min read

Key points

  • The ANSEM token fell from $0.03669 to $0.01714 between 3:55 a.m. and 4:10 a.m. ET on Tuesday, 53% in 15 minutes in CoinGecko's data. The claim that a hacked wallet caused it is unverified.
  • Strategy did announce 457 more bitcoin on November 23, and the Justice Department did report over $1 billion in stolen crypto frozen.
  • 0G still trades on Upbit: $58.4 million in 24 hours, at a price 65% above Binance's. We could not open Upbit's notice on deposits.

The claims

Tuesday's selloff came with a stream of quick claims on crypto social media and in market recaps: a memecoin crash blamed on a hack, a corporate bitcoin buy, a billion-dollar crypto freeze, a gaming token rally and an exchange warning. We checked seven of them against CoinGecko, company releases and the Justice Department. Each verdict sits in the table above.

Claims appear word for word, and the accounts behind them stay unnamed. An Unverified verdict says only that primary data could not settle the claim; it does not mean the claim is false. New to wallet hacks? Read how one signature can empty a wallet. Yesterday's edition: 4 of 6 crash claims held up.

The ANSEM crash is real; the cause is not settled

The price record is clear. In CoinGecko's five-minute data, the ANSEM token held near $0.0367 through the early hours of Tuesday, then fell to $0.01714 by 4:10 a.m. ET. Rolling 24-hour volume jumped from $0.9 million at 3 a.m. ET to $10.9 million at 5 a.m. ET. At 12:40 a.m. ET Wednesday CoinGecko put it at $0.0228, down 38.5% in 24 hours and 95% below its July record of $0.4436.

The story about why is not settled. A claim that a wallet tied to the trader known as Ansem was hacked spread within hours. No address, transaction or statement from the wallet owner came with it in what we could check, and we could not reach a block explorer to trace the claimed $2.15 million. So we make no finding either way, and nothing here suggests wrongdoing by anyone.

What holds up

Strategy's release confirms the 457 BTC purchase announced on Monday, November 23. The Justice Department's figures confirm more than $1 billion in crypto frozen or restrained (our story). And Big Time's jump is in the data (our story).

Two claims were close but off. The ANSEM drop was 53% in 15 minutes, not about 60% in an hour. And the market snapshot missed on the details. Bitcoin was under $60,000, but CoinGecko put its dominance at 57.7% and the whole market at $2.06 trillion.

0G: a 65% gap between two exchanges

We could not open Upbit's notice, so we leave the stated reason for any deposit halt out of this story. The trading data say a lot on their own. CoinGecko's ticker list shows Upbit's 0G/KRW pair trading $58.4 million in 24 hours, against $2.0 million on Binance's 0G/USDT pair. Upbit's price was $0.1536; Binance's was $0.0931.

A gap that wide usually lasts only when traders cannot move coins onto the pricier exchange to sell them. Globally, CoinGecko shows 0G down 16.0% in 24 hours and 29.1% in a week, with 617.5 million of its 1 billion tokens in circulation.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the ANSEM episode shows why a price chart and a cause are two different things. The 53% drop is a fact in the data; a hack is an accusation until the chain record or the wallet owner confirms it.
  • The 0G gap is the number to watch. If Upbit buyers are paying 65% more than the rest of the market, they could lose that premium quickly once coins can flow in again.
  • Even the true claims came rounded up. Sixty percent in an hour was really 53% in 15 minutes, and 58.5% bitcoin dominance was really 57.7%. Small errors like these spread fastest on a falling day.

What's next

  • Any statement from the wallet owner on the $2.15 million transfer
  • Whether Upbit's 0G price falls back toward Binance's
  • Strategy's next weekly bitcoin filing
