Ethena Pay adds Earn+, a leveraged USDe strategy on Aave V4, without publishing a rate

Two months after launching a money app on Avalanche, Ethena added Earn+, which supplies USDe and borrows USDT through a vault on Aave V4. There is no stated rate, and deposits are capped at $20,000 to $200,000 by tier.

LC Insight Editorial Team5 min read

Key points

  • Ethena Pay, a money app that holds balances in USDe, launched on Avalanche on September 1, 2026, in more than 50 countries.
  • Its new Earn+ option, added on October 28, puts deposits into a vault that supplies USDe and borrows USDT on Aave V4.
  • No rate has been published, deposits are capped at $20,000 to $200,000, and withdrawals take up to 24 hours.

What Earn+ is

Ethena Pay, the money app built by the team behind the USDe synthetic dollar, added a feature called Earn+ on October 28, 2026. Aave's announcement describes it as an in-app option that pays higher rewards on a user's dollar balance, powered by Aave V4 on Avalanche.

Users choose how much of their balance to move into Earn+. Aave says there is no lock-up period, and withdrawals are processed within 24 hours, landing back in the user's USD balance. Aave's post does not give a rate, and we found no published rate elsewhere. Deposits are capped at between $20,000 and $200,000, depending on the account tier.

How the money moves

Three parties sit behind the button, per Aave:

  • Upshift provides the vault that holds the deposits.
  • Steakhouse curates the vault and runs the strategy.
  • Aave V4 on Avalanche provides the lending market.

The strategy supplies USDe to Aave and borrows USDT from the liquidity pooled in Aave's Avalanche hub. In plain terms, it earns the yield on USDe while paying the borrowing rate on USDT, and the gap is the extra reward. Aave states that it "does not operate, curate, or manage the vault."

Aave V4 uses a hub-and-spoke design. Liquidity hubs pool deposits, and spokes are markets that borrow from them. The Avalanche hub is where the Earn+ strategy borrows its USDT.

What Ethena Pay is

Ethena launched Ethena Pay on September 1, 2026, as a mobile financial app built only on Avalanche, the Avalanche blog said on launch day. Digital dollar balances in the app are held in USDe. Users can hold money, send it, move between local currency and digital dollars, and spend through Visa's network, which Avalanche put at more than 130 million merchants.

The app was available on iOS in more than 50 countries at launch. Android, availability in the United States and the European Union, and accounts in more currencies were expected to follow, the post said. Aave's October post also put the launch footprint at about 50 countries.

Founder Guy Young said the app can tap the liquidity and apps already on Avalanche, then serve them through a simple experience where users never deal with DeFi (decentralized finance) directly. The Avalanche post said Ethena has processed more than $30 billion through its mint and redeem systems, and that USDe is integrated with more than 100 platforms and protocols.

Caps and USDe supply

Earn+ deposits are capped by account tier: $20,000 for Standard, $50,000 for Pro and $200,000 for VIP, according to Aave.

The bigger picture is USDe supply, since every dollar parked in the app is held as USDe. CoinGecko put USDe's market value at about $4.12 billion on September 1, the day Ethena Pay launched. It rose to about $4.77 billion by October 15, then eased to about $4.50 billion on November 1, according to CoinGecko. The token held close to its $1 target throughout.

Why this matters for USDe

Ethena's main product, USDe, earns its yield mostly from crypto markets, for example from the funding payments traders make on perpetual futures. That income rises and falls with trading activity. A consumer app gives Ethena another route to demand: everyday balances rather than traders.

Earn+ adds a second layer. Instead of just holding USDe, part of a user's balance goes into a leveraged strategy that depends on USDe paying more than it costs to borrow USDT. That makes the reward sensitive to two moving rates, neither of which Ethena controls. It also means a user's dollars sit in USDe and a DeFi vault, not in an insured bank deposit, so the risks differ from those of a normal savings account.

Supply growth so far has been modest. USDe added about $380 million of market value between the Ethena Pay launch and November 1, CoinGecko data shows, and some of that gain had already faded by the time Earn+ arrived.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • Earn+ shows where Ethena wants USDe demand to come from: everyday balances, not just traders. Every dollar parked in the app is USDe supply.
  • This is a carry trade. It pays while USDe yields more than USDT costs to borrow. If crypto funding rates fall or USDT gets expensive, the extra reward can shrink fast, and users should expect it to move.
  • Reach is the test. The app launched without the US, the EU or Android. Getting into those markets would be the clearest sign Ethena Pay can scale.

What's next

  • Publication of an Earn+ rate or rate history
  • Ethena Pay's planned Android, US and EU rollout
  • USDe supplied and USDT borrowed on Aave V4's Avalanche hub
