The top two traders' accounts shrank $206 million in two days, and $29.5 million left Aave

SMc060 and SM6e36 still lead DeepSea's 7-day profit board, but the assets DeepSea lists for them fell from $230.8 million to $24.6 million. On Arbitrum, their wallets pulled $29.5 million of USDC out of Aave in a day.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • DeepSea lists SMc060 at $8.92 million in total assets and SM6e36 at $15.68 million, down from $102.70 million and $128.08 million on November 15.
  • Both are still up big over 7 days: +$60.85 million and +$51.34 million, and DeepSea lists no open positions for either.
  • Their Arbitrum addresses sold $29.5 million of Aave's USDC deposit token in 3 trades in 24 hours, which means taking USDC out of Aave.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's smart money board ranks wallets by profit over 7 days. At 12:44 a.m. ET on Tuesday, No. 1 was SMc060 at +$60.85 million and No. 2 was SM6e36 at +$51.34 million. Both trade perpetual futures (bets on price with no expiry date) on Hyperliquid.

The surprise is the size of the accounts. SMc060's page shows $8.92 million in total assets and SM6e36's page shows $15.68 million. On November 15 at 6:35 a.m. ET, DeepSea listed $102.70 million and $128.08 million. That is a drop of about $206.2 million in two days, while both 7-day profits stayed positive. Neither page lists an open position.

DeepSea's 24-hour flow feed, read at 12:36 a.m. ET, shows the same addresses on Arbitrum. Aave's USDC deposit token, aArbUSDCn, had $29.5 million sold and nothing bought, by 2 wallets in 3 trades. Selling it means withdrawing USDC from Aave, the lending app.

  • SMc060's address: two sales of $10.00 million and $9.50 million, about 22 and 18 hours before our check
  • SM6e36's address: one $10 million sale; its page still lists $10.05 million of aArbUSDCn

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 17, 2026, 12:53 a.m. ET.

Where the money went

New to the tools? Read what DeepSea is.

We do not know. DeepSea does not say whether the $206 million left as withdrawals, moved to other wallets or was lost on trades. The 7-day profits argue against a large loss. SMc060's 30-day profit is listed at +$140.25 million and SM6e36's at +$115.55 million.

SMc060's Hyperliquid holdings list shows only about $8,700 of tokens, mostly USDC, plus 45 HYPE. DeepSea does not break down the rest of its $8.92 million.

Both trade lists are also full of tokens with look-alike names spelled with unusual characters to resemble USDC, shown with no dollar value. That pattern is common in address-poisoning spam, where scammers send fake tokens hoping a victim copies the wrong address later. We treat those rows as noise, not trades.

Checking the numbers

The feed's token row and wallet rows agree: $19.5 million from SMc060's address plus $10 million from SM6e36's equals the $29.5 million total, in 3 trades.

For market context, CoinGecko showed bitcoin at $64,816 and ether at $1,834.62 at 12:54 a.m. ET, both up on the day.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • DeepSea does not show where the money went. Both accounts still show large 7-day and 30-day profits, so the drop in assets is not shown as a trading loss.
  • With no open positions listed, the two biggest winners on the board have no market exposure on Hyperliquid that DeepSea can see. A new deposit to either account would be the first sign they are trading again.
  • A leaderboard rank built on 7-day profit can stay high after a trader has already left the table.

What's next

  • Whether new USDC reaches either Hyperliquid account
  • The remaining $10.05 million of aArbUSDCn at SM6e36's address
  • Whether either wallet opens a new position
