Rain (RAIN) has given back 40% since Monday's peak, and HTX carried half the trading

RAIN topped out near $0.0079 early Monday, November 16, then slid to about $0.0048 by Tuesday evening. Only about $20 million traded against a market value of $3.4 billion.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows RAIN at $0.004752 at 8:02 p.m. ET on November 17, 2026, down 20.6% in 24 hours and about 40% below its hourly peak of about $0.0079 at 4 a.m. ET on Monday.
  • The 24-hour low was about $0.00443 at 12:30 p.m. ET on Tuesday. Over the same day CoinGecko shows bitcoin up 0.1% and ether up 0.1%.
  • About $20.5 million traded in 24 hours, roughly 0.6% of RAIN's $3.41 billion market value. HTX's RAIN/USDT pair carried about 50% of it.

The move

Rain's token RAIN jumped early on Monday, November 16, 2026, as we reported at the time. CoinGecko's hourly prices show the climb did not stop at $0.0052: RAIN went from about $0.0035 late Sunday to about $0.0079 at 4 a.m. ET on Monday, more than double in a few hours, according to CoinGecko.

It has been falling since. RAIN traded near $0.0068 at 11 p.m. ET on Monday, about $0.0055 at 8 a.m. ET on Tuesday and touched a low of about $0.00443 at 12:30 p.m. ET. At 8:02 p.m. ET it was $0.004752, down 20.6% in 24 hours and about 40% below Monday's peak. It is still up 20.6% over seven days.

Bitcoin and ether were flat over the same 24 hours, up 0.1% each on CoinGecko, so the drop was RAIN's own. The token is about 76% below its all-time high of $0.01946, set on August 25, 2026.

Where it traded

CoinGecko lists about $20.5 million of RAIN volume in 24 hours across 15 markets. HTX's RAIN/USDT pair handled about $10.1 million (49.5%), BingX about $4.2 million (20.4%), Gate about $2.9 million (14.4%) and BloFin about $1.5 million (7.4%). Those four venues carried more than 90% of the trading.

That is thin for a token CoinGecko ranks No. 25 by market value. When a few order books set the price and daily turnover is under 1% of the market value, moves of this size can happen in either direction on modest flows.

What Rain is, and the supply

Rain is an options and prediction-market protocol on Arbitrum, according to its CoinGecko listing and its website. The RAIN token contract on Arbitrum is 0x2511...9099d.

CoinGecko counts about 714.4 billion RAIN in circulation out of a 1.15 trillion maximum, or about 62%. At $0.004752 that is a market value of about $3.41 billion and a fully diluted value of about $5.45 billion. Rain has not given a reason for the drop in any source we checked, and this story does not suggest one.

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a token that doubled in hours on thin trading and then gave back 40% in a day and a half is showing how little liquidity sits behind its headline market value. About 38% of the maximum supply is not yet circulating. This is not investment advice.
