Injective jumps 12% to $7.48 with no confirmed cause. INJ is still 86% below its record

INJ was one of CoinGecko's trending coins on November 17 with a 12% daily gain. We checked the numbers and found no official announcement that explains it.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko showed INJ at $7.48 at 6:40 a.m. ET on November 17, up 12.1% in 24 hours but down 6.3% over seven days.
  • Trading volume was $93.0 million against a market value of $748.4 million, about 12.4% of market value changing hands in a day.
  • The record is $52.62 (March 14, 2024). INJ is still 85.8% below it. We found no official Injective notice that explains the jump.

What the market data shows

CoinGecko listed Injective (INJ), the token of a blockchain built for trading apps, at $7.48 at 6:40 a.m. ET on Tuesday, November 17, 2026. That is 12.1% higher than a day earlier. The 24-hour low was $6.62.

The coin also appeared on CoinGecko's trending list that morning, which tracks what users search for most. An earlier snapshot a few minutes before showed a gain of about 9%, so the move was still growing as we checked.

A bounce inside a weak week

The jump looks smaller on a longer view. CoinGecko shows INJ down 6.3% over seven days. Its record is $52.62, set on March 14, 2024, so the price is still 85.8% below that peak.

The market value is $748.4 million and the circulating supply is 100 million INJ. The same page lists $93.0 million of 24-hour volume. So roughly one-eighth of the coin's value traded in a day, a busy session for a coin of this size.

What we could not confirm

We looked for an official reason: a post on Injective's own channels, a governance vote or a listing notice. In the time we had, we found no primary-source announcement dated November 16 or 17 that explains the move. Price pages and social feeds offer many theories, but those are not records, so we leave them out.

That does not mean there is no cause. It means the cause is unconfirmed. Until Injective or an exchange publishes something, the honest description is a 12% rise with no verified trigger.

Two practical points help readers who see the coin trending. First, a trending list shows attention, not a reason. Second, a coin that rises 12% on a day when Bitcoin is nearly flat is moving on its own news or its own flows, so the missing explanation matters. CoinMarketCap carries the same market-data fields and is the second place to compare the price against CoinGecko.

We will update this report if Injective, an exchange or a regulator publishes a notice that explains the move. Until then, readers should treat any single explanation they see online as a guess.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think a one-day gain with no announcement behind it is hard to read. The 6% weekly decline shows the rise has not yet changed the trend.
  • The volume is worth watching more than the price. $93.0 million is a strong day for a $748 million coin, which suggests traders were active, though the data does not say who.

What's next

  • Any post from Injective's official channels or a governance proposal that explains the move.
  • Whether the 24-hour volume stays near $93 million or fades back.
