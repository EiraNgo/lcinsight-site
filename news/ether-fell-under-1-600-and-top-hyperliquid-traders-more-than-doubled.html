Ether fell under $1,600, and top Hyperliquid traders more than doubled their longs to $76M

The 55 wallets DeepSea tracks on ether went from 91% short to 79% short in a day. Most of the new bets on a rise were opened near $1,600, and the biggest one uses just 3x leverage.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Ether was at $1,561.81 early Saturday (12:41 a.m. ET), down 9.6% in a day and 17.1% in a week, CoinGecko shows. The 24-hour low was $1,542.97.
  • DeepSea's ETH-PERP page counts 55 top Hyperliquid wallets 79% short: $288.53 million short and $75.83 million long. A day earlier the long side was $34.53 million.
  • Open bets on Hyperliquid's ETH-USD contract fell to $468 million from $532 million a day earlier, CoinGecko's data show, and funding turned slightly negative.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's ETH-PERP page follows the ether futures of the biggest traders it tracks on Hyperliquid, an on-chain futures exchange. At 12:39 a.m. ET on Saturday it counted 55 wallets: 79% short (bets on a fall) with $288.53 million at an average entry of $2,155.70, and 21% long with $75.83 million at an average of $1,604.30.

At 12:39 a.m. ET on Friday the same page showed 55 wallets, 91% short: $365.62 million short and $34.53 million long, with the longs' average entry at $2,223.10. The long side grew 2.2 times in a day, and its average entry dropped by more than $600, so most of the new longs were opened near today's price.

The page put ether at $1,563.50, down 9.65% in a day, with $932.55 million of open ether futures on Hyperliquid, down from $1.06 billion, and a funding rate of -0.0027%.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 21, 2026, 12:39 a.m. ET.

Who bought the drop

The largest long on the page is an unranked wallet with $21.93 million at 3x leverage from $1,554.80, a low setting that puts its liquidation price at $893.43. Smart trader #57 holds $15.69 million at 20x from $1,624, with a $552,200 paper loss. Smart trader #209 holds $8.63 million from $1,549 and Smart trader #176 $7.06 million from $1,608.70.

A long is a bet that the price will rise. Leverage, such as 20x, lets a trader bet 20 times the money put up, and the liquidation price is where the exchange closes the bet.

In our screenshot at 12:49 a.m. ET the board read $76.05 million long and $287.75 million short, still 79% short.

The big shorts are still there

The two largest bets are still old shorts. Smart trader #25 holds $47.04 million from $2,283.20, up $21.46 million on paper, and Smart trader #8 holds $38.88 million from $2,206.70, up $16.12 million. On Friday they showed $51.80 million and $45.94 million.

Some of the short side's $77 million drop is just the lower ether price shrinking the dollar value of unchanged bets. Revalued at today's price, Friday's short book comes to roughly $331 million, so roughly $42 million came from bets closed or cut. One new short sits close to the price: Smart trader #11 with $13.35 million from $1,579.10.

The wider market

On the exchange as a whole, the ETH-USD contract held $468 million of open bets at 12:41 a.m. ET, against $532 million a day earlier, according to CoinGecko's Hyperliquid listing. Daily turnover was $2.29 billion. The funding rate, a periodic payment between the two sides that ties the contract to the spot price, sat just below zero, which means shorts were paying longs.

Ether is about 68% below its August 2025 record of $4,946.05, CoinGecko shows. Hyperliquid's own feed and DefiLlama were out of our reach overnight.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the shift is real but small next to the shorts. Longs grew by about $41 million, while the short side still holds almost four dollars for every dollar of longs.
  • The biggest new long uses 3x, not 20x or 40x. That looks like a bet built to survive a further drop rather than a quick trade.
  • Most shorts on the page are deep in profit, which gives them room to wait. A fast bounce would test the newer shorts opened near $1,600 first, such as Smart trader #11 from $1,579.10.

What's next

  • Whether the long side on DeepSea's ETH board keeps growing past $75.83 million.
  • Whether ether breaks Saturday's 24-hour low of $1,542.97.
  • Whether funding on Hyperliquid's ETH-USD contract stays negative.
