Bitcoin fell 3%, yet its Hyperliquid bets held at $1.23 billion. HYPE's shrank $64 million

Open bets on Hyperliquid's 179 crypto markets slipped to $3.25 billion, CoinGecko data show. Bitcoin's market did not shrink in five days, while HYPE and ether bets fell and Hyperliquid dropped from third to sixth by open interest.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Hyperliquid's 179 crypto markets held $3.25 billion in open bets at 6:31 a.m. ET Friday, down from $3.45 billion on November 15, CoinGecko's tickers show.
  • Bitcoin's market held $1.23 billion, the same as on November 15. HYPE's fell to $603 million from $667 million and ether's to $539 million from $576 million.
  • Funding was negative on 46 of the 179 crypto markets. Hyperliquid now ranks sixth by open interest, down from third on November 15.

What moved

Bitcoin was down 3.4% in 24 hours at $61,758 at 6:34 a.m. ET Friday, CoinGecko shows. On Hyperliquid, traders take perpetual futures, price bets that never expire. Open interest, or open bets, is the money still in positions not yet closed.

CoinGecko's Hyperliquid tickers, last traded at 6:31 a.m. ET, put the 179 crypto markets at $3.25 billion of open bets. On November 15 they held $3.45 billion, and on November 13, $4.04 billion. They traded $7.85 billion in 24 hours.

Where bets shrank, and where they did not

Bitcoin's market held $1.23 billion, the same as five days earlier, even as the price slid. HYPE, Hyperliquid's own token, fell 5.6% on the exchange to $64.74, and its open bets shrank $64 million to $603 million. Ether's shrank $37 million to $539 million.

The biggest price drops among large markets came in ZEC, down 13.0% with $141 million open, NEAR, down 11.7%, and LIT, down 11.4%. Funding, the small fee that keeps a perpetual near the spot price, was negative on 46 of 179 crypto markets, meaning shorts paid longs there. On bitcoin, ether and HYPE it was flat to slightly positive.

CoinGecko's tickers do not report liquidations, so how much of the shrinkage was forced selling is not visible in this data.

Hyperliquid slips in the ranking

CoinGecko's ranking of derivatives venues puts Hyperliquid sixth, at 87,467 bitcoin worth of open bets, behind Binance (430,678), OKX (109,235), CoinW (96,907), MEXC (89,749) and XT (89,723). On November 15 it was third, at 87,686. Its own total barely moved. Rivals grew past it.

Its 172 stock, index and commodity markets held $2.15 billion, 39.8% of the exchange's $5.39 billion in open bets. DefiLlama, which reads Hyperliquid's on-chain perps data, put open interest at $7.712 billion and 24-hour volume at $13.709 billion, a wider count than CoinGecko's tickers.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think bitcoin bets holding steady through a 3% drop means traders kept positions open rather than cutting risk, so the next sharp move may hit more leverage.
  • HYPE losing bets faster than bitcoin fits a market pulling back from the exchange's own token first.
  • Hyperliquid's drop to sixth says more about its rivals than about the exchange. Its own total held near 87,500 bitcoin while others grew past it.

What's next

  • Bitcoin's Hyperliquid open bets against $1.23 billion.
  • HYPE open bets against $600 million.
  • Whether Hyperliquid regains a top-three spot by open interest.
