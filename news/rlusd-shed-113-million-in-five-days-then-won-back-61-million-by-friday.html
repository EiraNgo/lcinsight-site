RLUSD shed $113 million in five days, then won back $61 million by Friday night

Ripple's dollar token turned back up on Friday afternoon in CoinGecko's data, even as DeepSea's shark wallets sold slightly more RLUSD than they bought.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • RLUSD's market value on CoinGecko fell from $2.874 billion on November 22 to $2.761 billion at 6 a.m. ET on Friday, November 27, 2026, a drop of about $113 million.
  • It then climbed to $2.822 billion by 12:41 a.m. ET Saturday, about $61 million above Friday's low. CoinGecko lists a 24-hour gain of $40.9 million, or 1.47%.
  • In the same day, shark wallets sold $49,299,912 of RLUSD on Ethereum and bought $43,422,824, DeepSea's table built at 12:36 a.m. ET Saturday shows.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's 24-hour flow table, built at 12:36 a.m. ET on November 28, has an RLUSD line on Ethereum: shark wallets sold $49,299,912 and bought $43,422,824. That makes them net sellers of about $5.9 million, the opposite of the token's growth on CoinGecko over the same day. Sharks are wallets DeepSea tags for their large trading size.

DeepSea's RLUSD page tracks the Ethereum contract only. It shows a market value of $2.07 billion there, $91.95 million of trading volume in 24 hours and a whale net inflow of $38.98 million over seven days. The largest holder it lists, an unlabeled wallet ending in 6e22, holds 17.68% of the Ethereum supply.

The wallet ending in 3425, which we covered on November 24 and November 27, is seventh on that list with 4.81%. Its DeepSea page shows no RLUSD among its latest trades, which were in USDC, USDT, ether and LINK.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 28, 2026, 12:45 a.m. ET.

Down for five days

RLUSD is a dollar token issued by Ripple. Because it trades at about $1, its market value is close to the number of tokens in circulation. When the value falls, tokens have been taken out of the market.

CoinGecko's daily data shows RLUSD at $2.874 billion on November 22. It slipped on most days after that. Its hourly data puts the low at $2.761 billion at 6 a.m. ET on Friday, November 27.

The Friday turn

The rebound came in the afternoon. CoinGecko's hourly figures show RLUSD at $2.788 billion at 1 p.m. ET Friday and $2.822 billion at 5 p.m. ET, a rise of about $34 million in four hours. It has held near that level since.

At 12:40 a.m. ET Saturday, CoinGecko counted 2,821,558,332 RLUSD in circulation and a 24-hour gain of $40.9 million, or 1.47%. The price stayed between $0.9999 and $1.0002 over the past two days. Daily trading volume was $288.8 million.

Two counts, one token

CoinGecko and DeepSea show different totals. CoinGecko puts RLUSD at $2.82 billion, while DeepSea's Ethereum page shows $2.07 billion. CoinGecko's hourly data does not say on which network the new tokens appeared.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the Friday turn looks like fresh RLUSD being issued rather than traders swapping: about $34 million arrived in four hours, while DeepSea's sharks were net sellers over the same day.
  • The gap between CoinGecko's $2.82 billion and DeepSea's $2.07 billion on Ethereum likely reflects RLUSD held on other networks, such as the XRP Ledger. We could not check which network grew.
  • One day of growth does not undo the slide. RLUSD is still about $52 million below where it stood on November 22.

What's next

  • Whether RLUSD holds above $2.82 billion or resumes its slide.
  • Whether DeepSea's sharks turn back into net buyers of RLUSD.
  • Whether the top Ethereum holder, the wallet ending in 6e22, changes its 17.68% share.
