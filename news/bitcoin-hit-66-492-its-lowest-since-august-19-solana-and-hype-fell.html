Bitcoin hit $66,492, its lowest since August 19. Solana and HYPE fell twice as fast

Bitcoin set a new low for this slide overnight, $1,430 under the low in our Friday report, and is down 11.5% in a week, CoinGecko data show. Solana, Hyperliquid's HYPE and ether all fell to levels last seen in mid-August, and XRP slipped below $1.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Bitcoin traded at $67,202 at 6:43 a.m. ET on November 14, 2026, down 3.0% in 24 hours and 11.5% in seven days, CoinGecko data show. Its 24-hour low was $66,492.
  • That low is $1,430 below the $67,922 low in our November 13 report. CoinGecko's hourly history last showed bitcoin lower on August 19.
  • Solana fell 5.9% in a day and HYPE 6.8%, about twice bitcoin's drop. Ether and Solana hit their lowest since August 16, and XRP traded under $1.

What moved

Bitcoin kept falling overnight. At 6:43 a.m. ET on November 14, 2026, it traded at $67,202, down 3.0% in 24 hours, CoinGecko data show. Its 24-hour low was $66,492, and the weakest hour in CoinGecko's hourly data came at 11 p.m. ET on Friday.

Our November 13 report had a 24-hour low of $67,922. The new low is $1,430 lower. Bitcoin is now down 11.5% in seven days and 17.2% in 30 days, and 46.7% below its $126,080 record from October 6, 2025. CoinGecko's hourly history last showed it below $66,492 on August 19, 2026, when it traded near $65,888.

Solana and HYPE fell hardest

Solana and HYPE fell about twice as much as bitcoin in the last day, while ether and XRP fell about as much, CoinGecko data show.

  • Solana (SOL): $75.91, down 5.9% in 24 hours and 15.9% in a week. Its low of $74.95 was its weakest since the evening of August 16, ET.
  • Hyperliquid (HYPE): $68.73, down 6.8% in a day. Its low of $67.22 was the lowest since August 19. HYPE is 29.8% below its September 23, 2026, record of $97.96.
  • Ether (ETH): $1,908.82, down 2.6% in a day and 15.0% in a week. Its low of $1,880.84 was the lowest since the evening of August 16, ET.
  • XRP: $0.9912, down 3.0% in a day. Its 24-hour low of $0.9799 was below its August low in CoinGecko's hourly data.

BNB fell 3.5% to $617.01 and dogecoin 3.5% to $0.0630. CoinGecko put the whole crypto market at $2.27 trillion, down from the $2.32 trillion in our Friday report. Bitcoin made up 59.5% of it.

What we left out

Liquidation totals, the forced closing of borrowed bets, circulate online every time prices drop. They come from data aggregators that we could not check against exchange records, so we do not report them here. We found no single news event in the primary record that lines up with the overnight leg down. The low hour, 11 p.m. ET on Friday, was the same for bitcoin, ether, Solana and HYPE.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the key fact is breadth: bitcoin, ether, Solana and HYPE all hit their low in the same hour, which looks like one market-wide wave of selling, not news about any single token.
  • Mid-August is now the reference point. CoinGecko's hourly history last showed bitcoin below Saturday's low on August 19, near $65,888, about $600 lower. Its August low was deeper, near $62,470 on August 1.
  • Solana and HYPE falling twice as far as bitcoin in a day fits a common pattern in sell-offs: traders cut riskier tokens first. That is our reading, not a record of who sold.

What's next

  • Whether bitcoin holds above $65,888, its last hourly reading below Saturday's low, on August 19.
  • U.S. spot bitcoin ETF flows when markets reopen on Monday, November 16.
  • Whether XRP gets back above $1.
