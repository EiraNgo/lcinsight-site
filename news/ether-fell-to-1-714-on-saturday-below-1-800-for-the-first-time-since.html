Ether fell to $1,714 on Saturday, below $1,800 for the first time since July

Ether fell below $1,900 late Friday and $1,800 on Saturday afternoon, then hit $1,714.75 at 8:45 p.m. ET, CoinGecko data show. That is its lowest price since early July, and ether has lost 18.9% in a week.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Ether fell under $1,900 at 11 p.m. ET on November 13 and under $1,800 at 4 p.m. ET on November 14, CoinGecko's price history shows. Its low was $1,714.75 at 8:45 p.m. ET.
  • CoinGecko's daily prices last showed ether that low on July 2, when it was $1,698.56. The last daily price under $1,800 before this was on July 13.
  • Ether traded at $1,807.37 at 12:44 a.m. ET on November 15: down 4.6% in 24 hours, 18.9% in seven days and 63.5% below its $4,946.05 record.

What happened

Ether, the coin of the Ethereum network, fell below $1,900 and then $1,800 in less than a day, and hit $1,714.75 on Saturday night, its lowest since early July. CoinGecko's hourly prices show it at $1,910.41 at 10 p.m. ET on Friday, November 13, and $1,882.26 at 11 p.m. ET. The last hourly reading under $1,900 before that was on August 18.

Ether held near $1,900 through Saturday morning, then slid. It was $1,785.63 at 4 p.m. ET on Saturday, November 14, its first hourly price under $1,800 in the 90 days of data. The fall ran on into the evening.

The low and the bounce

CoinGecko's five-minute prices put the bottom at $1,714.75 at 8:45 p.m. ET on Saturday. Its 24-hour range lists a low of $1,713.65. Bitcoin bottomed in the same five minutes, at $62,123.97 on the same feed, with a 24-hour low of $62,081.

The bounce was quick. Ether was back at $1,794.25 by 9:20 p.m. ET and traded at $1,807.37 at 12:44 a.m. ET on Sunday, November 15. That leaves it 5.4% above the low but still under $1,900.

Lowest since July

CoinGecko's one-year daily record answers that. The last day ether was priced at or below Saturday's low was July 2, 2026, at $1,698.56. The last daily price under $1,800 came on July 13. The year's lowest daily price was $1,566.01 on June 25.

The slide has been fast. Ether's 90-day high was $2,782.33 on September 21, so it is now about 35% lower. Its record of $4,946.05 was set on August 24, 2025.

Ether against the market

Ether fell harder than bitcoin. CoinGecko's market table at 12:44 a.m. ET shows ether down 18.9% in seven days and 25.6% in 30 days. Bitcoin was down 14.3% and 20.8%. Solana lost 20.4% in a week and XRP 16.2%.

Ether's market value stood at $220.9 billion, with $20.5 billion traded in 24 hours. Money had also been leaving U.S. spot ether ETFs: they lost $311.8 million in the week to November 13, CoinMarketCap's flow data show.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the $1,800 break matters because it takes ether back to where it traded in early July. Most of what it gained in the summer climb to $2,782 is gone.
  • The bounce from $1,714 to above $1,800 within hours shows buyers were waiting near the July lows. One bounce on a Saturday night is thin evidence, though.
  • Ether falling more than bitcoin fits the pattern of this sell-off, where riskier coins drop faster when bitcoin slides.

What's next

  • Whether ether can climb back above $1,900, the level it lost on Friday night.
  • The July 2 area near $1,700 if the low is retested.
  • U.S. spot ether ETF flows when trading reopens on Monday, November 16.
