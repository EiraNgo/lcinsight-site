Two top-10 traders are short $117.7 million of bitcoin and ether, up $26.6 million on paper

SMdfdb and SM7da2 sit No. 6 and No. 8 on DeepSea's 7-day board. One holds a $55 million ether short from $2,283, the other a $56.3 million bitcoin short from $78,440.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • SMdfdb (No. 6, +$9.89 million in 7 days) has a $55.01 million ether short at 20x with a $13.48 million paper gain.
  • SM7da2 (No. 8, +$9.46 million) is short $56.34 million of bitcoin at 8x and $6.36 million of ether, up $13.15 million on paper.
  • They bet with the crowd: DeepSea counts top traders 81% short on bitcoin and 86% short on ether.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's smart money board ranks wallets by 7-day profit. Most of its top names have been covered here already. Two in the top 10 had not: SMdfdb at No. 6 and SM7da2 at No. 8, both Hyperliquid futures traders. Their owners are unknown.

SMdfdb's page, read at 12:53 a.m. ET: total assets $34.64 million, almost all of it ($34.11 million) in USDC. Profit: +$9.89 million in 7 days, +$8.46 million in 30 days and +$30.51 million in 90 days. Its one open position is an ether short of $55.01 million at 20x leverage, entered near $2,300, with a $13.48 million paper gain. Its latest trades, 12 days ago, were ether sales of up to 165 ETH ($401,200).

SM7da2's page: total assets $12.87 million, including $11.37 million in USDC. Profit: +$9.46 million in 7 days and +$17.51 million in 30 days. Open positions:

  • Bitcoin short: $56.34 million, 8x, entry near $78,400, paper gain $11.85 million
  • Ether short: $6.36 million, 10x, entry near $2,200, paper gain $1.30 million

Its latest trades, two days ago, were bitcoin sales, the largest 3.737 BTC ($244,600).

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 17, 2026, 12:53 a.m. ET.

Part of a crowded bet

New to the tools? Read what DeepSea is.

DeepSea's BTC-PERP page at 12:58 a.m. ET counted 85 tracked top traders 81% short: $691.79 million short against $163.11 million long. Its ETH-PERP page at 1:00 a.m. ET showed 57 wallets 86% short: $372.25 million against $60.80 million. SM7da2's bitcoin short is about 8% of those bitcoin shorts; SMdfdb's ether short is about 15% of the ether shorts.

Positions matching both, with the same size and leverage, appear on those pages under anonymous ranks, with exact entries: $78,440 for the bitcoin short and $2,283.20 for the ether short.

Checking the numbers

CoinGecko showed bitcoin at $64,816 and ether at $1,834.62 at 12:54 a.m. ET. At those prices, a $56.34 million bitcoin short from $78,440 should be up about $11.84 million, and a $55.01 million ether short from $2,283.20 about $13.45 million. Both match DeepSea within rounding.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think most of the two wallets' gains are still on paper: about $26.6 million of open profit against $19.35 million of combined 7-day profit.
  • SMdfdb's 20x leverage leaves little room: DeepSea lists its liquidation price near $2,913, about 59% above ether's price, but losses would mount long before that.
  • When this many big traders lean one way, a sharp move against them can force many to close at once. That is a risk to the crowd, not a forecast.

What's next

  • Whether SMdfdb cuts the ether short
  • Bitcoin's distance from SM7da2's $78,440 entry
  • Changes in DeepSea's 81% and 86% short shares
