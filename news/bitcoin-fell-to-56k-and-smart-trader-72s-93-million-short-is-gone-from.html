Bitcoin fell to $56K, and Smart trader #72's $93 million short is gone from the board

The wallet behind Friday's biggest bitcoin short now holds only $10.2 million in USDC and shows an $8.4 million weekly gain on DeepSea. Top Hyperliquid traders cut their bitcoin shorts by $203 million.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Bitcoin traded at $55,934 at 12:41 a.m. ET on Saturday, down 9.2% in a day and 15.7% in a week, CoinGecko shows. The 24-hour high, $62,224, stayed below Smart trader #72's $63,169 liquidation price.
  • DeepSea's wallet page for Smart trader #72 shows no open bet, $10.22 million in USDC and a seven-day profit of $8.40 million. Its last trades, about 10 hours earlier, were bitcoin buys near $58,250.
  • DeepSea's BTC-PERP page counts 87 top Hyperliquid wallets 94% short with $552.45 million, down from $755.29 million on Friday morning. Their longs fell to $38.15 million.

What DeepSea's data shows

On Friday at 6:37 a.m. ET the largest bet on DeepSea's BTC-PERP page was Smart trader #72: a $92.76 million short at 40x from $61,823. A short is a bet that the price will fall. At 12:39 a.m. ET on Saturday the label was no longer among the page's 25 largest positions.

DeepSea's wallet page for the same Hyperliquid address, 0x1f25…f925, read at 12:40 a.m., shows no open position. It lists $10.22 million in USDC as its only holding, $10.64 million in total assets and +$8.40 million of profit over seven days ($9.40 million over 30 days). Its latest trades, about 10 hours before our check, were a run of bitcoin buys, the biggest 8.697 BTC worth $506,600, which works out to about $58,250 per coin.

Buying back is how a short is closed. The page does not label these buys as the exit, so we cannot say at what price the whole $92.76 million bet ended. At $58,250, a short from $61,823 would be up about 5.8%.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 21, 2026, 12:39 a.m. ET.

The board shrank

The BTC-PERP page now counts 87 wallets, down from 93 on Friday morning. They are 94% short: $552.45 million at an average entry of $70,367, against $38.15 million long at $69,027. On Friday they held $755.29 million short and $78.92 million long. In our screenshot at 12:48 a.m. ET the board read $549.17 million short and $38.09 million long.

Part of the drop is the price itself, because the same bet is worth fewer dollars when bitcoin is cheaper. At Saturday's price, Friday's shorts would be worth about $685 million, so roughly $130 million came from bets closed or cut.

Old shorts sit on bigger gains. Smart trader #13 shows $48.84 million short from $78,440, up $19.36 million, and Smart trader #7 $46.08 million from $76,774, up $17.14 million. John Wick trimmed to $39.77 million. New shorts opened near the lows include Smart trader #15 with $16.55 million from $58,522 and Smart trader #198 with $16.20 million at 40x from $60,651.

The biggest long, Auros, holds $19.24 million at 40x. Its average entry fell to $59,273 from $61,934, and it shows a $1.15 million paper loss.

The wider market

CoinGecko's Hyperliquid data, read at 12:41 a.m. ET, put open bets on the BTC-USD contract at $1.03 billion, down from $1.23 billion on Friday morning, with $6.69 billion traded in 24 hours. Funding, the small fee that keeps a perpetual near the spot price, was slightly negative, so shorts paid longs. DeepSea's page shows $2.05 billion open, counted in a different way.

We could not reach Hyperliquid's own data feed or DefiLlama this morning to check the wallet's trade history directly.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the board tells the story of the night. The biggest new short of Friday morning is gone after the price fell, and the wallet now sits in USDC with a large weekly gain.
  • The group did not turn to buying. Longs halved again, and fresh shorts were opened within a few percent of the low.
  • Until a full trade history is visible, the exit price of the $92.76 million short is an estimate, not a record.

What's next

  • Whether Smart trader #72's wallet opens a new bitcoin bet.
  • Whether the long side on DeepSea's board falls below $38.15 million.
  • Whether bitcoin breaks Saturday's 24-hour low of $55,615.
