80 sharks bought $8.1 million of Tether's gold token as ether fell 5%, while PAXG was sold

While ether dropped 5%, large wallets on DeepSea's live feed piled into XAUt. The rival gold token, PAXG, went the other way.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • In the 24 hours to 6:36 a.m. ET on November 9, 80 shark wallets bought $8.11 million of XAUt (Tether Gold) and sold $47,430, DeepSea's live feed shows.
  • PAXG, Paxos's gold token, was net sold: $1.88 million out and $78,796 in, by 25 wallets.
  • CoinGecko showed both gold tokens up about 1% in 24 hours at 6:41 a.m. ET, while ether fell 5.2%.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's live feed adds up trades by shark wallets (large wallets it tracks) on several chains. In the 24 hours to 6:36 a.m. ET on November 9, XAUt on Ethereum drew 80 different sharks. Together they bought $8.11 million and sold just $47,430. XAUt is Tether's gold-backed token, and its price tracks gold.

One wallet did about half of the buying. DeepSea's page for Hold4312 shows two XAUt buys of 500 tokens, $2.02 million each, about three hours before our check. That leaves roughly $4.07 million spread over the other 79 wallets.

The other big gold token moved the opposite way. Sharks sold $1.88 million of PAXG and bought $78,796. That row had 25 wallets.

DeepSea's XAUt token page, read at about 6:42 a.m. ET, showed a 7-day whale net flow of plus $31.35 million, $29.65 million of DEX liquidity and $8.37 million of 24-hour DEX volume.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 9, 2026, 6:42 a.m. ET.

Gold tokens vs ether

CoinGecko showed XAUt at $4,093 at 6:41 a.m. ET, up 1.0% in 24 hours, with a $3.31 billion market cap and $210.9 million of trading. PAXG was at $4,096, also up 1.0%, with a $1.83 billion market cap and $93.3 million of trading. Ether was at $2,121, down 5.2%.

How big is it?

The sharks' $8.11 million of XAUt buying is about 3.8% of the token's $210.9 million of 24-hour trading on CoinGecko. It is small next to the market cap. What stands out is the number of wallets: 80 sharks on one side, almost no sellers.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the split between XAUt and PAXG shows big wallets choosing between gold tokens while staying in gold. The PAXG selling was less than a quarter of the XAUt buying.
  • One wallet made about half of the XAUt purchases, so the total says more about breadth (80 wallets) than about a crowd of equal-sized buyers.
  • Gold tokens rose on a day ether fell. The data shows the trades, not the reasons behind them.

What's next

  • Whether the 7-day whale net flow into XAUt keeps rising
  • Whether PAXG selling continues
  • XAUt's market cap on CoinGecko over the week
