Rain (RAIN) jumped 43% in about an hour overnight, and it is still 74% below its August record

RAIN climbed from about $0.0036 to $0.0052 between 12:50 and 1:55 a.m. ET on thin trading, with BingX carrying more than half of the day's volume.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows RAIN at $0.00504 at 2:04 a.m. ET on November 16, 2026, up 38.8% in 24 hours and 37.1% in the last hour.
  • It rose from about $0.00363 at 12:50 a.m. ET to about $0.00520 at 1:55 a.m. ET, a gain of about 43%, then eased back.
  • About $20.6 million traded in 24 hours against a market value near $3.6 billion. BingX carried 56.8% of it.

The move

Rain's token RAIN was flat for most of Sunday, November 15, 2026, according to CoinGecko. It touched its low of the day, about $0.00344, at 11:30 p.m. ET. After midnight it started to climb, and between 1:25 and 1:55 a.m. ET on Monday it jumped from about $0.00394 to about $0.00520.

From $0.00363 at 12:50 a.m. ET to the $0.00520 peak, that is a rise of about 43% in just over an hour. At 2:04 a.m. ET RAIN was at $0.00504, up 38.8% in 24 hours. Over the same 24 hours CoinGecko shows bitcoin up 0.7% and ether up 1.7%, so the jump was RAIN's own, not the market's.

The bounce does not undo the bigger picture. RAIN set its record of about $0.0195 on August 25, 2026, and is still about 74% below it. Over 30 days it is down 43.5%.

Where it traded

CoinGecko shows about $20.6 million of RAIN volume in 24 hours. BingX handled about $11.7 million (56.8%), HTX about $3.6 million (17.4%) and BloFin about $3.0 million (14.5%). Toobit, Gate, LBank and WhiteBIT followed with smaller amounts.

That volume is about 0.6% of the token's market value, so a modest amount of buying on one or two venues can move the price sharply in either direction.

What Rain is, and the supply

CoinGecko describes Rain as a prediction and options protocol on Arbitrum where users create and trade their own markets, with part of trading volume used to buy back and burn RAIN. The token contract on Arbitrum One is 0x2511...099d.

CoinGecko counts about 714 billion RAIN in circulation out of a 1.15 trillion maximum, about 62%. At $0.00504 that is a market value of about $3.6 billion (No. 24 on CoinGecko) and a fully diluted value of about $5.8 billion. Rain has not given a reason for the jump in any source we checked, and this story does not suggest one.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a 43% jump in an hour on about $20 million of daily volume, most of it on a single exchange, is a fragile move for a token valued in the billions. Fast overnight spikes on thin books often give back part of the gain, and RAIN had already slipped from its peak by 2 a.m. This is not investment advice.

What's next

  • Whether RAIN holds above $0.0045, the level it broke through at 1:35 a.m. ET
  • Whether volume spreads beyond BingX
  • Any official statement from Rain about the move
