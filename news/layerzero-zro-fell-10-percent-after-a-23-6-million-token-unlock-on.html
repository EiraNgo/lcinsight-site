LayerZero (ZRO) fell 10% after a 23.6 million token unlock, on lower trading volume

DefiLlama's schedule put a 23.63 million ZRO release, 5.9% of the circulating supply, at 11:02 a.m. ET Thursday. ZRO was down 9.9% by early Friday, November 20, on lighter volume than most of last week.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • DefiLlama lists a 23.63 million ZRO unlock on November 19, 2026, at 16:02 UTC (11:02 a.m. ET): 13 million for strategic partners and 10.63 million for core contributors.
  • CoinGecko showed ZRO at $1.28 at 12:47 a.m. ET on November 20, down 9.9% in 24 hours and 36.9% in 30 days. At that price, the unlock is worth about $30 million.
  • Volume was $50.8 million, below most daily readings since November 11. Binance, WhiteBIT and Bybit each carried under 9% of trading.

The unlock

A token unlock is when coins held back for a team or investors become free to move or sell. DefiLlama's ZRO unlock page lists one on November 19, 2026, at 16:02 UTC, or 11:02 a.m. ET: 23.63 million ZRO in total, with 13 million for strategic partners and 10.63 million for core contributors.

DefiLlama puts that at 5.91% of the circulating supply. At Friday's price of $1.28, the tokens are worth about $30 million. Unlocked tokens are not always sold, and the page does not show what the recipients did with them.

We checked the ZRO token page on Etherscan, the Ethereum block explorer. It showed 25,883 holders and 2,163 transfers in the 24 hours to 7:40 p.m. ET Thursday, down 7.45%, but did not list the individual transfers, so we could not trace the unlocked tokens. CoinGecko still counted 399.98 million ZRO in circulation, the figure before the unlock.

The price

ZRO traded between $1.24 and $1.45 in the 24 hours to early Friday, according to CoinGecko. At 12:47 a.m. ET on November 20, it was $1.28, down 9.9% on the day and more than 15% on the week. CoinGecko's midnight UTC prices show $1.4076 at the start of November 19, before the unlock, and $1.3092 a day later, a 7% drop.

The fall came on a weak day for the whole market, with bitcoin down 2.9% at 12:35 a.m. ET. ZRO is 83% below its December 2024 record of $7.47.

No rush to trade

Volume did not jump. The 24-hour figure of $50.8 million was lower than every daily reading from November 14 to 19, which ran from $63.2 million to $94.4 million. CoinGecko's ticker list shows trading spread thinly: Binance's USDT pair carried 8.8%, WhiteBIT 8.5% and Bybit 7.0%.

LayerZero is a messaging protocol that lets apps send data and tokens between blockchains. CoinGecko lists a maximum supply of 1 billion ZRO, so about 40% was circulating before this release.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the timing fits unlock pressure, but the evidence is thin. Volume fell rather than rose, which is not what heavy selling by new recipients usually looks like.
  • Until the unlocked tokens can be traced to exchange deposits, the drop is just as consistent with a broad market slide that hit smaller tokens harder.
  • With about 60% of the supply still outside circulation, future releases will keep testing ZRO's price.

What's next

  • Transfers from the strategic partner and contributor wallets to exchanges.
  • When CoinGecko's circulating supply updates to include the release.
  • Whether ZRO holds above the 24-hour low of $1.24.
