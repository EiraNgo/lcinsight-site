Only 89 of 210 KOL wallets made money in 30 days. Top earner kurtz won 11 of 11

DeepSea's realized PnL table shows most tracked influencer wallets lost money over 30 days, yet the group is up $374,000 as a few big winners carried the total.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Of 210 KOL wallets with closed trades in the past 30 days, 89 made money, DeepSea's PnL table showed at 12:38 a.m. ET on November 12, 2026. Together they were up $374,130.
  • They closed 13,725 trades and won 7,462, about 54%. The top wallet, kurtz on BNB Chain (ending in 947c), made $48,397 and won all 11 of its closed trades.
  • DeepSea's said vs did feed, which compares KOL posts with wallet moves, listed no cases in its latest build.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's realized PnL table (built 12:38 a.m. ET) tracks 297 KOL wallets: 187 on Solana, 67 on BNB Chain, 22 on Ethereum, 20 on Base and 1 on Monad. KOLs are crypto influencers. Of these, 210 closed trades in the last 30 days. Only 89 ended in profit, but the group still made $374,130 in all.

The said vs did feed, last built at 11:33 a.m. ET on November 11, listed no cases above its $300 floor, so there is no post-versus-trade gap to report this time.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 12, 2026, 12:42 a.m. ET.

The leaders

New to the tools? Read what DeepSea is.

The top wallet is on BNB Chain and ends in 947c. DeepSea labels it as the KOL kurtz. It made $48,397 in 30 days, 11 wins in 11 closed trades, $26,562 of it in the last week. Two Solana wallets follow. One ending in BGM9 made $40,248 and won 17 of 21. One ending in kbw8 made $38,694 from 774 trades, winning 756, about 98%.

The losers

The biggest loss in the table is minus $56,121, from a BNB Chain wallet ending in f5b3 on just 2 trades. DeepSea rates its data coverage at only 4%, the lowest of the group, so we treat that number with caution. Among wallets with high coverage, a Solana wallet ending in 2AtX lost the most: minus $16,378, winning 27 of 71.

The last 7 days

The 7-day column had results for only 6 KOL wallets, none on Solana. Five on BNB Chain all made money, $70,716 together, and won 22 of 24 trades. One on Base lost $30.

Market and what it means

CoinGecko showed SOL at $82.87 at 12:44 a.m. ET, down 22.7% in 30 days, and BNB at $673.79, down 8.9%. Realized PnL counts closed trades only, not coins still held.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the split is the headline: fewer than half of KOL wallets made money in a falling month, and a handful of winners lifted the total.
  • A 98% win rate over 774 trades is unusual. Many small, quick trades can produce it, so size matters as much as the rate.
  • The 7-day picture rests on very few wallets: five on BNB Chain won 22 of 24 trades, and no Solana KOL wallet showed a result.

What's next

  • Whether the 947c wallet keeps its perfect record
  • Whether more Solana KOL wallets show 7-day results
  • New cases in DeepSea's said vs did feed
