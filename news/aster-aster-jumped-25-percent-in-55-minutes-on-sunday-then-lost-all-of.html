Aster (ASTER) jumped 25% in 55 minutes on Sunday, then lost all of it in 15

ASTER rose from about $0.551 to $0.691 between 3:15 and 4:10 p.m. ET on November 15, then fell to $0.503 by 4:25 p.m. ET. It is now 79% below its record.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko's five-minute data shows ASTER at about $0.551 at 3:15 p.m. ET on November 15, 2026, and $0.691 at 4:10 p.m. ET, a rise of about 25% in 55 minutes.
  • By 4:25 p.m. ET it was at about $0.503, down 27% from the peak in 15 minutes and below where the jump started.
  • At 7:04 p.m. ET ASTER was at $0.511, up 2.1% in 24 hours but down 19.6% in seven days and 79% below its $2.41 record.

The spike and the drop

Aster's token ASTER spent most of Sunday afternoon near $0.55, according to CoinGecko. At 3:15 p.m. ET on November 15, 2026 it was at about $0.551. Then it climbed in steady steps: $0.575 at 3:30 p.m., $0.610 at 3:55 p.m., $0.624 at 4:00 p.m. and about $0.691 at 4:10 p.m. ET. That is a gain of about 25% in 55 minutes.

The fall was faster. Five minutes after the peak ASTER was at $0.627. At 4:20 p.m. ET it was at $0.518, and at 4:25 p.m. ET about $0.503, 27% below the top and below the $0.551 where the run began. It then settled between $0.51 and $0.53 for the rest of the evening.

The wider picture

At 7:04 p.m. ET ASTER was at $0.511, up 2.1% over 24 hours, down 19.6% over seven days and down 27.8% over 30 days. Its record is $2.41, set at 11:18 p.m. ET on September 23, 2025; the token is now about 79% below it. In the same 24 hours CoinGecko shows bitcoin up 1.9% and ether up 3.7%, so the afternoon spike did not track the market.

The token is ranked No. 59 by market value on CoinGecko, at about $1.31 billion, with about 2.57 billion of a maximum 8 billion tokens circulating. The fully diluted value is about $3.98 billion.

Where it traded

CoinGecko counts about $328 million of ASTER trading in the past 24 hours. Binance handled the largest share, about 14.7%, followed by KuCoin (9%), WhiteBIT, Websea and Bybit (about 5% each) and a PancakeSwap pool on BNB Chain (4.6%). ASTER is also on CoinGecko's trending list.

We found no official statement from Aster that explains the move, and this story does not suggest a cause. The price data does not show who bought or sold.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a 25% climb that is fully erased within 15 minutes points to short-lived buying or a squeeze that ran out, not a lasting change in demand. Large one-way moves like this often trigger liquidations on leveraged bets in both directions. This is not investment advice.
  • The token has now had two sharp intraday swings in two days, after a 21% drop in 80 minutes on November 14.

What's next

  • Whether ASTER holds above the $0.48 low it touched on November 14
  • Any official post from Aster about Sunday's move
  • Funding and open interest on ASTER perpetual futures
