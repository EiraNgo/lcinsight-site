Bitcoin sat flat at $64,749 on Sunday after a 13% weekly drop, and Zcash lost 28%

Bitcoin moved just 0.1% in 24 hours as the crash paused. The seven-day damage stayed deep across the top coins, with Zcash, Uniswap and Pepe down a quarter or more. Quant led the small Sunday bounce.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko showed bitcoin at $64,749 at 12:36 a.m. ET on November 16, 2026, down 0.1% in 24 hours and 13.4% in seven days. Its 24-hour range was $63,777 to $65,980.
  • Zcash fell 27.8% in seven days, the worst among the 20 largest coins. Uniswap lost 25.4%, Pepe 25.0% and Ethena 22.6%.
  • Quant rose 6.6% in 24 hours, the best of the top 60 by market value. Ether gained 0.7% to $1,818 but is down 15.0% for the week.

A quiet day after a hard week

Bitcoin went flat on Sunday after Saturday's slide. CoinGecko's market table at 12:36 a.m. ET on November 16 put bitcoin at $64,749, almost unchanged from a day earlier. Its lowest price in those 24 hours was $63,777 and its highest $65,980, a range of about 3%.

The week looks very different. CoinGecko's daily prices show bitcoin at $75,776 at 7 p.m. ET on November 7. Since then it has lost about $11,000, and CoinGecko's seven-day change reads minus 13.4%. Its daily price is the lowest since mid-August.

Who fell most this week

Among the 20 largest coins, Zcash took the deepest cut, down 27.8% in seven days to $888. Privacy coin Monero lost 18.8% and Solana 18.1%. Ether fell 15.0% to $1,818, BNB 14.1% to $590 and XRP 13.7% to $0.957.

Further down the top 60, decentralized exchange and meme tokens were among the worst: Uniswap fell 25.4%, Pepe 25.0% and Ethena 22.6%. Quant lost 21.2% for the week even after Sunday's bounce. Tron (down 4.7%) and Stellar (down 5.0%) held up far better than most large coins.

Sunday's small bounce

A few tokens rose over the 24 hours. Quant gained 6.6% to $156, the best in the top 60 by market value. Aster added 4.8% to $0.547 after a wild weekend, Canton Coin 4.2%, Aave 3.7% and Uniswap 2.8%. Cardano rose 2.4%.

Others kept sliding. Pepe fell another 3.6% and Crypto.com's CRO 3.0%. Zcash dropped 1.6%. Tokens backed by gold, Tether Gold and PAX Gold, were down about 6% for the week, so even the safe-haven tokens did not hold up.

How to read it

A flat day after a sharp drop can mean sellers are tired, or only that weekend trading is thin. Weekday trading, when U.S. funds and markets open, often shows which one it was.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think Sunday's calm looks like a pause. One flat day does not undo a 13% week or prove a bottom.
  • The likely next test is Monday, when U.S. markets and spot bitcoin funds trade again.
  • The weekly table says more than Sunday's bounce. Zcash, Uniswap and Pepe fell a quarter or more, and even gold-backed tokens lost about 6%, so few corners of crypto escaped.

What's next

  • Whether bitcoin holds above Sunday's $63,777 low
  • Monday's U.S. spot bitcoin fund flows
  • Whether Zcash's slide slows
