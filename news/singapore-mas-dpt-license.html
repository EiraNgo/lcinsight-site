Singapore MAS DPT license: PSA rules, capital, fees and process

Crypto firms serving customers in Singapore need a Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) license for digital payment token (DPT) services under the Payment Services Act. They apply as a standard or major payment institution, with base capital of S$100,000 or S$250,000.

LC Insight Editorial Team7 min read

Key points

  • In Singapore, buying, selling, exchanging, transferring or holding crypto for customers is a digital payment token service under the Payment Services Act 2019.
  • Firms apply as a standard payment institution (under S$3 million a month in transactions for any one service) or a major payment institution (no volume cap).
  • Base capital is at least S$100,000 for a standard and S$250,000 for a major institution, which also posts a S$100,000 or S$200,000 security.
  • Since June 30, 2025, firms that serve only customers outside Singapore fall under a separate regime, and MAS says it will generally not license them.

What is a DPT license in Singapore?

Singapore regulates crypto services mainly through the Payment Services Act 2019 (PS Act). Under it, a "digital payment token service" is one of the payment services that needs a license from the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS). A digital payment token, or DPT, is MAS's term for cryptocurrencies such as bitcoin and ether.

MAS's licensing guidelines say the DPT service covers buying or selling DPTs, running a platform where people exchange them, transferring DPTs, providing custodian wallets, and arranging DPT trades even without holding customer money or tokens.

The rule is about the service and the customer, not the token. That is why MAS asks new applicants for a legal opinion that checks each proposed product against the law. A firm that does any of these for customers in Singapore needs a license. It is not one standalone "DPT license": the firm gets a payment institution license with DPT service added. This guide explains public rules and is not legal advice. For the wider picture, including stablecoins, see our guide to Singapore's crypto rules.

What is the difference between a standard and a major payment institution?

The PS Act has three license types, set out on MAS's licensing page:

  • Money-changing license: for firms that only change foreign currency notes. Not for crypto.
  • Standard payment institution (SPI): for firms that stay under set limits: S$3 million in monthly transactions for any one payment service, S$6 million a month across two or more services, and S$5 million in daily outstanding e-money.
  • Major payment institution (MPI): for firms that go above those limits. An MPI has no cap on volume or float.

A smaller crypto firm can start as an SPI, but MAS says it must apply for an MPI license once it exceeds the SPI thresholds. The thresholds count transactions per service, so a firm that offers DPT service alongside cross-border money transfer is measured against the S$6 million combined limit as well as the S$3 million limit for each service. Most large exchanges serving Singapore customers therefore need MPI status.

What is the FSMA DTSP regime from June 30, 2025?

A second law covers a different group. Under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2022 (FSMA), firms that provide digital token services only to customers outside Singapore are digital token service providers (DTSPs). MAS said in June 2025 that the license requirement for them took effect on June 30, 2025.

MAS also said it will generally not issue a license to these firms, citing higher money laundering risk and the difficulty of supervising activity that happens abroad. Existing firms of this kind had to stop the activity when the regime took effect.

Firms serving customers in Singapore stay under the PS Act, the Securities and Futures Act or the Financial Advisers Act, as before, and may also serve overseas customers. Firms dealing only in tokens such as utility or governance tokens are outside the DTSP licensing.

How much capital does a DPT license require?

MAS's licensing page sets base capital and, for major institutions, a security deposit:

  • SPI: base capital of at least S$100,000, plus a buffer based on the firm's operations.
  • MPI: base capital of at least S$250,000, plus a buffer.
  • MPI security: S$100,000 if monthly transactions average no more than S$6 million for every service over a calendar year, otherwise S$200,000. It must be a cash deposit with MAS or a bank guarantee, in place before the firm starts business.

Capital is the floor, not the budget. MAS also expects people, systems and audits, described below.

What are the MAS license fees?

MAS's how to apply page sets a non-refundable application fee. For the DPT service it is S$1,000 for an SPI and S$1,500 for an MPI. The overall fee is S$1,000 for an SPI or S$1,500 for an MPI, or the sum of the fees for each service applied for, whichever is higher.

Licensees also pay an annual license fee set in the Payment Services Regulations. MAS's guidelines give worked examples, such as S$10,000 a year in later years for a firm that moves from an SPI to an MPI license with account issuance and e-money issuance, and say all license fees are non-refundable.

How do you apply for a DPT license?

MAS's application steps are: confirm the service is regulated, choose the license type, check the law and guidelines, then apply online through the MAS e-licensing portal and pay the fee. The file includes an ACRA business profile, three years of audited accounts, organization and shareholding charts, a business plan, a legal opinion from a law firm experienced in the PS Act, and anti-money laundering (AML) policies and a risk assessment.

DPT applicants face extra steps. They must file a report from an independent external auditor on their AML and consumer protection controls, signed off within three months before submission. After in-principle approval, they must have an external auditor assess technology and cybersecurity risks. MAS's guidelines also say the firm needs a permanent place of business in Singapore, executive directors resident there, and an in-house local compliance officer for DPT services.

How long does a MAS license take?

MAS does not publish a standard processing time on its application page or in its guidelines. The one fixed period is for applications MAS puts on hold, for example after a major restructuring or a change of key managers: the default hold is six months and cannot be extended. Timing in practice depends on the quality of the file and the rounds of questions that follow.

Applicants can plan around the steps MAS does describe. The external auditor report on AML and consumer protection must be fresh, signed off within three months before the file goes in, so it is usually one of the last pieces prepared. The technology and cybersecurity assessment comes later, after in-principle approval. An MPI must also have its security deposit or bank guarantee in place before it starts business.

Where can you find licensed DPT firms?

When MAS approves an application, it emails the e-license and adds the firm to its Financial Institutions Directory (FID), according to its how to apply page. The FID can be filtered by license type, such as major or standard payment institution, and shows which payment services each firm may provide. Check the exact legal entity and whether DPT service is listed, because a brand can operate several entities in different countries. LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the real barrier is not the S$250,000 capital but the controls: independent audits of AML and technology, a local compliance officer and resident directors.
  • The June 2025 DTSP rule closed the option of a Singapore shell serving only foreign users. The likely result is fewer, larger licensees with real local operations.
  • Users should check the directory entry, not the brand. The license belongs to one entity and covers only the services listed.

Frequently asked questions

Is a DPT license the same as an MPI license?

Not exactly. DPT service is one payment service a firm can be licensed for, either as a standard or a major payment institution. Large crypto firms above the SPI limits need an MPI license.

Can a Singapore crypto firm serve only overseas customers?

Since June 30, 2025, that falls under the FSMA DTSP regime, and MAS says it will generally not issue licenses for it.

What is the MAS application fee for DPT service?

S$1,000 for a standard payment institution and S$1,500 for a major payment institution, per MAS's how to apply page. The fee is non-refundable.

Do I need a local director for a MAS license?

MAS's guidelines expect executive directors to be resident in Singapore, and for a Singapore-incorporated applicant at least one executive director must be a citizen or permanent resident, or an Employment Pass holder if another director is.

How do I check if a crypto firm is licensed in Singapore?

Search the MAS Financial Institutions Directory and confirm the firm's license type and that digital payment token service is listed.
