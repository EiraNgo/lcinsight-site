Gold token PAXG is 32% off its January record, yet holders added 16,200 ounces since August

PAX Gold fell 5.7% in a week to $3,826, CoinGecko data show. Its supply, one token per ounce, rose to 447,522 ounces, up 16,209 since its August 29 low.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • PAX Gold (PAXG) traded at $3,825.55 at 6:41 a.m. ET on November 15, 2026, down 5.7% in seven days and 31.9% below its all-time high of $5,619.09 on January 28, per CoinGecko.
  • Its supply rose to 447,522 tokens from 431,313 in the August 29 reading, a gain of 16,209, or 3.8%. Each token stands for one fine troy ounce of gold.
  • This week the supply barely moved (down about 205 tokens), so nearly all of the $100 million drop in PAXG's market value came from the gold price.

What moved

PAXG is a token in which one unit should represent one fine troy ounce of a London Good Delivery gold bar held by Paxos Trust Company, according to its CoinGecko listing. Its price tracks gold, and its supply counts the ounces that token holders own.

At 6:41 a.m. ET on November 15, 2026, CoinGecko put PAXG at $3,825.55, down 5.7% in seven days. Its all-time high was $5,619.09 on January 28, so it is 31.9% below that. Its highest daily reading was $5,495.62 on January 29. Bitcoin fell 13.6% in the same seven days.

Price down, ounces up

Supply has moved the other way from the price. CoinGecko's daily data put PAXG at 431,313 tokens on August 29, the lowest since July. Sunday's figure is 447,522, which matches CoinGecko's own circulating and total supply. That is 16,209 more ounces, or 3.8%, while the price fell from $4,462 to $3,826.

This week was quiet for supply. It slipped about 205 tokens since the November 9 reading. PAXG's market value fell $100 million to $1.712 billion in that time, so almost all of the drop was the gold price, not holders leaving.

The supply is still 13% below its March 28 reading of 514,129 tokens. The data show totals only, not who bought or redeemed, and we found no statement from Paxos on the change.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the rise in supply since August shows some buyers kept adding tokenized gold as its price fell, though a 3.8% gain is modest.
  • Gold fell less than bitcoin this week, but it did fall: holders who used PAXG as a crash hedge still lost money in dollar terms.
  • A market value drop is not the same as an exit. For PAXG this week, it was almost all price.

What's next

  • Whether PAXG supply holds above 447,000 tokens.
  • The PAXG price against its all-time high of $5,619.
  • Paxos's monthly PAXG reserve reports.
