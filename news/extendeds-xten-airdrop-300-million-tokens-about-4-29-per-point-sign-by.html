Extended's XTEN airdrop: 300 million tokens, about 4.29 per point, sign by December 31

Perpetuals exchange Extended has set aside 30% of its XTEN token for points holders and opened a signing step in its app. U.S., UK and Canadian users are excluded, and half the supply goes to founders, team and investors.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Extended's docs set 300 million XTEN, 30% of a fixed 1 billion supply, for points holders, fully unlocked at launch. With 70 million points given out, that works out to about 4.29 XTEN per point.
  • Users must accept the XTEN Community Distribution Terms in the Extended app by 11:59 p.m. UTC on December 31, 2026 (6:59 p.m. ET). Unaccepted tokens go to the Ecosystem Fund.
  • No trading date is set. At 6:45 a.m. ET on November 18, CoinGecko listed XTEN with no price and zero circulating supply. Extended's own exchange carried open bets worth about $48.9 million.

The airdrop, and what Extended is

Extended has set aside 300 million XTEN, 30% of its 1 billion supply, for holders of its 70 million points, or about 4.29 XTEN per point by our math. Holders must accept the terms in the Extended app by 11:59 p.m. UTC on December 31, 2026 (6:59 p.m. ET). New to airdrops? Read how they work and how to spot the scams.

Extended is a decentralized venue for perpetual futures: leveraged price contracts that never expire. It began on Starknet and now links accounts to EVM wallets. At 6:45 a.m. ET on November 18, CoinGecko listed 347 perpetual markets on Extended, with 24-hour volume of 4,334 BTC (about $278 million) and open interest, the value of open bets, of 762.87 BTC (about $48.9 million).

Who qualifies and how to claim

Extended's points page says the final snapshot was taken at 00:00 UTC on November 6, 2026 (7 p.m. ET on November 5), and that all 70 million points have been distributed. Its airdrop page says users with a positive points balance at that snapshot can claim, if they are 18 or older, not sanctioned, not in an excluded country and have an account linked to a self-custodial EVM wallet.

To claim, users log in to the Extended app, accept the XTEN Community Distribution Terms and sign with their wallet. They then choose once, with no changes later, which Extended account gets the tokens. Starknet accounts must name a self-custodial EVM wallet with an active Extended account. The deadline is 11:59 p.m. UTC on December 31, 2026, which is 6:59 p.m. ET.

The terms exclude users in the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom, among others. EU users can claim but cannot trade XTEN on Extended at launch, the airdrop page says.

Supply and unlocks

The tokenomics page splits 1 billion XTEN like this. Of the community share, 17,384,355 XTEN (5.8%) come from points earned by the in-house market-making desk of X10 Ltd. Founders and team get a 12-month lockup, then monthly unlocks through month 48. Investors get 25% at month 12 and the rest monthly through month 48. About 32.9% of supply is unlocked at launch.

Where to claim, and the risks

  • Claiming happens only in the Extended app. The contracts page still lists the XTEN token, on Circle's Arc chain, as "coming soon" and says there is no sale or presale.
  • Extended says it will never ask for a seed phrase and no payment is needed. Fake claim sites copy every airdrop; do not connect a wallet from a link in a direct message or reply.
  • Half the supply belongs to insiders. Their first unlocks come 12 months after launch.
  • No price exists yet. CoinGecko showed XTEN with zero circulating supply.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the 4.29-per-point rate is the number most holders will use, but it is our division of two official figures, not a rate Extended's docs state.
  • With up to 300 million XTEN free at launch and the insider half locked for a year, early trading will be driven almost entirely by airdrop recipients.
  • Excluding U.S., UK and Canadian users cuts out a large group of traders, which may shape where and how XTEN first trades.

What's next

  • An official XTEN launch date and contract address on Extended's contracts page
  • XTEN's first price and circulating supply on CoinGecko
  • How much of the 300 million goes unclaimed by December 31
