Longs paid on 84% of HYPE futures bets on Friday. Now shorts pay on 61%

HYPE slipped 7.7% in a week to $66.91, and $280 million of its futures bets closed. On Hyperliquid itself traders still pay to bet on a rise, while Binance, Bybit and OKX now show negative funding.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • HYPE traded at $66.91 at 6:36 a.m. ET on November 16, 2026, down 1.6% in 24 hours and 7.7% in seven days, per CoinGecko.
  • 97 HYPE perpetual markets held $2.32 billion in open bets, down from $2.60 billion on November 13.
  • Funding was negative on 41 markets holding 61% of the money. On November 13, positive funding covered 84%.
  • Hyperliquid's own HYPE market fell to $648.4 million in open bets from $797.6 million, yet its funding stayed positive.

What moved

Hyperliquid runs its own blockchain for leveraged futures with no end date, and HYPE is its token (our Hyperliquid guide). At 6:36 a.m. ET on Monday, November 16, 2026, CoinGecko showed it at $66.91, down 1.6% in 24 hours and 7.7% in seven days. Its market value was $15.91 billion, No. 10.

CoinGecko's hourly prices show a seven-day low of $62.43 at 5 p.m. ET on Saturday, November 14, the evening bitcoin fell to $62,612. On Friday, November 13, we reported HYPE at $73.66, the best performer in the top 50 that day.

Where the money left

Open interest counts the dollars in futures bets nobody has closed yet. At 6:45 a.m. ET, CoinGecko's derivatives list showed 97 HYPE perpetual markets holding $2.32 billion, down from $2.60 billion on Friday. They traded $3.94 billion in 24 hours, nearly five times HYPE's $812 million of spot trading.

Most of the drop came from Hyperliquid's own market, the largest, which fell to $648.4 million from $797.6 million, down 19%. Binance's slipped to $365.2 million from $385.3 million, and Gate's to $187.5 million from $213.6 million.

Who pays whom now

Funding is a periodic payment that ties a perpetual's price to the spot market (our explainer). A negative rate means sellers betting on a drop, called shorts, pay buyers holding longs.

On Monday, funding was negative on 41 HYPE markets, positive on 50 and zero on 6. The negative group held 61% of all open bets. On Friday the positive group held 84%. Binance, Gate, CoinW, Bybit and OKX all showed negative funding.

Hyperliquid was the exception. Its HYPE market still showed positive funding of 0.0004% per hour, so longs there kept paying. On Hyperliquid's own board, HYPE's open bets stayed second only to bitcoin's $1.17 billion and above ether's $551.5 million. Hyperliquid settles every trade on its own chain, so these figures come from a public order book. They show the size of the bets, not who holds them.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the split matters: traders on Hyperliquid, the token's home exchange, still lean long, while larger centralized venues lean short. The two groups are betting against each other.
  • Most of the $280 million that closed came off Hyperliquid's own HYPE market. That points to holders there cutting size rather than switching sides.
  • Negative funding across most venues is a cost for shorts. If HYPE steadies, that cost can push some of them to close, which would add buying.

What's next

  • HYPE against its seven-day low of $62.43
  • Whether funding on Hyperliquid's HYPE market turns negative
  • Total HYPE open bets against $2.32 billion
