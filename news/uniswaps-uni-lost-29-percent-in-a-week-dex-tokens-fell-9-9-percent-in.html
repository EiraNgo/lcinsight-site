Uniswap's UNI lost 29% in a week. DEX tokens fell 9.9% in a day, three times bitcoin's drop

Tokens of automated market makers, the pool-based exchanges behind most DeFi trading, lost 9.9% of their value in 24 hours to early Sunday. Bitcoin fell 3%. CoinGecko data show UNI, Curve and Aerodrome leading the slide.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko's automated market maker (AMM) token group fell 9.9% in 24 hours to $6.55 billion at 12:43 a.m. ET on November 15, 2026, the worst of all groups worth more than $5 billion.
  • Uniswap's UNI fell 9.6% in a day and 29.1% in a week to $5.04. It is 47% of the group's value.
  • Bitcoin fell 3.0% and ether 4.6% in the same 24 hours. The wider DeFi token group fell 6.1% to $57.75 billion.

What moved

Tokens of pool-based crypto exchanges fell 9.9% in 24 hours to $6.55 billion at 12:43 a.m. ET on Sunday, November 15, more than three times bitcoin's 3.0% drop, CoinGecko data show. Uniswap's UNI led the slide, down 9.6% in a day and 29.1% in a week to $5.04.

An automated market maker, or AMM, is a DEX that trades from pools of tokens instead of an order book. Uniswap, Curve and PancakeSwap are the best known. Their tokens usually carry voting rights and, at some, a claim on fees.

CoinGecko's category data put these tokens in one AMM group. Among CoinGecko groups worth more than $5 billion, none fell further. Bitcoin fell 3.0% to $64,813 over the same day and ether 4.6% to $1,807.

Which tokens fell most

CoinGecko's list of the largest AMM tokens shows a broad slide. Uniswap's UNI fell 9.6% in a day to $5.04, and 29.1% over seven days. At $3.06 billion, UNI is 47% of the whole group, so its drop alone explains much of the fall. Its 24-hour trading of $678 million equals 22% of its market value.

Curve's CRV fell 9.3% to $0.21, Pendle 7.2%, Aerodrome's AERO 6.1% and Raydium's RAY 4.6%. PancakeSwap's CAKE held up best of the large ones, down 2.9%. Pump.fun's PUMP, which CoinGecko also lists in the group, fell 12.9%.

The rest of DeFi

The wider DeFi token group fell 6.1% to $57.75 billion. Ethena's ENA fell 8.6%, Morpho 8.3% and Sky 7.5%. Aave held up better, down 3.3% in a day to $105.66, though it is down 20.1% in a week. Aster, the token of an onchain futures exchange, fell 17.7%, the steepest among the 20 largest DeFi tokens.

Lending tokens as a group fell 5.8% and perpetual exchange tokens 5.9%. CoinGecko prices show what tokens are worth, not how much money left the protocols themselves, which this data does not measure.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think DEX tokens fall harder than bitcoin in a sell-off because they are smaller, trade on thinner markets and carry more risk in the eyes of traders.
  • UNI's weekly loss is far larger than bitcoin's 14%, so traders appear to be cutting riskier DeFi holdings first.
  • A day of trading worth a fifth of UNI's market value shows heavy turnover, which can work in both directions once selling slows.

What's next

  • Whether UNI holds above $5 after a 29% weekly fall
  • The AMM group against $6.55 billion
  • Aave's weekly loss against the wider DeFi group
