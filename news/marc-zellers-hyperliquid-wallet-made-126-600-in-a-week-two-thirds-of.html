Marc Zeller's Hyperliquid wallet made $126,600 in a week, two thirds of its 30-day profit

DeepSea ranks the wallet No. 6 on its KOL board. It moved $1.70 million of volume, holds little else, and shows no flags in DeepSea's risk database.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • DeepSea lists +$126,600 over 7 days and +$186,600 over 30 days for the Hyperliquid wallet it ties to this KOL.
  • About 68% of the 30-day profit came in the last 7 days.
  • DeepSea sees no transfers by this KOL in tracked tokens over 7 days, and its risk check shows no flags.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's KOL board follows the wallets of crypto influencers (KOLs). No. 6 by 7-day profit is the account DeepSea labels Marc "Zeller", with the X handle it lists as @lemiscate. DeepSea ties one wallet to this name, on Hyperliquid.

The profile: profit +$126,600 over 7 days and +$186,600 over 30 days, volume $1.70 million. Holdings outside the trading account are small, about $6,500, mostly 2.139 ETH ($3,900), with a few dollar-pegged and staked tokens. DeepSea logged no transfers by this KOL in tracked tokens over the last 7 days. Its risk check found none of the KOL's wallets in its scam database.

DeepSea has no "said versus did" case for this KOL, so we cannot compare posts with the wallet.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 17, 2026, 6:46 a.m. ET.

Checking the numbers

CoinGecko showed ether at $1,814.61 at 6:40 a.m. ET. At that price, 2.139 ETH is $3,881, which matches DeepSea's $3.9K. The 7-day profit is 67.8% of the 30-day profit, so most of it is recent. We could not open an independent explorer or Hyperliquid page for the wallet in this run, so the profit figures rest on DeepSea alone. Data from DeepSea and CoinGecko.

New to this?

KOL stands for key opinion leader, a crypto influencer with a public following. DeepSea ties public accounts to wallets it can identify and tracks their profit and loss. That helps readers compare what a KOL says with what the wallet does. In this case DeepSea shows profit and volume but no list of open positions, so the story is about size and timing: $1.70 million traded, $186,600 earned in 30 days, and $126,600 of that in the last week. A wallet that earns most of its profit in a few days can look strong, yet one bad week can erase it. We name only what DeepSea labels, and the owner's motives are unknown.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the profit is small next to the $1.70 million traded, and one good week drives most of it.
  • DeepSea's board shows who earned, not why. Followers should not read a ranking as a signal to copy.
