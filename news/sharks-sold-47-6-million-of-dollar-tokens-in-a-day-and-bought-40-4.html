Sharks sold $47.6 million of dollar tokens in a day and bought $40.4 million

DeepSea's 24-hour shark lists show 18 stablecoin and dollar-yield token rows in 492 trades. That is a net $7.28 million out, led by Aave USDC on Arbitrum and AUSD on Monad.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • In DeepSea's 24-hour feed built at 6:35 a.m. ET on November 15, 2026, shark wallets sold $47.64 million of dollar tokens and bought $40.36 million, in 492 trades.
  • Aave's USDC deposit token on Arbitrum led the selling at a net $3.8 million out, then AUSD on Monad at $2.97 million.
  • The biggest net buys were mUSD on Monad (+$1.90 million) and USD₮0 on Unichain (+$1.29 million), each from a single wallet.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea tracks sharks, wallets that hold or trade large amounts. Its 24-hour flow feed, built at 6:35 a.m. ET on Sunday, lists 18 rows for stablecoins and dollar-based yield tokens across seven chains. Together they show $40.36 million bought and $47.64 million sold, a net $7.28 million out, in 492 trades.

These were the largest net moves.

  • Aave Arbitrum USDCn (Arbitrum): $26.2 million bought, $30 million sold, net minus $3.8 million, 3 wallets
  • AUSD (Monad): $1.61 million bought, $4.57 million sold, net minus $2.97 million, 1 wallet
  • sUSDC (Arbitrum): $2 million sold in a single trade, nothing bought
  • mUSD (Monad): $6.12 million bought, $4.21 million sold, net plus $1.90 million, 1 wallet in 257 trades
  • USD₮0 (Unichain): $5.20 million bought, $3.91 million sold, net plus $1.29 million, 1 wallet in 26 trades

Leave out the Aave rows and sharks still sold more than they bought: $17.64 million against $14.16 million.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 15, 2026, 6:35 a.m. ET.

Two familiar wallets

New to the tools? Read what DeepSea is.

Every Monad dollar token on the list traces to one address, Holdeaee, which we wrote about on November 14. DeepSea shows it with $7.93 million bought and $10.30 million sold in 317 trades in a day. Its realized result over 7 days is minus $2.66 million on 313 closed trades, 141 of them winners. The Unichain buyer is Hold8b87, also covered on November 14; it was still on DeepSea's one-hour buying list at 6:35 a.m. ET.

DeepSea's wallet totals suggest most of the Aave rows came from the two Hyperliquid traders who lead its smart money board this week, which we cover in a separate story.

The bigger picture

To test the feed, we matched DeepSea's token rows with its wallet rows. Hold8b87's Unichain totals, $5.20 million in and $3.91 million out, equal the USD₮0 row to the dollar. Holdeaee is the only Monad wallet listed, and Monad's token rows sum to within about $35,000 of its totals. DefiLlama and block explorers were out of reach for us during this run, so there is no comparison here with total stablecoin supply.

All this happened in a rough week for prices. CoinGecko put bitcoin at $65,430 at 6:34 a.m. ET Sunday, 13.6% lower than seven days before.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the pattern is shuffling, not fleeing: sharks moved about $88 million of dollar tokens in a day, but the net outflow was under a tenth of that.
  • Two wallets explain much of the Monad and Unichain activity, so these totals say more about a few traders than about sharks as a group.
  • DeepSea's feed does not show what sellers received, so a dollar token sold may simply have become another dollar token.

What's next

  • Whether net shark selling of dollar tokens grows if prices keep falling
  • Holdeaee's 7-day loss, now $2.66 million
  • Further Aave USDC withdrawals on Arbitrum
