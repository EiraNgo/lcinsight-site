Meme coin Macca the Gator is up 272% in a day, and all of its trading runs through one Uniswap pool

MACCA, an Ethereum meme token whose CoinGecko price history starts at 2 p.m. ET on November 14, rose from about $0.0046 to a record $0.0190 on Sunday. It is on CoinGecko's trending list.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows MACCA at about $0.0172 at 7:04 p.m. ET on November 15, 2026, up 272% in 24 hours. It is No. 5 on CoinGecko's trending list.
  • It set a record $0.0190 at 4:35 p.m. ET on November 15, about 6.7 times its low of $0.0028 from 2:20 p.m. ET on November 14.
  • The only market CoinGecko tracks is one Uniswap v4 pool on Ethereum, with about $3 million of trading in 24 hours.

What moved

Macca the Gator, ticker MACCA, is a meme token on Ethereum. CoinGecko's price history for it starts at 2 p.m. ET on Friday, November 14, 2026. Its lowest price on CoinGecko is about $0.0028, at 2:20 p.m. ET that day. At 7:05 p.m. ET that evening it was near $0.0046.

The big move came on Sunday morning. CoinGecko's hourly data shows about $0.0059 at 7:05 a.m. ET, $0.0102 at 9:05 a.m. and $0.0155 at 11:05 a.m. ET, more than 2.6 times higher in four hours. After a dip it climbed again to a record $0.0190 at 4:35 p.m. ET.

At 7:04 p.m. ET on November 15 MACCA stood at about $0.0172, up 272% in 24 hours and about 10% below its fresh record. Bitcoin gained 1.9% and ether 3.7% in that window, per CoinGecko.

Where it traded

MACCA has a single market on CoinGecko's list: a Uniswap v4 pool on Ethereum that pairs it with ETH. Roughly $3 million changed hands there over Sunday's 24 hours, about a sixth of the token's market value. No centralized exchange lists it yet, according to CoinGecko.

That setup is fragile. When one pool holds all the liquidity, a handful of wallets can push the price up sharply, and the same handful can pull it back down.

Supply and value

There are 1 billion MACCA, a fixed number, and CoinGecko treats every one as circulating, so market value and fully diluted value are the same: about $17 million at Sunday evening's price. That ranks it No. 850 on CoinGecko.

The project's CoinGecko page tells a story about a pink gator in a palm-print shirt with a martini and says "no schemes, no stress". There is no named team, audit or roadmap. Its links are maccagatormeme.vip and @MaccaGatorMeme on X. Contract on Ethereum: 0x37f5da...b74e4d72a9.

What we could not check

Holder data was not part of what we verified, so this story says nothing about how the supply is split or which wallets bought on Sunday. No official post we found gives a reason for the rally. If the team explains it, we will update.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We read a 3.7x day in one pool, on a day bitcoin added under 2%, as demand focused on MACCA itself rather than a rising tide. CoinGecko's figures cannot tell us whose wallets were buying. This is not investment advice.
  • This is the third new Ethereum meme token on a single Uniswap pool to reach CoinGecko's trending list this weekend. A token with barely a day and a half of price history can reverse as fast as it rose.

What's next

  • Does MACCA stay above $0.0155, its 11:05 a.m. ET level on Sunday?
  • A second pool or a first exchange listing on CoinGecko
  • Posts from @MaccaGatorMeme on X explaining the run
