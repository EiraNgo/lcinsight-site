Anthropic tokens hit $116 on day one, then fell 22%. Hyperliquid ANT bets traded $1.71 billion

On-chain versions of Anthropic shares peaked near $116 on Thursday and traded near $90 by Friday morning. Fewer than $4 million of the tokens exist, while bets on Hyperliquid's ANT market traded $1.71 billion in a day.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Robinhood's Anthropic token peaked at $115.77 at 12:05 p.m. ET Thursday and Ondo's at $115.96 at 12:27 p.m. ET, CoinGecko shows. By 6:45 a.m. ET Friday both traded near $90, about 22% lower.
  • At about 7 a.m. ET Friday, CoinGecko counted about 26,300 Robinhood tokens ($2.37 million), 8,850 Coinbase tokens ($0.81 million) and 8,130 Ondo tokens ($0.73 million).
  • Hyperliquid's xyz:ANT perpetual traded $1.71 billion in 24 hours, second only to bitcoin among its 351 markets, with $129.5 million in open bets.

What moved

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share on November 18, with Nasdaq trading under ANT set for November 19. Tokens that track the shares started trading on-chain on November 19, CoinGecko's price records show.

CoinGecko's record shows the Robinhood token, issued on Robinhood's own chain, hit $115.77 at 12:05 p.m. ET Thursday. Ondo's token peaked at $115.96 at 12:27 p.m. ET and Coinbase's token, on Base, at $99.22 at 2:49 p.m. ET. At 6:45 a.m. ET Friday they traded at $90.22, $90.34 and $91.77. That is still about 9% above the $83 IPO price.

How much exists on-chain

Not much. At about 7 a.m. ET Friday, CoinGecko listed about 26,300 Robinhood tokens worth $2.37 million, 8,850 Coinbase tokens worth $0.81 million and 8,130 Ondo tokens worth $0.73 million. That is under $4 million in all. The xStocks version shows no supply figure.

Trading was far larger than supply. The Robinhood token traded $36.3 million in 24 hours, $29.1 million of it in one Uniswap V3 pool on Robinhood's chain. The Coinbase token's busiest pool, on Aerodrome on Base, traded $6.27 million. Ondo's token traded mostly on LBank.

The bigger market is in price bets

On Hyperliquid, traders do not hold shares at all. They take perpetual futures, price bets that never expire. CoinGecko's Hyperliquid tickers, read at 6:31 a.m. ET, show the xyz:ANT market at $90.69, down 12.5% in 24 hours, after $1.71 billion of trading. Only bitcoin's market, at $5.21 billion, traded more. Ether's traded $969 million.

Open bets on xyz:ANT stood at $129.5 million. A second market, io:ANTH, which CoinGecko tags as Anthropic pre-IPO, held $54.3 million and traded $185 million.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the gap between $4 million of tokens and $1.71 billion of perp trading suggests most on-chain demand for Anthropic went into leveraged bets rather than ownership.
  • Robinhood's and Ondo's tokens peaked 22 minutes apart and then slid together, which suggests they follow the same share price rather than separate on-chain demand.
  • Even after the slide, the tokens traded about 9% above the $83 IPO price. The losses sit with day-one buyers near $116.

What's next

  • Whether token supply grows past $4 million once more issuers mint.
  • Open bets on xyz:ANT against $129.5 million.
  • The tokens against the $83 IPO price.
