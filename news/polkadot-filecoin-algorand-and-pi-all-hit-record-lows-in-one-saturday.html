Polkadot, Filecoin, Algorand and Pi all hit record lows in one Saturday evening

As bitcoin slid on Saturday, November 14, 2026, Polkadot's DOT, Filecoin's FIL, Algorand's ALGO and Pi Network's PI all set all-time lows on CoinGecko within about three and a half hours. Each is now 97% to 99.7% below its peak.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Algorand's ALGO and Pi Network's PI set record lows at about 5:13 p.m. ET on November 14, 2026; Polkadot's DOT and Filecoin's FIL followed at about 8:46 p.m. ET, per CoinGecko.
  • DOT bottomed at about $0.684 and FIL at about $0.588. Both are down more than 35% in 30 days, against about 20% for bitcoin.
  • By 6:44 a.m. ET Sunday the four had bounced 5% to 8% off their lows, but all four are still more than 97% below their record highs.

What happened

Four coins in CoinGecko's top 100 by market value set new all-time lows on Saturday evening, November 14, 2026, as the wider market fell. Algorand's ALGO hit about $0.0750 and Pi Network's PI about $0.0602, both at about 5:13 p.m. ET. About three and a half hours later, at about 8:46 p.m. ET, Polkadot's DOT hit about $0.684 and Filecoin's FIL about $0.588.

DOT and FIL carry the exact same timestamp on CoinGecko, which fits one market-wide wave of selling rather than news about either project. We found no statement from any of the four projects tied to the drop.

Bitcoin traded at about $65,508 at 6:44 a.m. ET Sunday, down 13.6% in seven days, after a 24-hour low of $62,081.

How far they have fallen

These are some of the best-known names from earlier cycles. Three of the four peaked in 2019 or 2021; PI peaked on February 26, 2025, at $2.99. DOT's record of $54.98 came on November 4, 2021, and FIL's $236.84 on April 1, 2021. ALGO's $3.56 dates to June 20, 2019.

Measured from those peaks, FIL is down about 99.7%, DOT about 98.7%, PI about 97.9% and ALGO about 97.8%, per CoinGecko's market data.

Where they stand now

At 6:44 a.m. ET on Sunday, November 15, DOT traded at about $0.736, down 6.5% in 24 hours and 7.6% above its low. Its market value was about $1.26 billion, rank 61 on CoinGecko.

FIL traded at about $0.624, down 9.1% in a day, the steepest daily drop of the four, and 6.1% above its low. ALGO was at about $0.0800 and PI at about $0.0633, each 5% to 7% above their lows.

Outside the top 100, Kava's KAVA set another record low of about $0.0320 at 9:14 p.m. ET Saturday, below the low we reported that morning.

What a record low means

An all-time low is the lowest price CoinGecko has recorded since it began tracking a coin. It says nothing on its own about a project's code or team. It does mean that every holder who bought at any earlier time is at a loss on paper, which can keep pressure on the price when the market bounces and some holders sell to get out near break-even.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the timing tells the story. Lows that land in the same minute for unrelated coins point to forced and panic selling across the market, not to project news.
  • The likely test is the next bitcoin bounce. Coins that set fresh lows on a weak day often lag when the market recovers.
  • We think the 97% to 99.7% drops from their peaks show how far some big names of 2019 and 2021 have faded, even while they stay in the top 100.

What's next

  • Whether DOT, FIL, ALGO and PI hold above Saturday's lows
  • Bitcoin's weekly close on Sunday, November 15
  • Any project statements tied to the drops
