Rumor vs record: XRP Ledger users fell 15%, not 60%. Four more weekend claims checked

A headline that the XRP Ledger lost 60% of its users spread overnight. XRPSCAN's counts show the drop only if you compare a record day with a day that is not over. We checked it and four other viral weekend claims.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • XRPSCAN counted 250,286 active users on the XRP Ledger on Saturday, November 21, 2026, 14.5% below the 292,890 record of November 19 and 67% above October 15.
  • A drop of about 60% shows up only against Sunday's unfinished count of 119,642, taken with less than half the day's ledgers closed.
  • XRP's 50-day average did slip under its 200-day average on November 21, and SHIB is back at April 2021 prices. A reported bitcoin print near $17,000 on Kraken could not be checked on any record we could open.

Why we did this check

As bitcoin bounced to about $60,930 on Sunday, November 22, 2026, a set of weekend headlines and posts spread on crypto X, led by a claim that the XRP Ledger had lost 60% of its users. We checked five of them against ledger and price records. We do not name the people who posted them. The table above gives each verdict and its source.

The XRP Ledger users claim

XRPSCAN's daily metrics, read at 6:40 a.m. ET, count 250,286 active users on Saturday, November 21, by UTC day. The record was 292,890 on Thursday, November 19. That is a drop of 14.5%, not 60%.

The 60% figure matches only one comparison: Thursday's record against Sunday's count of 119,642. But Sunday was not over. XRPSCAN had logged 9,573 ledgers for the day, against about 20,650 on a full day. Compared with mid-October, users are up: 149,790 on October 15.

The price claims

On CoinMarketCap's daily closes, XRP's 50-day average dropped under its 200-day average on November 21, a pattern traders call a death cross. It is a lagging signal: it describes the fall from $1.33 in mid-October that already happened. XRP traded at $0.930 at 6:37 a.m. ET, per CoinGecko.

For SHIB, the records back both claims. Its price is where it was on April 19, 2021, and its November loss is 22.4% on daily closes.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the XRP users claim is a common data trap: comparing a full day with a day still in progress. Any daily count read in the morning will look like a crash.
  • A death cross and an April 2021 price are both true descriptions of a fall that has already happened. Neither one says what comes next.
  • The $17,000 Kraken print stays unverified. Until Kraken publishes trade data or a statement, a low on a single exchange is a claim, not a record.

What's next

  • XRPSCAN's full count for Sunday, November 22, once the UTC day closes at 7 p.m. ET.
  • Any statement or trade record from Kraken on the reported low print.
