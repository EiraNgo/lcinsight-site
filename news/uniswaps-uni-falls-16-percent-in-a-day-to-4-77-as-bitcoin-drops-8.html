Uniswap's UNI falls 16% in a day to $4.77 as bitcoin drops 8% to its lowest since July

UNI slid from about $5.70 to $4.77 between Friday and Saturday evening, its lowest level since late August, and is down 33% in a week. Bitcoin fell from about $68,240 to $62,720 and ether lost about 10% over the same 24 hours.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko showed UNI at about $4.76 at 9:01 p.m. ET on November 14, 2026, down 16.4% in 24 hours and 33% in seven days.
  • UNI touched about $4.68 at 4:15 p.m. ET, a level CoinGecko's daily prices last showed on August 30, 2026. It now trades about 89% below its May 2021 record of $44.92.
  • The whole market fell: bitcoin dropped about 8% to near $62,720, with a low near $62,120 at 8:45 p.m. ET, its lowest daily price since July 3. Ether lost about 10% to near $1,731.

The drop

UNI, the governance token of the Uniswap decentralized exchange, traded at about $4.76 at 9:01 p.m. ET on Saturday, November 14, 2026, according to CoinGecko. That is down 16.4% in 24 hours and 33% in seven days. Its 24-hour range ran from about $5.64 to $4.64.

Unlike a sudden crash, this was a steady slide. CoinGecko's five-minute samples show UNI near $5.70 at 9:05 p.m. ET on Friday and still near $5.50 at 7 a.m. ET on Saturday. The selling picked up after that: the worst hour ran from 8:35 to 9:35 a.m. ET, when UNI fell about 4.3% from $5.39 to $5.16. It reached a low near $4.68 at 4:15 p.m. ET, bounced to about $4.87 by 7 p.m. ET and slipped back to $4.77.

The whole market fell

UNI fell about twice as far as bitcoin. CoinGecko's samples show bitcoin dropping from about $68,240 at 9:05 p.m. ET on Friday to about $62,720 at 9:03 p.m. ET on Saturday, down 8.1%, with a low near $62,120 at 8:45 p.m. ET. CoinGecko's daily prices last showed bitcoin that low on July 3, 2026. Ether fell from about $1,927 to $1,731, down 10.2%. Other large DeFi tokens fell by similar amounts.

Where it traded

CoinGecko put UNI's market value at about $2.89 billion, ranking it No. 30, with about $616 million traded in 24 hours, up from about $453 million a day earlier. Websea's UNI/USDT pair led with about $131 million, followed by Binance with about $66 million, then BTCC, XT.COM and OKX. Prices across these markets stayed in line, between about $4.75 and $4.77. UNI was also on CoinGecko's trending list during the evening.

The price data does not say why UNI fell, and we do not assign a cause. The move matched the direction of the wider market, only larger.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think UNI's slide reads as part of a broad sell-off rather than a token-specific shock: it fell steadily for hours alongside bitcoin, ether and other DeFi tokens, with no single crash candle. Its larger fall fits a token that tends to swing harder than bitcoin.
