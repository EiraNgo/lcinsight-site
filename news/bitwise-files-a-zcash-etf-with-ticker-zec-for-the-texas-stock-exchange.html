Bitwise files a Zcash ETF with ticker ZEC for the Texas Stock Exchange, fee still blank

Bitwise's S-1, filed November 17, names Coinbase as custodian and says the fund will not stake. The fee is still blank. Separately, Wednesday's bitcoin ETF outflow was revised to $306.2 million, almost double the first reading.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Bitwise Zcash ETF filed an S-1 registration with the SEC on November 17, 2026. Its shares are expected to list on the Texas Stock Exchange (TXSE) under the ticker ZEC.
  • Coinbase Custody Trust Company would hold the fund's Zcash, BNY Mellon would handle cash, and the fund says it will not stake. The sponsor fee is left blank as "0.__%".
  • CoinMarketCap's data now show U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs lost $306.2 million on Wednesday, November 18, not the $157.5 million first reported. November's outflow is now about $2.70 billion.

What Bitwise filed

Bitwise filed a registration statement (Form S-1) for the Bitwise Zcash ETF with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on November 17, 2026. An S-1 is the document a new fund must file before it can sell shares to the public. Filing it does not mean the SEC has approved the fund.

The trust is a Delaware statutory trust. Its shares are "anticipated to be listed" on the Texas Stock Exchange LLC (TXSE) under the ticker ZEC, the same symbol the coin uses on crypto markets. The fund would hold Zcash (ZEC) directly and value it with the CF Zcash-Dollar US Settlement Price, a benchmark from CF Benchmarks set at 4:00 p.m. ET each day.

Who holds the coins, and what is still missing

The filing names Coinbase Custody Trust Company as the Zcash custodian. Coinbase, Inc. would execute trades only when none of the approved trading firms (Wintermute, Nonco and Virtu) can, and BNY Mellon would serve as cash custodian, administrator and transfer agent. The fund "will not stake or otherwise seek to derive additional Zcash" from its holdings, the S-1 says.

Key numbers are still open. The sponsor fee appears only as "0.__% per annum", and the sponsor may waive part of it for a time. Bitwise Asset Management bought 8 shares at $25 each on October 15, 2026, and a Bitwise affiliate is expected to buy the first 20,000 shares for $500,000 as seed money. The filing also explains that Zcash supports both public (transparent) and private (shielded) transfers.

ZEC traded at $954.89 at 6:35 a.m. ET on November 19, up 1.9% in 24 hours and down 3.9% in seven days, CoinGecko data show. It ranks 11th by market value at about $16.3 billion.

Wednesday's bitcoin ETF outflow, revised

On Thursday morning we reported a $157.5 million outflow from U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs on Wednesday, November 18, based on the first reading. CoinMarketCap's ETF flow data now show an outflow of $306.2 million for that day, after more issuers reported. That makes it the fifth-largest outflow day of November so far, just behind November 11 ($308.6 million).

With the revision, bitcoin funds have lost about $2.70 billion over 13 sessions in November, with only two inflow days. CoinMarketCap's ether series also doubled Wednesday's ether fund outflow, to $34.4 million from $17.0 million, putting November at about minus $701.5 million.

What happens next

The S-1 starts a review process. Bitwise still has to fill in the fee and other blanks in later amendments, and the fund cannot launch until its registration is effective and the exchange can list it. The filing gives no target date.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the choice of the Texas Stock Exchange matters as much as the coin. A privacy coin fund tests which listings the new exchange is ready to take on.
  • The blank fee suggests Bitwise is still watching what rivals charge. The likely result is a fee set close to launch, as with earlier crypto funds.
  • A doubled revision for Wednesday is a reminder that first-day ETF flow numbers can be far off. Late reporters can change the picture.

What's next

  • An amended S-1 with the sponsor fee filled in.
  • Whether the Texas Stock Exchange files to list the shares.
  • Thursday's bitcoin ETF flows and any further revision to Wednesday.
