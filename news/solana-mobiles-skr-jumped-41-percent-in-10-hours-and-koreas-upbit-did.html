Solana Mobile's SKR jumped 41% in 10 hours, and Korea's Upbit did half the trading

Seeker, the token tied to Solana Mobile, rose from under 1 cent to $0.0122 on Sunday night as daily trading roughly quadrupled. Korean won markets on Upbit and Bithumb carried most of it.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko's hourly data shows SKR at $0.00867 at 2 p.m. ET on November 15, 2026, and $0.01221 at midnight ET, up 41%. It traded at $0.01144 at 12:36 a.m. ET on November 16, up 29% in 24 hours.
  • Rolling 24-hour trading rose from about $2.2 million on Sunday afternoon to $9.4 million. Upbit's SKR/KRW market was the largest in CoinGecko's exchange list.
  • SKR is still 79.5% below its $0.0558 record from January 21, 2026, and down 18.4% over 30 days.

What moved

SKR, the Seeker token tied to Solana Mobile, rose 41% in about 10 hours on Sunday, November 15, from $0.00867 at 2 p.m. ET to $0.01221 at midnight ET, per CoinGecko. Its CoinGecko listing links to solanamobile.com/skr and describes a community-run mobile app store and network on Solana, with no gatekeepers between app makers and users.

For most of the weekend SKR drifted just under 1 cent. CoinGecko's hourly prices show $0.00870 at 9 p.m. ET on Saturday and $0.00867 at 2 p.m. ET on Sunday, November 15. Then it climbed: $0.00944 at 7 p.m. ET, $0.0111 at 8 p.m. ET, a 17% jump in one hour, and $0.01221 at midnight ET, 41% above the afternoon price.

At 12:36 a.m. ET on Monday it had cooled to $0.01144, up 29% in 24 hours, per CoinGecko's market data. Its 24-hour range was $0.00865 to $0.01257. Bitcoin moved 0.1% in the same window.

Korea did the trading

CoinGecko's rolling 24-hour volume for SKR was about $2.2 million at 1 p.m. ET Sunday. It reached $7.0 million by 9 p.m. ET and $9.4 million at 12:36 a.m. ET.

In CoinGecko's per-exchange list, the biggest market by far was Upbit's SKR/KRW pair, at about $12.2 million, roughly half of all volume listed there. Bithumb's won market added $3.2 million, Bybit $2.6 million and Gate $1.3 million. Coinbase and Kraken each had about $0.7 million. The per-exchange figures are counted differently from the headline volume, so the two totals do not match, but the order is clear: Korean won markets led.

Supply and value

CoinGecko counts 7.33 billion SKR circulating out of 10.73 billion, about 68%. That is a market value of $83.8 million (No. 277) and a fully diluted value of $122.7 million.

The CoinGecko page shows a record of $0.0558 on January 21, 2026, one day after its low of $0.00542, so the token has spent most of the year sliding. Even after Sunday's move it is 79.5% below that record, 18.4% lower over 30 days and only 3.2% higher over seven days.

What we could not check

We did not find a Solana Mobile statement in the records we read that explains the timing. Who bought on Upbit is not visible in exchange data.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the Upbit share is the key detail: when a mid-size token moves mostly on one Korean won market, the price can swing back just as fast once that local buying stops.
  • The likely test is whether volume holds near $9 million. Before Sunday evening it ran near $2 million a day.
  • We would treat the jump as a Sunday-night event until weekday trading confirms it. SKR is still lower over 30 days and only 3.2% higher over seven.

What's next

  • Upbit's share of SKR trading in the next 24 hours
  • Any Solana Mobile announcement about the Seeker app store or token
  • Whether SKR holds above 1 cent
