Allora's first cliff unlocks 160 million ALLO on November 11, about 38% of circulating supply

One year after launch, Allora's schedule frees a third of the tokens held by early backers and core contributors. At CoinGecko's price on November 9, that is about $23.6 million of new supply against a $62 million market cap.

LC Insight Editorial Team4 min read

Key points

  • Allora's tokenomics give early backers (31.05%) and core contributors (17.5%) a 12-month cliff, with 33% of their tokens unlocking at the cliff.
  • Allora launched its mainnet and ALLO on November 11, 2025, so the first cliff falls on November 11, 2026: about 160.2 million ALLO by our math from the official schedule.
  • CoinGecko showed about 420.9 million ALLO circulating at $0.1473 on November 9, so the cliff equals roughly 38% of circulating supply and about $23.6 million.

What unlocks and when

Allora is a network for AI models that make predictions, paid in its ALLO token. Its official tokenomics post from October 23, 2025, sets a total supply of 1 billion ALLO. Two groups, early backers with 31.05% and core contributors with 17.5%, are on what the post calls a 3-year lockup: a 12-month cliff where 33% unlocks, then the rest vests month by month over 24 months.

The Allora Foundation announced the mainnet and ALLO launch on November 11, 2025. Twelve months later is November 11, 2026.

The numbers

Together the two groups hold 48.55% of supply, or 485.5 million ALLO. A third of that (33%) is about 160.2 million ALLO. That is our calculation from Allora's published schedule, not a figure the foundation has posted for this date.

CoinGecko showed ALLO at $0.1473 at about 9:01 p.m. ET on November 9, 2026, with 420.9 million tokens circulating and a market cap of about $62.0 million. At that price the cliff is worth about $23.6 million, and it would lift circulating supply by about 38%. ALLO was down 7.3% over seven days.

A figure of 88.5% of circulating supply has been shared for this unlock. We could not match it to Allora's schedule or CoinGecko's supply data.

What comes after the cliff

The other 67% of the two groups' tokens, about 325.3 million ALLO, then vests over 24 months: roughly 13.6 million a month. The foundation's own 9.35% and the ecosystem and partnerships pool (8.85%) each unlocked part of their share at launch and release the rest linearly over two years, adding about 3.7 million ALLO a month between them by our math.

Why unlocked does not mean sold

An unlock makes tokens transferable. It does not force anyone to sell. Backers can hold, stake or move tokens to market makers, and contributors often face their own company rules. The real test is what the wallets do in the days after November 11. This is not investment advice.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think this is one of the heavier unlocks of the week relative to size. A release near 38% of the float is a big test for a token with about $62 million in market value.
  • Because the new tokens go only to early backers and the team, any selling would come from the people with the lowest cost basis, which is why markets tend to watch these cliffs closely.
  • The monthly vesting that follows is smaller but steady, so supply pressure, if it comes, does not end on November 11.

What's next

  • Any statement from the Allora Foundation on the November 11 release
  • Large ALLO transfers to exchanges after the cliff
  • CoinGecko circulating supply after November 11
