Terra Luna Classic (LUNC) jumps 17% in a day as Binance carries 41% of trading

LUNC rose about 17% in 24 hours to $0.00004528 early Thursday and climbed CoinGecko's trending list. Binance handled about $14.3 million of the roughly $35 million traded, including $5.6 million in Turkish lira.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows LUNC at $0.00004528 at 4:01 a.m. ET on November 19, 2026, up 16.6% in 24 hours and 18.8% in seven days, but down 1.1% over 30 days.
  • About $34.9 million traded across CoinGecko's listed markets in 24 hours. Binance carried about $14.3 million (41%), BTCC 11.5%, LBank 9% and Pionex 6.6%.
  • Even after the jump, LUNC is 99.99996% below the $119.18 record that CoinGecko lists for this asset from April 5, 2022.

The move

Terra Luna Classic's token LUNC traded between $0.00003822 and $0.00004965 in the 24 hours before Thursday morning, according to CoinGecko. At 4:01 a.m. ET on November 19, 2026 it stood at $0.00004528, up 16.6% on the day and 18.8% on the week. Over 30 days it was still down 1.1%, so the jump mostly undid a slide rather than setting a new range. LUNC ranked 141st by market cap and was among the top trending coins on CoinGecko's trending list, second only to bitcoin at 4:02 a.m. ET.

Where it traded

CoinGecko's ticker data put about $34.9 million of LUNC volume across all listed markets in 24 hours. Binance handled about $14.3 million (41%). Of that, about $5.6 million was in Binance's LUNC/TRY market, priced in Turkish lira. BTCC followed with about $4.0 million (11.5%), LBank $3.2 million (9%) and Pionex $2.3 million (6.6%). OrangeX, CoinW and Bitkub each had smaller shares.

By quote currency, tether (USDT) pairs carried about $26.1 million, Turkish lira pairs $5.6 million and Thai baht pairs about $1.5 million.

Supply to keep in mind

CoinGecko lists about 5.52 trillion LUNC in circulation out of a total supply of about 6.44 trillion, with no maximum supply set. At Thursday's price, that puts the market cap at about $250 million and the fully diluted value at about $292 million. We did not find an official explanation for the jump in the data we checked, so we do not assign one.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • A 17% day that leaves LUNC lower than a month ago looks more like a rebound than a breakout. With 41% of volume on one exchange and a large share in lira, the move leans on a few venues rather than broad demand.

What's next

  • Whether LUNC holds above the 24-hour low of $0.00003822
  • Whether Binance's share of trading falls as the move cools
  • Any official statement from the Terra Classic community about the move
