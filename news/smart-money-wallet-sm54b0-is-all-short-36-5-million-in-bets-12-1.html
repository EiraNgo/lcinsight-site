Smart money wallet SM54b0 is all short: $36.5 million in bets, $12.1 million up on paper

The No. 9 trader on DeepSea's 7-day board has ten open Hyperliquid shorts and no long positions. Together they show about $12.1 million of paper profit.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • SM54b0 ranks No. 9 on DeepSea's smart money board: +$8.51 million in 7 days, $33.61 million in assets.
  • Its biggest short is $16.13 million of Solana at 20x leverage, entered near $105.91, with a $7.17 million paper gain.
  • All ten open positions are shorts, worth about $36.5 million, with about $12.1 million of open profit.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's smart money board ranks wallets by 7-day profit. SM54b0 is No. 9, a Hyperliquid futures trader (a bet on price moves with borrowed money). Its owner is unknown, and it has not been covered here before.

Its DeepSea page shows $33.61 million in total assets, mostly $22.82 million in USDC plus $5.54 million of HYPE. Profit: +$8.51 million in 7 days, +$18.58 million in 30 days, +$16.04 million in 90 days. It trades little: 30-day volume is only $9.86 million.

It has ten open positions, all shorts (bets on a price fall):

  • Solana: $16.13 million, 20x, entry near $105.91, paper gain $7.17 million
  • Ether: $7.27 million, 20x, paper gain $2.65 million
  • HYPE: $5.26 million, 10x, paper gain $54,100
  • Seven smaller shorts (PUMP, LINK, bitcoin, AAVE, GRASS, XMR, VVV), $0.54 to $1.39 million each

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 17, 2026, 6:46 a.m. ET.

Checking the numbers

CoinGecko showed Solana at $73.34 at 6:40 a.m. ET. A $16.13 million short implies about 220,000 SOL. At an entry of $105.91, that is a gain of about $7.16 million, matching DeepSea within rounding. Adding DeepSea's ten open-profit figures gives about $12.07 million, against $8.51 million of profit already booked in 7 days.

Solana is 44% of the short book. DeepSea lists two small losing positions (LINK and XMR). Data from DeepSea and CoinGecko.

New to this?

A short is a bet that a price will fall. The trader borrows the coin, sells it, and hopes to buy it back cheaper. Leverage (20x here) multiplies both gains and losses, so a small price rise can wipe out a large part of a position. "Paper gain" means the profit is not locked in until the position is closed. Smart money is DeepSea's label for wallets with a record of profit, not a promise of future results. DeepSea also shows that this wallet earned +$16.04 million over 90 days and +$20.68 million over 180 days, so the recent week is part of a longer record.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think this wallet is a patient holder, not a fast trader: $9.86 million of volume in 30 days against $36.5 million of open shorts.
  • Most of the profit is still on paper. A rebound in Solana would shrink it fast, and 20x leverage leaves little room.
  • This is a data view of one wallet, not a forecast.
