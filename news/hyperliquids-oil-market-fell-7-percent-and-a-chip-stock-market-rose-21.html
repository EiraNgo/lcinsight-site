Hyperliquid's oil market fell 7% and a chip-stock market rose 21%. Crypto bets shrank $420M

Hyperliquid's crypto markets held $9.18 billion of open bets, down from $9.60 billion a day earlier. Its stock and commodity markets now hold a third of the total.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Hyperliquid's 179 crypto markets held $9.18 billion of open bets in CoinGecko's data last updated at 12:16 a.m. ET on November 10, down about $420 million from $9.60 billion late on November 8.
  • Its 160 builder-run stock and commodity markets held $4.45 billion, 32.7% of the $13.63 billion total.
  • The Brent oil market fell 7.26% in 24 hours with $323.7 million open, and shorts were paying longs. The SOXL chip-stock market rose 20.73%.

Oil down, chips up

Hyperliquid lists perpetual futures, bets on a price that never expire. It also lists builder markets on stocks and commodities. CoinGecko shows 160 of them with the XYZ: prefix, next to 179 crypto markets.

In the 24 hours to 12:16 a.m. ET on November 10, those markets moved the most. CoinGecko's per-market data shows the BRENTOIL market down 7.26% to $90.38, with $323.7 million in open bets, and the CL (U.S. crude) market down 6.19% to $85.16 with $145.3 million open. Chip stocks went the other way: SOXL, a leveraged chip-stock fund ticker, rose 20.73% and traded $409.6 million in a day, AMD rose 11.07% and MU 5.85%.

Who is paying whom

Funding is a small fee that keeps a perp's price near the real one. When it is negative, shorts pay longs, a sign that more traders are betting on a fall. Brent oil's funding was minus 0.011% an hour and crude's minus 0.0053%. On the SKHX market, longs paid 0.0147% an hour, about 12 times bitcoin's 0.00125%.

Ether's market, which had negative funding on November 9, was back to positive at 0.00072% an hour with $1.69 billion open. Solana's flipped back to positive too, with $412.4 million open.

Crypto bets keep shrinking

Open bets on Hyperliquid's crypto markets fell to $9.18 billion, about $420 million less than the $9.60 billion CoinGecko showed late on November 8. Bitcoin held $3.15 billion, HYPE $1.62 billion and Zcash $486.8 million as ZEC fell 5.93%. The builder markets edged up to $4.45 billion from $4.41 billion, and their 24-hour trading of $5.41 billion nearly matched crypto's $5.73 billion.

Hyperliquid's own API could not be reached from our tools this run, so these figures come from CoinGecko's copy of Hyperliquid's market data.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the split shows Hyperliquid traders using it more and more as a place to bet on oil and chip stocks, while crypto bets keep shrinking.
  • Negative funding on oil after a 7% drop means many traders added shorts into the fall. If oil bounces, those shorts would be the first under pressure.
  • Ether and Solana funding turning positive again suggests the selling pressure seen on November 9 has eased, at least on this exchange.

What's next

  • Whether builder markets pass a third of Hyperliquid's open bets for good
  • Whether oil funding stays negative if prices steady
  • Whether crypto open bets fall below $9 billion
