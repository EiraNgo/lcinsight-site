Sentient (SENT) up 19% as volume jumps 15-fold to $66 million, led by Upbit won trading

SENT climbed to about $0.0233 early Thursday, November 19, up 43% since November 10. Daily trading went from about $4 million to $66 million, and Korean won pairs on Upbit carried a quarter of it.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows Sentient (SENT) at $0.02328 at 6:35 a.m. ET on November 19, 2026, up 19.4% in 24 hours and 42.5% in seven days. It is still 52% below its January 31 record of $0.04805.
  • Reported 24-hour volume reached about $66 million, against about $4 million a day on November 10 and 11. That is about 39% of SENT's $169 million market value.
  • Upbit's SENT/KRW market carried about 24.8% of the volume in CoinGecko's ticker list, Binance's USDT pair 18% and Binance's Turkish lira pair 8.9%. We found no official cause for the move.

The move

Sentient's token SENT traded between $0.01918 and $0.02546 in the 24 hours to early Thursday, according to CoinGecko. At 6:35 a.m. ET on November 19, 2026, it stood at $0.02328, up 19.4% on the day and 42.5% on the week.

The climb has been steady rather than a single spike. CoinGecko's daily prices, taken at midnight UTC, show SENT at $0.01625 on November 10, $0.01782 on November 17, $0.01867 on November 18 and $0.02061 at the start of November 19, before the latest leg up. It ranked 174th by market value and was among the biggest gainers with a market cap above $100 million.

Sentient describes itself on CoinGecko as an open-source AI network built to compete with closed AI labs. The SENT token runs on Ethereum.

Where it traded

Trading jumped far more than the price. CoinGecko's daily data show about $4.0 million of SENT volume on November 10 and $3.6 million on November 12. The 24-hour figure at 6:35 a.m. ET on Thursday was about $66 million, more than 15 times those days.

CoinGecko's ticker list at about 6:50 a.m. ET, which counts about $85.9 million across 47 markets, shows Upbit's SENT/KRW market in front with about $21.3 million (24.8%). Binance's SENT/USDT market followed with $15.5 million (18%), and Binance's SENT/TRY market, priced in Turkish lira, added $7.7 million (8.9%). OKX, OrangeX, WhiteBIT, Bybit and LBank each carried 3.6% to 5.3%. A second Korean exchange, Bithumb, added 3%, so won pairs made up close to 28% of the listed total.

Upbit's won price was about 2% above the dollar price on Binance at the time we checked, a gap that often shows heavy local buying.

Supply to keep in mind

CoinGecko lists about 7.24 billion SENT in circulation out of a maximum supply of about 34.36 billion, so roughly 21% of all tokens are out. At Thursday's price, that gives a market value of about $169 million and a fully diluted value, counting every token that can ever exist, of about $800 million.

We did not find an official Sentient announcement that explains the jump in the pages we checked, so we do not assign one. The token's record low was $0.01131 on July 29, 2026, so SENT has about doubled from that point.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the volume matters more than the 19%. A 15-fold jump in trading with a quarter of it on one Korean won market points to a burst of local demand, which can fade as fast as it came.
  • With only about a fifth of the supply in circulation, the gap between the $169 million market value and the $800 million fully diluted value is large. Tokens that are not yet circulating could reach the market later.
  • The 2% Upbit premium is the number to watch. If local buyers stop paying above the global price, the Korean rush is likely cooling.

What's next

  • Whether Upbit's share of trading falls as the move cools.
  • Whether SENT holds above the 24-hour low of $0.01918.
  • Any official Sentient statement or token unlock schedule.
