Aster's ASTER drops 21% in 80 minutes to $0.48 while bitcoin slips about 2%

ASTER drifted near $0.62 for most of Saturday, then fell from about $0.61 at 2:35 p.m. ET to a low near $0.483 at 3:55 p.m. ET on November 14. Trading volume more than doubled and the token jumped onto CoinGecko's trending list.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko showed ASTER at about $0.501 at 7:02 p.m. ET on November 14, 2026, down 22.5% in 24 hours and 26.5% in seven days.
  • Most of the fall came in 80 minutes: from about $0.610 at 2:35 p.m. ET to about $0.483 at 3:55 p.m. ET, a drop of about 21%. Bitcoin lost about 2% over a similar stretch.
  • ASTER's 24-hour trading volume rose to about $188 million, from roughly $70 million to $79 million before the drop, and the token sat at No. 7 on CoinGecko's trending list in the evening.

The drop

ASTER, the token of the Aster perpetual futures exchange, traded at about $0.501 at 7:02 p.m. ET on Saturday, November 14, 2026, according to CoinGecko. That is down 22.5% in 24 hours. Its 24-hour range ran from about $0.644 to $0.481.

For most of the day the price moved little: CoinGecko's samples kept it between about $0.61 and $0.64 from midnight to 2:35 p.m. ET. Then it broke lower. The single sharpest step came between 2:45 and 2:50 p.m. ET, when ASTER fell from about $0.601 to $0.548, almost 9% in five minutes. ASTER's record high was $2.41 on September 24, 2025, so the token now trades about 79% below that peak.

Where it traded

CoinGecko put ASTER's market value at about $1.29 billion, ranking it No. 59, with about $188 million traded in 24 hours. Before the drop, its rolling 24-hour volume sat between about $70 million and $79 million. Binance's ASTER/USDT pair led trading with about $29.2 million, followed by KuCoin, WhiteBIT and Bybit. Unlike some recent crashes, prices across these big markets stayed in line, all between about $0.497 and $0.499 when we checked.

What we know about the cause

The price data does not say why ASTER fell, and we found no primary-source explanation at publication time, so we do not assign a cause. The wider market was weaker but far calmer: CoinGecko's bitcoin samples show BTC slipping from about $65,845 at 1 p.m. ET to about $64,387 at 4:25 p.m. ET, a drop of about 2%.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the shape of the move points to a burst of selling rather than a slow slide: the price sat still for hours, lost about a fifth of its value in 80 minutes while bitcoin moved about 2%, and volume more than doubled. Without an official statement, the cause remains open.
