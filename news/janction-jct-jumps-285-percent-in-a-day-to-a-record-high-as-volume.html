Janction (JCT) jumps 285% in a day to a record high as volume tops its market cap

JCT climbed from about $0.0025 to a new all-time high near $0.0107 on Sunday morning. About $175 million changed hands in a day, more than the token's $120 million market cap, and three venues handled 86% of it.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows JCT at $0.01056 at 7:08 a.m. ET on November 8, 2026, up 285% in 24 hours, after a new all-time high of $0.01066 at 6:48 a.m. ET.
  • 24-hour trading volume was about $174.7 million against a market cap of about $120.5 million. KuCoin (35%), PancakeSwap Infinity on BNB Chain (31%) and Bitget (20%) carried most of it.
  • About 11.49 billion of JCT's 50 billion maximum supply is in circulation, so the fully diluted value is about $524 million, more than four times the market cap.

The move

Janction's token JCT traded as low as $0.00253 in the 24 hours before Sunday morning, according to CoinGecko. By 6:48 a.m. ET on November 8, 2026 it had printed a new all-time high of $0.01066, a little over four times that low. At 7:08 a.m. ET it was at $0.01056, up 285% on the day, 110% on the week and 340% over 30 days. JCT was among the top trending coins on CoinGecko the same morning.

Where it traded

CoinGecko's ticker data put about $175 million of JCT volume across all markets in 24 hours. KuCoin handled about $61.5 million (35%), the PancakeSwap Infinity CLMM pool on BNB Chain about $55.1 million (31%) and Bitget about $34.2 million (20%). Uniswap V3 on BNB Chain, LBank and MEXC followed with smaller shares. JCT exists on Ethereum and BNB Chain.

Daily volume larger than the market cap means the same coins changed hands many times in one day. That kind of turnover often comes with sharp swings in both directions.

Supply to keep in mind

CoinGecko lists 11.49 billion JCT in circulation out of a total and maximum supply of 50 billion, about 23%. At Sunday's price, the market cap is about $120.5 million and the fully diluted value about $524 million. We did not find an official explanation for the jump in the data we checked, so we do not assign one.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • A 4x move in a day with volume above market cap is a sign of heavy short-term trading. The gap between circulating and maximum supply is the number to watch, since more than three quarters of the supply is not yet trading.

What's next

  • Whether JCT holds near the new high
  • Any official statement from Janction about the move
  • Changes in circulating supply
