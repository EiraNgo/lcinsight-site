World Liberty's USD1 shrank $1.04 billion in 23 days, most of it on Solana and Ethereum

DefiLlama data show the dollar stablecoin fell from $3.89 billion on November 3 to $2.85 billion, a 26.7% drop, while its price stayed at $1. The shrinking stopped on November 23.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • USD1 in circulation fell from $3.886 billion on November 3 to $2.847 billion on November 26, 2026, a drop of $1.04 billion or 26.7%, DefiLlama data show.
  • Over 30 days, Solana lost $527 million of USD1 and Ethereum $481 million. BNB Chain lost $80 million and is now the token's biggest network.
  • The price held: CoinGecko showed $0.9997 at 6:44 a.m. ET, in a 24-hour range of $0.9995 to $1.00.

A steady drain, then a pause

World Liberty Financial's USD1 lost $1.04 billion of supply in 23 days, falling 26.7% from $3.886 billion on November 3 to $2.847 billion on November 26, DefiLlama data show. USD1 is a dollar stablecoin, a token meant to stay worth $1. When holders redeem it for dollars, tokens are removed and the supply shrinks.

According to DefiLlama's daily USD1 data, the supply was almost flat from October 28 to November 3, then fell almost every day from November 4. Daily drops of $70 million to $110 million were common through November 17. The largest was $110.6 million, from November 16 to November 17. Since November 23 the total has stayed near $2.85 billion, up just $2.3 million in three days.

CoinGecko's market value chart tells the same story: about $3.86 billion on November 3 and $2.85 billion on November 26.

Where the tokens left

DefiLlama's network split compares today with 30 days ago. On Solana, USD1 fell from $1.39 billion to $868 million, down 37.8%. On Ethereum it fell from $1.31 billion to $833 million, down 36.6%. On BNB Chain it slipped only 6.8%, to $1.10 billion, which makes it the largest home for USD1 now. Solana also led the past week's smaller drop, losing $54.9 million of USD1.

On Etherscan, the Ethereum explorer, the USD1 contract shows a total supply of 854,196,443 USD1, held by 42,872 addresses, with 801 transfers in 24 hours. DefiLlama's circulating count on Ethereum is slightly lower, at $832.8 million.

Far steeper than USDT and USDC

Other dollar tokens shrank too, but much less. Over the same 30 days DefiLlama shows USDT down 4.6%, from $181.86 billion to $173.54 billion, and USDC down 2.8%, to $69.28 billion. USD1 lost 27.6% over that window.

The drop did not hurt the peg. CoinGecko counted $900 million of USD1 traded in 24 hours, about a third of its market value, and the price stayed between $0.9995 and $1.00 all day.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the steady pace is what stands out. A loss of more than $1 billion spread over two weeks of $70 million to $110 million days looks like ongoing redemptions, not one holder leaving at once.
  • The drain was concentrated on Solana and Ethereum while BNB Chain barely moved. The public data do not show who redeemed or why, so we do not guess at motives.
  • The pause since November 23 matters. If supply stays flat near $2.85 billion, the outflow may have run its course; a new daily drop would show it has not.

What's next

  • Whether USD1 supply on DefiLlama stays near $2.85 billion or starts falling again.
  • The Solana and Ethereum totals, which led the decline.
  • USD1's price against $1 on CoinGecko.
