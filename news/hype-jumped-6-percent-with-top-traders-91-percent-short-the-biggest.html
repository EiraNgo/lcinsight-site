HYPE jumped 6% with top traders 91% short. The biggest short is $17.9 million in the red

DeepSea counts $286.5 million of HYPE shorts against $26.8 million of longs among 63 top Hyperliquid traders. The biggest bet against HYPE, opened near $28.79, shows a $17.9 million paper loss.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • DeepSea's HYPE page shows 63 tracked top traders 91% short: $286.51 million short versus $26.81 million long.
  • HYPE traded near $70 after a 6.1% daily gain, per DeepSea and CoinGecko, above the shorts' average entry of $66.07.
  • The largest short, $30.36 million at 10x, was opened near $28.79 and shows a $17.89 million paper loss.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's HYPE-PERP page tracks the futures positions of top traders on Hyperliquid, the exchange behind the HYPE token. At 12:59 a.m. ET on Tuesday it counted 63 tracked wallets. Together they were 91% short (betting on a fall): $286.51 million short at an average entry of $66.07, against $26.81 million long at an average of $71.60.

The page put HYPE at $70.00, up 6.14% in a day, with $1.32 billion of open HYPE futures on Hyperliquid. Because HYPE sits above the shorts' average entry, the short side as a group was losing money at that moment.

The five largest positions were all shorts:

  • Smart trader #4: $30.36 million at 10x, entry $28.79, paper loss $17.89 million
  • Smart trader #55: $21.36 million at 10x, entry $84.02, paper gain $4.27 million
  • Smart trader #125: $18.20 million at 5x, entry $72.67, paper gain $691,700
  • Smart trader #83: $15.72 million at 5x, entry $73.13, paper gain $702,400
  • Smart trader #61: $15.17 million at 10x, entry $65.67, paper loss $940,000

The largest long on the list was $5.22 million at 10x, entered at $76.06, with a $430,700 paper loss.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 17, 2026, 12:59 a.m. ET.

Checking the numbers

New to the tools? Read what DeepSea is.

CoinGecko showed HYPE at $70.14, up 6.08% in 24 hours, at 12:54 a.m. ET, in line with DeepSea's price. We checked the largest short by hand. A $30.36 million position at $70.00 is about 433,700 HYPE. Opened at $28.79, it is $41.21 per token behind, about $17.87 million in all, matching DeepSea's $17.89 million within rounding.

The tracked shorts equal about 22% of the $1.32 billion of open HYPE futures DeepSea lists.

Why we did not use DeepSea's wider board

DeepSea's separate long/short board, which covers a wider set of wallets, still showed HYPE leaning long. Its bitcoin row matched our November 16 reading to the cent, so we treat that board as not updated and use the HYPE-PERP page here.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the crowded short side is the story to watch: when most big traders bet one way and the price moves the other, forced buying to close shorts can add to a rise.
  • We think the largest short is not under pressure yet, despite its paper loss. DeepSea lists its liquidation price at $144.57, about double HYPE's price.
  • Leverage on the five biggest shorts is modest, 5x to 10x. The nearest liquidation price among them on DeepSea's page is about $84.77, roughly 21% above HYPE's price, so a small further rise would not by itself force them closed.

What's next

  • Whether the 91% short share falls as HYPE rises
  • The $30.36 million short opened near $28.79
  • Open HYPE futures above or below $1.32 billion
