Bitwise's Avalanche ETF (BAVA) files its prospectus: 0.34% fee, 12% staking cut

The SEC filing for the Bitwise Avalanche ETF names NYSE Arca, Coinbase as AVAX custodian and a plan to stake some or all of the fund's AVAX.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Bitwise filed a 424B3 prospectus for the Bitwise Avalanche ETF on November 23, 2026. Ticker: BAVA, listed on NYSE Arca.
  • The sponsor fee is a unitary 0.34% a year of the fund's AVAX holdings.
  • The fund plans to stake some or all of its AVAX. Staking expenses are 12% of rewards, so the trust keeps about 88%.

The filing

Bitwise Avalanche ETF filed a 424B3 prospectus on SEC EDGAR on November 23, 2026 (file no. 333-290257). The prospectus is dated the same day. The fund is a Delaware statutory trust sponsored by Bitwise Investment Advisers. Its ticker is BAVA and its shares are to list on NYSE Arca.

We did not find a first trading date in the parts of the document we read.

Fees and custody

The prospectus sets a "unitary management fee of 0.34% per annum" of the trust's Avalanche holdings. Coinbase Custody Trust Company holds the AVAX. CSC Delaware Trust Company is trustee and The Bank of New York Mellon is administrator. Shares are priced off the CME CF Avalanche-Dollar Reference Rate (New York variant) at 4:00 p.m. ET.

Staking

The trust will stake some or all of its AVAX, using one or more staking agents. The sponsor may prefer Attestant, Ltd., an affiliate of Bitwise. Staking expenses equal 12% of the extra AVAX earned, so the trust receives about 88%. A liquidity reserve is expected to be about 30% of holdings.

Staking adds a fee layer and a conflict-of-interest question, because the staking agent may be a sponsor affiliate. The prospectus discloses it.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the 12% staking cut is the number to compare with other staking funds, more than the 0.34% headline fee.
  • A filing is not a launch. Without a stated trading date, readers should not assume BAVA trades yet.

What's next

  • A first trading date for BAVA.
  • Seed capital and first-day flows once it lists.
