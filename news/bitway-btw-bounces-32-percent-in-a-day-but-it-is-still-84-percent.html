Bitway (BTW) bounces 32% in a day, but it is still 84% below its October record

BTW climbed from a low near $0.335 late Monday to about $0.45 on Tuesday evening, after falling more than 60% in a week. A PancakeSwap pool on BNB Chain carried half the trading.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows BTW at $0.4517 at 9:02 p.m. ET on November 17, 2026, up 32.1% in 24 hours, after a low of about $0.335 at 10:45 p.m. ET on Monday.
  • The bounce comes after a long slide: BTW is still down 60.6% in seven days, 80.6% in 30 days and about 84% below its $2.77 record from October 13.
  • About $25 million traded in 24 hours. A PancakeSwap pool on BNB Chain handled about 51% of it, while bitcoin and ether each slipped about 1%.

The move

Bitway's token BTW hit about $0.335 at 10:45 p.m. ET on Monday, November 16, 2026, its lowest price of the week, according to CoinGecko. It climbed overnight and reached about $0.526 at 3:35 a.m. ET on Tuesday, a gain of roughly 57% from the low.

Part of that gain faded. BTW traded between about $0.40 and $0.47 for most of Tuesday and was at $0.4517 at 9:02 p.m. ET, up 32.1% in 24 hours. Over the same day CoinGecko shows bitcoin down 0.9% and ether down 1.3%, so the bounce was BTW's own.

It follows two steep drops we covered earlier, on November 13 and November 15. Even after Tuesday's gain, BTW is down 60.6% in seven days and 80.6% in 30 days, and about 84% below its all-time high of $2.77.

Where it traded

CoinGecko lists about $25.1 million of BTW volume in 24 hours across 19 markets, more than double the roughly $9.3 million in the day to Sunday evening. A BTW/USDT pool on PancakeSwap Infinity on BNB Chain handled about $12.8 million (51%). HTX carried about $4.1 million (16%), Bitget about $3.7 million (15%) and Gate about $2.6 million (10%).

That is still thin next to BTW's market value of about $680 million. When one pool sets half the price and daily turnover is under 4% of the market value, swings of this size can happen in either direction.

What Bitway is, and the supply

CoinGecko describes BTW as the utility and governance token of Bitway, which offers yield, payment and financing products, according to its listing and its website. The token runs on BNB Chain and Ethereum.

CoinGecko counts about 1.51 billion BTW in circulation out of a 10 billion maximum, or about 15%. At $0.4517 that is a market value of about $680 million and a fully diluted value of about $4.5 billion. Bitway has not given a reason for the bounce in any source we checked, and this story does not suggest one.

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a one-day bounce of this size means little on its own after a 60% weekly fall. About 85% of the maximum supply is not yet circulating, so the long-term supply picture has not changed. This is not investment advice.

What's next

  • Whether BTW holds above Monday's low near $0.335
  • Whether trading stays concentrated in the PancakeSwap pool
  • Any official statement from Bitway about the moves
