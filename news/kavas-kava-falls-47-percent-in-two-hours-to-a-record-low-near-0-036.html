Kava's KAVA falls 47% in two hours to a record low near $0.036

KAVA sat flat near $0.068 for two days, then broke down at 5:05 a.m. ET on November 14 and hit an all-time low of about $0.0356 before 7 a.m. ET. Prices on Korea's Upbit and Bithumb stayed far above the rest of the market.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko showed KAVA at about $0.0366 at 7:02 a.m. ET on November 14, 2026, down 47% in 24 hours from about $0.068.
  • The fall began at about 5:05 a.m. ET and set a new all-time low of about $0.0356 at 6:57 a.m. ET, more than 99% below the 2021 record of $9.12.
  • KAVA last traded at about $0.052 on Upbit and $0.069 on Bithumb, far above the roughly $0.037 on Binance, Gate and PancakeSwap.

The drop

Kava's token KAVA traded at about $0.0366 at 7:02 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 14, 2026, according to CoinGecko. That is down 47% in 24 hours. KAVA was the number three trending coin on CoinGecko Saturday morning.

For two days before, the price barely moved: CoinGecko's samples kept it between about $0.067 and $0.069 from the evening of November 12 until 5 a.m. ET on November 14. CoinGecko then recorded a new all-time low of about $0.0356 at 6:57 a.m. ET. KAVA's record high was $9.12 on August 30, 2021. New to these numbers? Our guide on market cap versus FDV explains them.

Where it traded

CoinGecko lists 54 KAVA markets with about $29 million of combined 24-hour volume, most of it after the drop began. Hourly volume was under $3 million before 5 a.m. ET and about $17 million in the hour that followed. Binance's KAVA/USDT pair led with about $6.9 million, then PancakeSwap on BNB Chain with $5.7 million.

Two Korean markets did not follow the fall. Upbit's KAVA/KRW pair last traded at about $0.052 and Bithumb's at about $0.069, close to the price before the drop, while Binance, Gate, Bybit and PancakeSwap were near $0.037. A gap that wide suggests those markets are not trading in line with the rest; the exchanges' own notices are the place to check why.

What we know about the cause

Not much yet. Some market posts link the fall to a security problem on the Kava chain, but we could not confirm that on any primary source we were able to open, including Kava's own channels, so we treat it as unverified and do not assign a cause. CoinGecko lists about 1.08 billion KAVA in circulation, all of the supply, for a market value of about $39 million. KAVA is down about 75% over the past year.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the shape of the move matters most. A token that sits flat for two days and then loses almost half its value in two hours, with volume jumping about six times, points to a specific event rather than a slow sell-off, and the Korean price gaps suggest not every market has caught up with it.

What's next

  • An official statement from Kava on what happened
  • Whether the Upbit and Bithumb prices close the gap with other markets
  • Whether KAVA holds above Saturday's record low of about $0.0356
  • Hourly volume as the day goes on
