Momentum (MMT) doubles to $0.25 in a day as trading volume hits 100 times last week's

The token of a Sui-based exchange ran from about $0.12 to $0.25 in 24 hours, with $310 million traded against a $51 million market value. It is still 94% below its 2025 record.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows MMT at $0.2531 at 6:44 a.m. ET on November 18, 2026, up 106% in 24 hours. It sat near the top of CoinGecko's trending list.
  • About $310 million of MMT traded in 24 hours, roughly six times its $51.5 million market value. A week earlier CoinGecko's daily volume figure was about $3 million.
  • Upbit's won market carried about 25% of the trading CoinGecko lists by venue, ahead of Coinstore (17%) and Binance's USDT and lira pairs (13% and 12%).

The move, hour by hour

Momentum's MMT token rose 106% in 24 hours to $0.2531 at 6:44 a.m. ET on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, while about $310 million of it traded, roughly 100 times the daily volume of a week earlier. Momentum is a decentralized exchange on the Sui network.

The token set an all-time low of $0.09526 on Friday, November 14, 2026, at 5:13 p.m. ET, according to CoinGecko. Four days later it was worth more than two and a half times that.

The climb came in two steps. On Tuesday, November 17, MMT traded near $0.117 until about 11 a.m. ET, then rose to about $0.138 by 11:40 a.m. ET and held there through the evening. The bigger jump came overnight: CoinGecko's five-minute prices show about $0.152 at midnight ET and about $0.220 at 1 a.m. ET, a gain of roughly 45% in one hour. The token kept rising to a 24-hour high of about $0.262 near 5:50 a.m. ET.

Over the same 24 hours bitcoin fell 0.9%, ether 1.1% and Sui's SUI 1.2%, so the move was MMT's own, not the market's.

Where it traded

CoinGecko's aggregate puts MMT's 24-hour volume at about $310 million. Seven days earlier its daily volume reading was about $3 million, so trading rose roughly a hundredfold.

Of the trading CoinGecko lists by market, Upbit's MMT/KRW pair handled about $105.8 million (24.8%). Coinstore's USDT pair followed with about $74.1 million (17.4%), then Binance's MMT/USDT at $55.8 million (13.1%) and Binance's MMT/TRY at $51.8 million (12.2%). Bithumb's won pair added $28.1 million (6.6%) and OKX $18.9 million (4.4%). The two big South Korean exchanges together carried about 31%.

Volume near six times a token's market value means its circulating coins changed hands several times in a day. Moves on that kind of turnover can reverse as fast as they arrive.

What Momentum is, and its supply

CoinGecko files Momentum under decentralized exchanges and the Sui ecosystem, and also lists it among Binance HODLer Airdrops and Binance Wallet IDO tokens. MMT exists as a native Sui coin and as a token on BNB Chain (0xac24...f34a). Its official X account is @MMTFinance.

CoinGecko counts about 204.1 million MMT in circulation out of a 1 billion maximum, or about 20%. New to supply math? Read FDV vs. market cap. At $0.2531 that is a market value of about $51.5 million (No. 393 on CoinGecko) and a fully diluted value of about $253 million. MMT's all-time high was $4.03 on November 4, 2025, so even after this week's run it is about 94% below that record.

We found no official statement from Momentum explaining the jump in the sources we could check, and this story does not suggest a cause. We could not open a Sui explorer from our tools this morning, so we do not report holder data.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a token that doubles from a fresh record low on volume a hundred times last week's is being moved by short-term traders on a few exchange order books, not by a visible change in the project.
  • Turnover of six times market value in a day means the same coins are changing hands again and again. That kind of move can unwind as fast as it came.
  • With about 80% of the maximum supply not yet circulating, the long-term overhang is large. This is not investment advice.

What's next

  • Whether MMT holds above about $0.22, the level it reached in the first overnight hour
  • Whether daily volume falls back toward the $3 million seen a week ago
  • Any official statement from Momentum on @MMTFinance
