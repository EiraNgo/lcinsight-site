Binance opens a $500 million Anthropic IPO token sale, a day after cutting its perp price 13x

Binance Wallet takes USDC orders for Anthropic stock tokens from 1 a.m. to 9 a.m. ET on Wednesday, at an indicative $70 to $78 each. On Tuesday, Binance Futures shrank the price of its Anthropic pre-IPO contract by a factor of 13.116 and renamed it ANTUSDT.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Binance Wallet's Anthropic IPO xStocks campaign accepts USDC from 1 a.m. to 9 a.m. ET on November 18, 2026, with a total cap of 500 million USDC and an indicative price of 70 to 78 USDC per token.
  • On November 17, Binance Futures applied a 13.116 scale factor to its ANTHROPICUSDT Pre-IPO Perp, matching a new estimate of 13,116,075,288 Anthropic shares, and renamed it ANTUSDT.
  • Binance says the IPO may not happen, allocation is not guaranteed and the tokens are not direct ownership of Anthropic shares.

The token sale

In an announcement signed November 18, 2026, Binance said its Binance Wallet app will run a subscription for Anthropic tokenized stocks from xStocks, a partner that charges a service fee. A tokenized stock is a crypto token meant to track the price of a share.

Applications run from 06:00 to 14:00 UTC on Wednesday, November 18 (1 a.m. to 9 a.m. ET). Users pay in USDC only, at least 100 USDC, and the money is held in a non-custodial smart contract until tokens are handed out or funds are refunded. The whole campaign is capped at 500 million USDC.

Each user's limit depends on Alpha Points, a Binance loyalty score: 20,000 USDC above zero points, up to 500,000 USDC above 200 points. Holders of a Trenchers Badge get 50,000 USDC more. Binance warns: "Users may receive full allocation, partial allocation, or no allocation." Unused funds are refunded.

The price, and a share count puzzle

Binance gives an indicative price of 70 to 78 USDC per token, before a 5% xStocks partner fee. It says this implies an Anthropic valuation of $1.26 trillion to $1.41 trillion. The final price is set after the subscription closes by the issuer and the underwriters, and tokens go out no earlier than two hours after Nasdaq opens on the distribution day, which Binance has not fixed.

The numbers do not line up with Binance's own futures contract. At 13,116,075,288 shares, the count Binance uses for its perp, $70 to $78 a share works out to about $918 billion to $1.02 trillion. Binance's valuation range instead implies about 18 billion shares. The notice does not say which share count it uses, and we could not confirm it.

How the perp was resized on Tuesday

Binance's ANTHROPICUSDT Pre-IPO Perp is a futures contract with no expiry that tracks one Anthropic share before listing. It had priced that share on an estimate of 1 billion shares since June 2. In a November 15 notice, Binance set a scale factor of 13.116, based on 13,116,075,288 shares it read from Anthropic's amended S-1 filing.

Under Binance's contract adjustment rules, the mark price is divided by the factor and position sizes are multiplied by it, so the dollar value of a position stays the same. Open profit or loss is settled in cash first.

Trading stopped at 08:30 UTC (3:30 a.m. ET) on November 17. Binance finished early, at 10:25 UTC (5:25 a.m. ET), and reopened at 10:30 UTC (5:30 a.m. ET), half an hour ahead of schedule. The contract now trades as ANTUSDT. All open orders were canceled and trading bots were shut down beforehand.

What Binance does not promise

Both notices carry warnings. Binance says neither product gives ownership of Anthropic shares, and that it is not endorsed by or affiliated with the issuer. The token notice says there is no guarantee the IPO goes ahead. The futures notice says the perp's price may not reflect the IPO price. The campaign notice does not name a chain, a listing date or a ticker, and does not list which countries are excluded.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the order of events is the point: Binance fixed the perp's share count one day and opened a token sale the next. Both point to an IPO Binance expects soon, though it gives no date.
  • The gap between 13.1 billion and about 18 billion shares is large. Until Binance explains its valuation math, the $1.26 trillion figure is best read as Binance's estimate, not Anthropic's.
  • A 500 million USDC cap with per-user limits of up to 550,000 USDC with the badge means a small number of large accounts could take much of the sale.

What's next

  • The final token price after the window closes at 9 a.m. ET on November 18.
  • Whether Binance adjusts ANTUSDT again if Anthropic files a new share count.
  • An IPO date or Nasdaq ticker in Anthropic's own filings.
