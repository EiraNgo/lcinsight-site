Impossible Cloud Network (ICNT) is up 91% in three days and has doubled in a month

ICNT reached $0.437 early Friday, November 20, up 31% in 24 hours. Daily volume went from $2.45 million to $38.8 million in four days, led by Bitget, Bybit and Korea's Bithumb.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko showed ICNT at $0.4372 at 12:37 a.m. ET on November 20, 2026, up 30.8% in 24 hours, 59.9% in seven days and 100.7% in 30 days.
  • From a midnight UTC price of $0.2283 on November 17, the token has climbed about 91%. It is still 26% below its December 19, 2025, record of $0.5911.
  • Bitget carried 22.4% of the $38.8 million reported volume, Bybit 20.1% and Bithumb's Korean won market 14.7%. Only about 36% of the 700 million ICNT supply is in circulation.

The move

Impossible Cloud Network's token ICNT traded between $0.3363 and $0.4498 in the 24 hours to early Friday, according to CoinGecko. At 12:37 a.m. ET on November 20, 2026, it was $0.4372, up 30.8% on the day.

This is the third strong day in a row. CoinGecko's midnight UTC prices show $0.2283 on November 17, $0.2770 on November 18, $0.3337 on November 19 and $0.4253 at the start of November 20. Since its record low of $0.0809 on September 16, 2026, the token has risen about 5.4 times.

CoinGecko's project profile describes Impossible Cloud Network as a decentralized cloud for storage, compute and networking. Hardware providers add capacity, and services use the ICNT token to access it. Projects like this are often called DePIN, short for decentralized physical infrastructure networks.

Where it traded

CoinGecko's daily readings show volume rising from $2.45 million on November 16 to $13.6 million on November 17, $26.0 million on November 19 and $38.8 million in the 24 hours to early Friday. That is about 35% of ICNT's $110.6 million market value.

The ticker list, which counts 24 markets, is spread more widely than many recent Korean-led rallies. Bitget's USDT pair led with $8.69 million (22.4%), Bybit followed with $7.79 million (20.1%) and Bithumb's won market added $5.68 million (14.7%). Gate carried 7.9% and Bitvavo's euro pair 6.7%.

On-chain trading was smaller. Uniswap and PancakeSwap pools on Base, the Coinbase-built network, together handled about $3.19 million, or 8.2%. Bithumb's price was about 0.7% above Bitget's when we checked.

Supply to keep in mind

CoinGecko lists 253 million ICNT in circulation out of a fixed maximum of 700 million, about 36%. At Friday's price that gives a market value of $110.6 million and a fully diluted value, counting every token that can exist, of about $306 million. We found no official Impossible Cloud Network statement that explains the rally, and its website was out of reach from our system this morning, so we do not assign a cause.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the spread of trading across Bitget, Bybit, Bithumb and Europe's Bitvavo makes this rally look broader than a single-exchange spike, though centralized exchanges still did more than 90% of the volume.
  • Doubling in a month from a September record low leaves room for sharp pullbacks. Three straight days of gains with volume up about 16 times is the kind of move that often cools quickly.
  • With about 64% of the supply not yet circulating, the gap between the $110.6 million market value and the $306 million fully diluted value is worth watching.

What's next

  • Whether daily volume stays above $20 million.
  • Whether ICNT holds above the 24-hour low of $0.3363.
  • Any official ICN statement, listing or unlock schedule.
