Meme coin Gato The Cat jumped 39% in a day and tops CoinGecko's trending list, all in one Uniswap pool

GATO, a cat-themed Ethereum token whose CoinGecko price history starts on Saturday, November 14, rose 39% in 24 hours to Sunday, November 15, while bitcoin fell 1.8%. Every trade CoinGecko tracks ran through a single Uniswap pool.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows GATO at about $0.0156 at 9:03 a.m. ET on November 15, 2026, up 38.7% in 24 hours. It is No. 1 on CoinGecko's trending list.
  • It fell to about $0.0096 at 4:50 p.m. ET on Saturday, then climbed 81% to a record $0.0173 at 8:27 a.m. ET on Sunday. It now sits about 10% below that peak.
  • CoinGecko lists one market for GATO, a Uniswap v4 pool on Ethereum against ETH, with about $4.2 million of trading in 24 hours. Bitcoin fell 1.8% and ether 2.4% over the same day.

What moved

Gato The Cat (GATO) is a cat-themed meme token on Ethereum. CoinGecko's price history for it starts at 2 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 14, 2026, so it has little more than a day of data there. Its record low on CoinGecko, about $0.0023, came at 5:12 a.m. ET that morning.

At 9:10 a.m. ET on Saturday it traded near $0.0116. It dipped to about $0.0096 at 4:50 p.m. ET, then rose in steps overnight and through Sunday morning to a record $0.0173 at 8:27 a.m. ET, a gain of 81% from the Saturday low. That peak is about 7.5 times its record low from the day before.

At 9:03 a.m. ET on Sunday GATO stood at about $0.0156, up 38.7% in 24 hours and about 10% below the peak.

The wider market fell. CoinGecko shows bitcoin down 1.8% and ether down 2.4% over the same 24 hours.

Where it traded

CoinGecko lists a single market for GATO: a Uniswap v4 pool on Ethereum that pairs GATO with ETH. All of the roughly $4.2 million traded over the past 24 hours went through that pool; GATO has no listing on a centralized exchange there.

When a coin trades in only one pool, a handful of big buys or sells is enough to push its price sharply up or down.

The No. 2 coin on CoinGecko's trending list, Identity.md (IMD), has a similar profile: a young Ethereum meme token with 1 billion coins and one Uniswap v4 market. Nothing we found links the two projects.

Supply and value

CoinGecko counts 1 billion GATO in circulation, which is also the total and maximum supply. That puts the market value at about $15.6 million, rank No. 892 on CoinGecko, and the value of all coins at the same figure.

The project's description on CoinGecko is a story about a street cat who "bought the entire bank". It gives no details on the team, allocation, audit or plans. CoinGecko lists the website gatothecat.fun and the X account @gatothecat9.

What we could not check

We could not check who holds GATO or how the supply was first spread, so we do not know how concentrated it is. The token contract on Ethereum is 0xab54...081a87. No official post we found explains the jump, so the cause is unknown. If the team posts news, this story will be updated.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a 39% gain on a day when bitcoin and ether both lost ground points to demand for GATO in particular rather than a lift for the whole market. CoinGecko data cannot tell us who the buyers were. This is not investment advice.
  • A token with about a day of history, a record low and a record high 7.5 times apart, and one trading pool can swing hard both ways.
  • Two young one-pool Ethereum meme tokens holding the top two trending spots shows where attention on CoinGecko is going this weekend.

What's next

  • Whether GATO holds above the $0.0096 low from 4:50 p.m. ET on November 14
  • Whether a second market or an exchange listing appears on CoinGecko
  • Any statement from the team on X (@gatothecat9)
