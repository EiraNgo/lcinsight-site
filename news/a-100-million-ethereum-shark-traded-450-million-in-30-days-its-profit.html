A $100 million Ethereum shark traded $450 million in 30 days. Its profit: $26,100

Three quarters of the wallet sits in three dollar tokens. In four minutes from 12:30 a.m. ET it bought $340,000 of Ripple's RLUSD, sold $131,000 of LINK and made 18 buys of under $10 each.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • DeepSea shows an Ethereum shark wallet ending in 91cc with $100.07 million in assets, including $75.2 million in three dollar tokens: USD1, RLUSD and USDe.
  • It made 12,787 trades worth $449.88 million in 30 days, about 4.5 times its own size, for a profit of $26,100 and a 32% win rate.
  • Between 12:30 and 12:34 a.m. ET on November 8 it bought 339,604 RLUSD ($340,046) and sold 12,229 LINK ($131,245), DeepSea's feed shows. CoinGecko prices match.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's wallet page for a shark it calls Hold91cc (Ethereum, address ending in 91cc) lists $100.07 million in total assets. The five biggest holdings: USD1 $26.53 million, RLUSD $25.18 million, USDe $23.53 million, QNT $10.43 million and XAUt $9.50 million. The three dollar tokens add up to $75.24 million, about 75% of the wallet.

DeepSea's trade stats for the wallet: 12,787 trades and $449.88 million of volume in 30 days when we first read it (12,851 trades a few minutes later, in the screenshot), a 32% win rate and +$26.1K of profit over both 7 and 30 days. Its realized PnL table at 12:30 a.m. ET gave +$28,254 on 679 closed trades, 216 of them winners, all inside the last 7 days.

In DeepSea's live feed, the wallet logged 34 trades between 12:30 and 12:34 a.m. ET. The big ones: two buys of RLUSD worth $340,046, a sale of 12,228.73 LINK for $131,245, a sale of 492,415 ZBT for $30,860 and a CYBER buy of $11,937. Eighteen were buys of under $10 each, in SUPER, DEXE, OGN, SKL, TURBO and BIO.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 8, 2026, 12:35 to 12:55 a.m. ET.

Checking the numbers on CoinGecko

CoinGecko priced LINK at $10.74 at 12:43 a.m. ET, which puts 12,228.73 LINK at about $131,300, in line with DeepSea. RLUSD, Ripple's dollar token, was at $1.00, so 339,604 coins match the $340,046. ZBT was at $0.0604, making the ZBT sale about $29,700 at that price.

CoinGecko also explains the larger holdings. QNT was at $196.11, so $10.43 million is about 53,200 QNT. XAUt, Tether's gold token, was at $4,053.93, so $9.50 million is about 2,343 tokens. USD1 traded at $0.9975 and USDe at $0.9989.

A lot of trading for a small result

The wallet's $449.88 million of volume for $26,100 of profit is a return of about 0.006% on what it traded. The $10 buys in many small tokens, spread across dozens of trades in a few minutes, look automated. We do not know who runs the wallet; DeepSea gives it no name beyond Hold91cc, and we could not open Etherscan in this run to look further.

The pattern is the same as in other high-volume sharks we have covered: a big pile of dollars, constant small trades, and a result close to zero. Its large size comes from holdings, not from trading gains.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think this wallet works like a treasury in dollars and gold with a trading engine on top. No single coin drives it.
  • Selling LINK about a minute after buying RLUSD fits that: more of the wallet moved toward a dollar token in the same minutes it was buying $7 slices of small tokens.
  • Win rate alone says little here. A 32% win rate on 679 closed trades still ended slightly positive, so the few winners were larger than the many small losers.

What's next

  • Whether the RLUSD, USD1 and USDe balances keep growing
  • Whether the wallet's 30-day profit stays above zero
  • Further LINK sales from the wallet
