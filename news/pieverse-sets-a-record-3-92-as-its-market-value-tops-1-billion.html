Pieverse sets a record $3.92 as its market value tops $1 billion

PIEVERSE jumped about 10% in its last hour to a new high early Sunday, beating the $3.64 record from November 18. It is up about 103% in a week.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko recorded a new all-time high of $3.92 for PIEVERSE at 3:01 a.m. ET on November 22, 2026, about 8% above the $3.64 record it set on November 18.
  • At 3:02 a.m. ET the token was at $3.89, up about 28% in 24 hours and about 103% in seven days, with a market value of about $1.07 billion (No. 65 on CoinGecko).
  • CoinGecko lists about $82.5 million of PIEVERSE trading in 24 hours. A PancakeSwap pool on BNB Chain carried about 33%, ahead of South Korea's Upbit at about 27%.

A new high before dawn

Pieverse's token PIEVERSE hit a record $3.92 at 3:01 a.m. ET on Sunday, November 22, 2026, according to CoinGecko. The old record was $3.64, set on the evening of November 18, as we reported then.

CoinGecko's hourly prices show PIEVERSE at about $2.89 at 8 a.m. ET on Saturday, its low of the past day. It rose to $3.11 at noon and $3.37 at 2 p.m., then held near $3.40 into midnight. It reached $3.48 at 1 a.m. and $3.55 at 2 a.m. before jumping to $3.89 at 3:02 a.m. ET, about 10% in an hour. That left it up about 28% in 24 hours, about 103% in seven days and about 274% in 30 days.

The broad market also rose, but far less. Bitcoin was at $59,027, up 2.3% in 24 hours, and ether at $1,664.12, up 2.5%, per CoinGecko.

Where it traded

CoinGecko's ticker list sums to about $82.5 million of PIEVERSE trading in 24 hours, well below the $190.4 million we counted at the November 18 record. The PancakeSwap Infinity PIEVERSE/USDT pool on BNB Chain was the biggest venue with about $26.9 million (32.6%). Upbit's PIEVERSE/KRW market followed with about $22.0 million (26.7%), then Bybit at $9.0 million (11.0%), Gate at $8.3 million (10.1%) and Bithumb at $8.0 million (9.7%).

The two South Korean won markets, Upbit and Bithumb, carried about 36% of the volume, down from about 52% on November 18.

Supply to keep in mind

Of a 1 billion maximum supply, 274.75 million tokens (about 27.5%) circulate, per CoinGecko. At $3.89 that is a fully diluted value, the value if every token existed, of about $3.9 billion, against a market value of about $1.07 billion. We found no official explanation for the move in the sources we could check, and we do not assign one.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, the notable part is that this record came on less than half the volume of the November 18 run, with on-chain trading on PancakeSwap back in front of the won markets.
  • A 10% jump in one hour at a quiet time of day is a thin-market move, and such moves can reverse just as fast.
  • With 72.5% of supply not yet circulating, any unlock schedule matters. This is not investment advice.

What's next

  • Whether PIEVERSE holds above $3.64, the November 18 record
  • Whether volume picks up as US and Asian markets get busier
  • Any official statement from Pieverse on @pieverse_io
