HYPE fell 4% to $64.99, and top traders' shorts shrank $40 million since Tuesday

DeepSea's top HYPE traders are still 92% short, but with $246.63 million instead of $286.51 million on Tuesday. Their average entry, $65.41, now sits right at the price.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • HYPE traded at $64.99 at 12:35 a.m. ET on Friday, down 4.3% in 24 hours and 8.5% in a week, CoinGecko shows.
  • DeepSea's HYPE-PERP page counts 61 top Hyperliquid traders 92% short with $246.63 million, down from $286.51 million across 63 wallets on Tuesday.
  • The biggest short, entered at $28.79, is still $15.68 million down on paper, about $2.2 million less than on Tuesday.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's HYPE-PERP page follows the HYPE futures of top traders on Hyperliquid, the exchange behind the token. At 12:39 a.m. ET on Friday it counted 61 tracked wallets, 92% short (betting on a fall) with $246.63 million at an average entry of $65.41, against $21.77 million long at an average of $72.07.

When we last wrote about this board, at 12:59 a.m. ET on Tuesday, it showed 63 wallets with $286.51 million short at an average of $66.07 and $26.81 million long. The short side is about $40 million smaller. Part of that is the price itself: Tuesday's page put HYPE at $70.00, and a lower price shrinks the dollar size of the same bet. At Friday's price, Tuesday's shorts would have been worth about $267 million, so roughly half of the drop came from closed or smaller positions.

The page put HYPE at $65.14, down 3.97% in a day, with $1.22 billion of open HYPE futures on Hyperliquid. The three largest positions:

  • Smart trader #7: $28.15 million short at 10x, entry $28.79, paper loss $15.68 million
  • Smart trader #61: $19.80 million short at 10x, entry $84.02, paper gain $5.83 million
  • Smart trader #170: $16.87 million short at 5x, entry $72.67, paper gain $1.99 million

The rank numbers on this page change. The $28.79 short was listed as Smart trader #4 on Tuesday, with $30.36 million and a $17.89 million paper loss.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 20, 2026, 12:39 a.m. ET.

The price

HYPE fell to a 24-hour low of $64.76, according to CoinGecko. At 12:35 a.m. ET on Friday it traded at $64.99, down 4.3% in a day and 8.5% in a week, and about 34% below its September 23 record of $97.96.

A short is a bet that the price will fall, made here with perpetual futures, contracts with no end date. Leverage, such as 10x, lets a trader bet ten times the money put up.

Why the average matters

The short side's average entry, $65.41, is almost exactly where HYPE trades. So as a group the shorts are close to flat, even after a 4% drop. A few very early bets, opened far below today's price, explain it. Besides the $28.79 short, Smart trader #15 holds $6.50 million from $32.46 (paper loss $3.25 million), Smart trader #159 $5.91 million from $31.12 ($3.08 million down) and Smart trader #209 $5.92 million from $40.50 ($2.23 million down).

The board moves by the minute. In our screenshot at 1:03 a.m. ET, after a small uptick, the short side read $250.06 million, still 92%.

Shorts opened above $70 are in profit. The few longs are not: the long side's average entry of $72.07 is about 11% above Friday's price.

The wider market

CoinGecko's Hyperliquid data at 12:58 a.m. ET put open bets on the HYPE-USD contract at $613 million, counted one side only, about half of DeepSea's two-sided $1.22 billion. On CoinGecko's derivatives list, funding was positive on all three large HYPE contracts it tracks (Hyperliquid, Binance and Gate).

DeepSea's page does not say which shorts were closed or cut since Tuesday, and we could not open Hyperliquid's own data feed this morning to check.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think about half of the $40 million drop on the short side reflects the lower price. The rest points to some shorts taking profit or cutting size. The three largest shorts look unchanged once the price is taken into account.
  • With the average short entry at the price, the group has little cushion. A bounce of a few percent would put the shorts as a whole in the red.
  • The early $28.79 short remains the board's biggest single risk. It is far from its liquidation price, above $150, but it was already carrying a large paper loss on Tuesday.

What's next

  • Whether HYPE holds above Friday's $64.76 low.
  • Whether the short total on DeepSea's HYPE board keeps shrinking.
  • Whether funding on Hyperliquid's HYPE contract turns negative.
