Smart money trader SMad53 traded $47.6 billion in 30 days. Its open bets total $3.06 million

The No. 6 wallet on DeepSea's 7-day board made $28.57 million in a month after a flat 90 days. It holds 30 small bets, all at 3x, and one bitcoin short makes up 90% of the total.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • SMad53 ranks No. 6 on DeepSea's 7-day smart money board: +$9.59 million in 7 days, +$28.57 million in 30 days, $107.42 million in listed assets.
  • Its 90-day result is only +$171,300, so the month's gain followed a loss of about $28.4 million in the two months before.
  • Its 30 open positions total $3.06 million, all at 3x leverage. A $2.75 million bitcoin short is 90% of that.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's smart money board orders traders by what they made in the past week. At 12:38 a.m. ET, SMad53 was No. 6, a futures trader on Hyperliquid (bets on prices with borrowed money). DeepSea does not say who runs it. It last appeared in our November 9 story, among the board's top four.

Its DeepSea page shows $107.42 million in total assets and $47.58 billion of trading volume in 30 days, about $1.59 billion a day. Its profit was +$9.59 million in 7 days, +$28.57 million in 30 days, +$171,300 in 90 days and +$258,100 in 180 days. Spot holdings are $2,522 of USDC. The page does not break down where the rest of the $107.42 million sits.

Its open positions are small for an account this size. DeepSea lists 30, with 16 shorts (bets on a fall) and 14 longs, all at 3x:

  • Bitcoin short: $2.75 million, 42.55 BTC, entry $64,649.80, paper loss $1,453
  • Ether short: $117,568, entry $1,815.28, paper gain $207
  • INIT long: $102,391, paper loss $1,069
  • Twenty-seven more, each under $14,000

All 30 together show a paper loss of about $2,248.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 18, 2026, 12:45 a.m. ET.

How the numbers check out

Data from DeepSea, Arbiscan and CoinGecko. CoinGecko showed bitcoin at $64,598 at 12:51 a.m. ET. DeepSea's value for the short, $2.75 million for 42.55 BTC, implies a price near $64,684, within 0.2% of CoinGecko. At either price, bitcoin sits close to the $64,649.80 entry, which is why the paper result is tiny.

Hyperliquid deposits arrive through Arbitrum. Arbiscan shows the same address holding just 2,322.62 USDC on Arbitrum, so the account's money is not parked in that wallet.

What leverage and volume mean

Leverage of 3x means a position is three times the money put up for it, so a 1% price move changes the stake by about 3%. Volume counts every trade both ways, so a wallet that buys and sells all day can post huge volume with modest positions. Paper gains and losses are not locked in until a position closes. DeepSea calls a wallet smart money because of past profits; that label says nothing about the next trade.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think this wallet trades like a high-frequency account: billions in monthly volume and many small positions at one leverage level.
  • The 30-day and 90-day figures tell different stories. A $28.57 million month that only brings the 90-day result to +$171,300 means the gain mostly won back earlier losses.
  • These figures describe one account's record so far and do not predict its next move.

What's next

  • Whether SMad53 keeps its top-10 place as the 7-day window rolls
  • Whether DeepSea shows where the $107.42 million in assets sits
