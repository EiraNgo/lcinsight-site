Portal triples in a day as $266 million trades hands, more than 7 times its market value

PORTAL, the token of the Web3 gaming platform Portal, jumped from about 1.2 cents to about 4.1 cents on Friday, its highest price in at least 90 days. Binance handled more than half of the trading. Bitcoin and ether fell over the same 24 hours.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows Portal (PORTAL) at about $0.0409 at 7:02 p.m. ET on November 27, 2026, up 227% in 24 hours. It had sat near $0.0124 for more than a week.
  • About $266.3 million of PORTAL traded in 24 hours, about 7.3 times its market value of $36.4 million. Binance's USDT and Turkish lira pairs carried about $150.6 million of it.
  • Even after the jump, PORTAL is 98.8% below its record of $3.36 from February 29, 2024. Over that day bitcoin lost 1.1% and ether 1.6%.

What moved

Portal (PORTAL) is the token of Portal, a gaming platform that says it wants to bring "the first billion gamers into Web3", according to its CoinGecko listing. The token trades on Ethereum, Base and Solana. From November 17 to November 26 its daily price on CoinGecko stayed between about $0.0115 and $0.0125.

The move started before dawn in New York on Friday, November 27. PORTAL was at about $0.0124 at 4 a.m. ET and about $0.0174 at 5 a.m. ET, then about $0.0242 at 7 a.m. ET. The biggest leg came around midday: from about $0.0273 at 11 a.m. ET to about $0.0386 at noon ET. It kept edging up through the afternoon and reached about $0.0409 at 7:02 p.m. ET, up 227% in 24 hours. CoinGecko's 24-hour range runs from $0.0123 to $0.0425.

The rest of the market went the other way. Bitcoin fell 1.1% and ether 1.6% in that window, which makes this a PORTAL story rather than a market story. At 7:03 p.m. ET Portal was No. 6 on CoinGecko's trending list.

Volume far above market value

The trading was huge for a token this size. CoinGecko counts about $266.3 million of PORTAL traded in 24 hours. Its market value is about $36.4 million, rank No. 489, so the day's volume was about 7.3 times the value of every PORTAL in circulation. Hourly data show 24-hour volume near $2 million to $2.5 million before 5 a.m. ET, so almost all of it came with the jump.

Binance was the center of it. Its PORTAL/USDT pair handled about $90.6 million and its PORTAL/TRY pair, priced in Turkish lira, about $60.0 million, together about 57% of the total. BTCC followed with about $45.8 million, Bitvavo's euro pair with about $15.6 million and Bybit with about $8.8 million.

The bigger picture

A tripling in a day still leaves PORTAL far from its peak. CoinGecko's record high is $3.36, set on February 29, 2024. At about $0.0409 the token is 98.8% below that record. Its record low was about $0.00735 on May 29, 2026, and the price is now about 5.6 times that level.

About 891.0 million of the 1 billion PORTAL that can ever exist are already circulating, per CoinGecko, so about 11% of the supply is not yet in circulation.

What we could not check

No official announcement we could find accounts for the rally, so the reason remains open. Market data alone does not reveal the buyers or whether a handful of large accounts produced the volume. CoinGecko lists the website portalgaming.com and the X account @Portalcoin. The Ethereum contract is 0x1bbe...1fed. We will update this story if the team or an exchange explains the move.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a 227% gain on a day when bitcoin and ether both slipped says buyers were chasing this one token, not riding a broad rebound. This is not investment advice.
  • Volume of more than 7 times market value in a single day means the same tokens changed hands many times. That kind of churn is common in sharp rallies of small tokens and can reverse just as fast.
  • Binance carried more than half the trading, and its lira pair alone was the second biggest market, so what happens on Binance matters most for where PORTAL goes next.

What's next

  • Whether PORTAL holds above $0.0386, the level it reached at the midday jump
  • Whether 24-hour volume falls back toward a few million dollars
  • A post from the Portal team (@Portalcoin on X) or from Binance explaining the move
