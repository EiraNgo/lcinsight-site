Rumor vs record: Solana 1.0 is real; Zelle's $1.25 million stage transfer is unconfirmed

Solana's London conference set off a wave of posts: Solana 1.0, 150-millisecond finality, Zelle picking Solana, a $1.25 million on-stage transfer and NYSE's owner getting ready for Solana tokens. We checked six claims against official pages and CoinGecko. Three hold up, one is misleading and two cannot be confirmed.

LC Insight Editorial Team4 min read

Key points

  • The Solana Foundation said on November 16 that Solana is now "mainnet" instead of "mainnet beta" and has a shared protocol version, 1.0. It credits Alpenglow with finality of about 150 milliseconds.
  • CoinGecko lists ZelleUSD (ZLUSD) as a Solana token issued for Zelle by Anchorage Digital Bank. It shows no price and a supply of 147,651 tokens, far below the $1.25 million said to have moved on stage.
  • The only ICE release on NYSE's token platform that we could open, from January 19, 2026, names no blockchain. DTCC's Solana link is planned for the second half of 2027 and is not live.

Rumor vs record

What is going aroundVerdictWhat the record shows
"Solana is officially out of beta. Solana 1.0 is here."TrueThe Solana Foundation's November 16 post says Solana 1.0 marks the network's move from "mainnet beta" to "mainnet." It also introduces one protocol version number that is separate from the version numbers of the validator programs Agave and Firedancer. The post adds that announcing a version does not switch on every related improvement at once. Source
"Alpenglow is live: Solana transactions now settle in 150 milliseconds."TrueThe same post calls Alpenglow's mainnet activation a recent milestone and says it enables finality of about 150 milliseconds. Finality is the point where the network treats a payment as settled. The post gives no activation date, and it says "about" 150 ms, not a fixed time for every transaction. Source
"Zelle picked Solana for its ZelleUSD stablecoin."TrueCoinGecko lists ZelleUSD (ZLUSD) with a Solana token address and describes it as a dollar token issued for Zelle by Anchorage Digital Bank, a national bank regulated by the OCC, "deployed on the Solana blockchain." Zelle's own June 11, 2026, release named no blockchain and said its first overseas service, to India, was expected before the end of 2026. We could not open a newer Zelle release. Source
"Zelle just sent $1.25 million in ZelleUSD on Solana, live on stage."UnverifiedWe found no official Zelle, Anchorage or Solana Foundation page we could open that records this transfer or gives a transaction ID. At 6:45 a.m. ET on November 18, CoinGecko showed ZLUSD as a preview listing with no price and a total supply of 147,651.11 tokens. If ZLUSD tracks $1, that is under $148,000 in all. A $1.25 million mainnet transfer cannot be squared with that supply unless tokens were created and then destroyed, which we could not check. Source
"NYSE's parent ICE is ready to accept Solana tokens and may run a Solana validator."UnverifiedThe only ICE release on the topic we could open, from January 19, 2026, says NYSE is building a tokenized securities platform that will support "multiple chains," names no chain, and says the venue needs regulatory approval. Comments made on stage in London were not posted on any ICE page we could open. A validator is a computer that helps confirm Solana transactions. Source
"Wall Street's DTCC is putting stocks on Solana."MisleadingThe Solana Foundation's November 12 post says DTCC plans to let eligible assets held at its depository be turned into tokens on Solana through its Tokenization Service. The link is set for the second half of 2027. Nothing is live today. Source

Why we did this check

Solana's Breakpoint conference at Olympia London, held November 16 and 17, produced a burst of big headlines. Many posts mixed confirmed news with stage remarks and older plans, often in one line. We split six of the most shared claims and checked each against the Solana Foundation's own posts, Zelle's and ICE's releases, and CoinGecko. We did not use news articles as evidence. New to dollar tokens? Read our stablecoin guide.

What is confirmed

The Solana Foundation's November 16 post confirms the "beta" label is gone and that Solana now has a single protocol version, 1.0. It credits Alpenglow, a new way for validators to agree on blocks, with finality of about 150 milliseconds. The post does not give an activation date.

For ZelleUSD, CoinGecko lists a Solana token address and names Anchorage Digital Bank as issuer for Zelle. Zelle's June 11 release introduced the token without naming a chain.

What does not add up yet

The $1.25 million figure is the weak point. CoinGecko shows ZLUSD as a preview listing, with no price and a total supply of 147,651.11 tokens at 6:45 a.m. ET on November 18. We could not open a Solana block explorer from our system, and no official page gives a transaction ID for the stage demo.

ICE's January release does not mention Solana, and DTCC's Solana link is planned for the second half of 2027.

How SOL is trading

SOL traded at $74.41 at 6:45 a.m. ET on Wednesday, up 1.6% in 24 hours but down 10.7% over seven days, according to CoinGecko. Bitcoin was down 12.8% over the same week.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the Solana 1.0 news is mostly a label change on top of upgrades that were already rolling out. That matters for how outsiders see the network, but it is not a new feature on its own.
  • The Zelle story is real at the token level, but stage demos are not the same as live payments. Until Zelle or Anchorage posts a transaction or a launch date, the $1.25 million transfer should be read as a demo, not usage.
  • Conference weeks bundle old plans with new ones. The DTCC and ICE items are plans with dates in 2027 or no date at all.

What's next

  • A Zelle or Anchorage post with a ZLUSD launch date or a transaction ID for the demo
  • Changes in ZLUSD supply on CoinGecko
  • Any ICE filing or release that names Solana
