Six sharks netted $1.07 million of Venice's VVV in a day as the AI token slid 2.8%

Six large wallets tracked by DeepSea bought $2.73 million of the private-AI token on Base and sold $1.66 million, while VVV fell 2.8% and bitcoin fell 2.3%.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Six shark wallets bought $2,731,266 of VVV on Base and sold $1,663,997 in 86 trades, in DeepSea's 24-hour table built at 6:35 a.m. ET on November 25, 2026.
  • That is a net $1.07 million bought. VVV was $24.86 at 6:37 a.m. ET on CoinGecko, down 2.8% in 24 hours but up 15.6% in seven days.
  • One shark that bought VVV in the latest hour holds 435,036 VVV, worth about $10.8 million, according to BaseScan.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's 24-hour flow table, built at 6:35 a.m. ET on November 25, has a VVV line on Base: 6 shark wallets, $2,731,266 bought and $1,663,997 sold, in 86 trades. The token address in the table matches the VVV contract on BaseScan. Sharks are wallets DeepSea tags for their large trading size.

In the most recent hour, the table shows one shark buying $6,772 of VVV in two trades and none selling. That wallet's own hourly line shows the same $6,772. The 24-hour table does not split the VVV total by wallet.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 25, 2026, 6:35 a.m. ET.

Sharks bought more than they sold

VVV is the token of Venice, a company that offers private AI chat, image and code tools, according to its website. The token lives on Base, the Ethereum layer 2 network built by Coinbase.

The six sharks traded $4.40 million of VVV in both directions. They ended about $1.07 million net buyers. For scale, CoinGecko counted $45.7 million of VVV traded across exchanges in the same 24 hours, so the sharks' swaps equal about a tenth of it. The two figures are measured differently: DeepSea counts on-chain swaps by tagged wallets, while CoinGecko counts mostly exchange trades.

The shark we checked on BaseScan

We checked the shark that bought in the latest hour on BaseScan, the Base block explorer. It held 435,036 VVV, which BaseScan valued at about $10.83 million, or 0.38% of the supply BaseScan lists. BaseScan shows no name tag for the wallet, and we do not know who controls it or why it traded.

Across all tokens, DeepSea's wallet table shows this shark bought $962,846 and sold $447,666 in 32 trades over 24 hours. BaseScan's token page counted 160,104 VVV holders at 6:38 a.m. ET.

The price slipped with the market

The net buying did not lift the price. VVV traded at $26.03 at 7 a.m. ET on November 24 and slid to about $24.21 by 3 p.m. ET, according to CoinGecko's hourly prices. At 6:37 a.m. ET on November 25 it was $24.86, down 2.8% in 24 hours. Bitcoin lost 2.3% over the same day, to $58,467.

VVV is still up 15.6% over seven days and is 28% below its record of $34.53 from September 21, 2026. Its market value is about $1.22 billion. On Hyperliquid, an on-chain futures exchange, the VVV-USD perpetual held about $30.8 million of open bets, CoinGecko's Hyperliquid listing shows.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the two-way trading matters as much as the net figure. Six wallets swapped $4.4 million for a net purchase of about $1.07 million, which looks like active trading with a buying tilt rather than one big entry.
  • VVV held up better than bitcoin over the week, and the shark tilt fits that pattern. It did not stop a 2.8% daily drop, so we read it as a sign of interest, not a call on direction.
  • One wallet holding 435,036 VVV, about $10.8 million, can move this market on its own. We think its BaseScan balance says more about the next few days than a single day of net flows.

What's next

  • Whether the VVV line in DeepSea's table stays net bought in the next run.
  • The 435,036 VVV balance of the shark checked on BaseScan.
  • Whether VVV holds above $24.21, its low on November 24.
