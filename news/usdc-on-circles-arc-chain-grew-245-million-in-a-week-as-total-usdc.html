USDC on Circle's Arc chain grew $245 million in a week as total USDC shrank $1.1 billion

DefiLlama data put USDC on Arc at $1.60 billion, up 18% in a week and 3.5 times its September low. It is level with Polygon, a hair behind in seventh place among USDC chains.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • USDC on Arc reached $1.60 billion in DefiLlama's latest reading, up $245 million, or 18.1%, from the November 12 reading, while total USDC fell $1.10 billion to $69.06 billion.
  • It is 3.5 times the $452 million low of September 28 and 2.5 times the first reading on September 17.
  • DefiLlama counts $607 million locked in apps on Arc, 12th among all chains.

What moved

Arc is the blockchain built by Circle, the company that issues the USDC stablecoin. DefiLlama's USDC history for Arc, read at about 12:42 a.m. ET on November 19, 2026, showed $1.60 billion in the latest reading. The November 12 reading was $1.35 billion, so $245 million was added in a week.

The series starts on September 17 at $649 million. It slid to $452 million by September 28, then climbed. It passed $1 billion on October 28 and has risen in 13 of the last 15 readings.

Against the rest of USDC

The rise runs against the total. DefiLlama's stablecoin table, read at about 12:37 a.m. ET, showed all USDC at $69.06 billion, down from $70.16 billion a week earlier. In that table Arc and Polygon each held about $1.60 billion, behind Ethereum, Solana, Hyperliquid L1, Base and Arbitrum. Polygon was ahead by less than $1 million, which put Arc seventh.

Against the table's previous-day figures, Arc added $66 million. Over the same window USDC on Solana fell $1.02 billion, the largest drop of any chain.

Coins held versus coins used

Holding USDC on a chain is not the same as putting it to work there. DefiLlama's chain list, read at about 12:41 a.m. ET, counted $607 million locked in apps on Arc, ranking it 12th, just behind Polygon at $610 million. That is a bit over a third of the USDC on Arc. The data do not show who holds the rest.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think Arc is gaining share of a shrinking USDC supply. That is a different signal from new money coming into stablecoins.
  • Only about 38% of Arc's USDC is counted inside apps, so much of the growth may be balances parked by a small number of large holders.
  • If Arc keeps rising while Solana falls, the two lines in DefiLlama's data will be the clearest place to watch where USDC moves next.

What's next

  • USDC on Arc against $1.60 billion and Polygon's $1.60 billion.
  • Total USDC against $69.06 billion.
  • Arc's app deposits against $607 million.
