DeFi lenders supplied $12.7 billion in a week. Morpho Blue had 84% of liquidations

DeepSea's lending data for October 26 to November 1 shows deposits far ahead of withdrawals, borrowing almost exactly matched by repayments, and $12.69 million liquidated, most of it on Morpho Blue.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Wallets supplied $12.70 billion to EVM lending protocols and withdrew $8.98 billion from October 26 to November 1, 2026, DeepSea shows.
  • Borrowing ($3.24 billion) and repayments ($3.25 billion) nearly canceled out. Liquidations totaled $12.69 million, $10.61 million of it on Morpho Blue.
  • Net supply rose $2.06 billion on Ethereum and $1.65 billion on Base. Aave v3 saw more withdrawn ($7.00 billion) than supplied ($6.55 billion).

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's weekly report for October 26 to November 1, 2026 tracks lending protocols on EVM chains (Ethereum and networks built like it). Wallets supplied $12.70 billion and withdrew $8.98 billion, a net supply of plus $3.72 billion. They borrowed $3.24 billion and repaid $3.25 billion, so net borrowing was minus $4.54 million.

$12.69 million was liquidated. A liquidation happens when a borrower's collateral falls too far in value and the protocol sells it to repay the loan.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 3, 2026, 6:30 p.m. ET.

Protocol by protocol

Aave v3, the largest, had $6.55 billion supplied and $7.00 billion withdrawn, $1.39 billion borrowed and $1.44 billion repaid, with $1.73 million liquidated. Morpho Blue had $3.97 billion supplied, $770.44 million borrowed, $768.96 million repaid and $10.61 million liquidated, about 84% of the week's total. Spark had $2.13 billion supplied and $1.93 billion withdrawn, with just $27 liquidated, DeepSea shows.

A caveat on Morpho

DeepSea's table shows $0 withdrawn from Morpho Blue all week (Venus also shows $0). DeepSea does not explain this. If you add up the protocol rows, Morpho's $3.97 billion is more than the whole plus $3.72 billion net supply, because Aave v3 was net minus $450 million. So the headline net figure leans on that one row, and readers should treat it with care.

Where the money landed

By chain, net supply was +$2.06 billion on Ethereum and +$1.65 billion on Base, Coinbase's layer 2 network. BSC added $7.33 million and Arbitrum $1.77 million, while Polygon lost $1.17 million.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the near-perfect match of $3.24 billion borrowed and $3.25 billion repaid points to a calm week for credit: net borrowing barely moved.
  • Liquidations of $12.69 million are small next to billions supplied, but 84% of them happened on Morpho Blue. DeepSea's data does not say which Morpho markets were hit or why.
  • Base's +$1.65 billion is large for a layer 2 and worth tracking, though without a withdrawal figure for Morpho we cannot say how much of it will stay.

What's next

  • Whether DeepSea's next report shows Morpho Blue withdrawals
  • Aave v3 net supply after a minus $450 million week
  • Base net supply versus +$1.65 billion
  • Liquidation totals if markets turn volatile
