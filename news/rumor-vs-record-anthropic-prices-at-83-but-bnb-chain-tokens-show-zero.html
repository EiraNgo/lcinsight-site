Rumor vs record: Anthropic prices at $83, but BNB Chain tokens show zero supply pre-open

Anthropic sold 980 million shares at $83, above its range, and starts trading on Nasdaq today as ANT. A viral post says tokenized ANT goes live on BNB Chain on day one. We checked five claims: two hold up, two are unverified and one is misleading.

LC Insight Editorial Team4 min read

Key points

  • Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share on November 18 for 980 million Class A shares, above the $70 to $78 range and the 825 million shares in its November 17 filing. Nasdaq trading under ANT is expected to start November 19.
  • CoinGecko lists BNB Chain contracts for Ondo's "Anthropic (Ondo Tokenized Stock)" (ANTON) and xStocks' Anthropic xStock (ANTX). At 6:48 a.m. ET both were preview listings with a circulating supply of 0 and no price.
  • We found no record of a "bStocks" Anthropic token, and no data that ties this week's stablecoin and ETF outflows to the IPO.

"Anthropic priced above its range and lists on Nasdaq today as ANT."TrueAnthropic's November 18 release says it priced 980,000,000 Class A shares at $83.00 each, with trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under "ANT" expected to begin November 19 and the deal expected to close November 20. Its amended S-1 of November 17 had offered 825,000,000 shares at a range of $70.00 to $78.00. At $83, the 980 million shares come to about $81.3 billion before fees, by our math. Source
"Tokenized ANT will be live on [BNB Chain] on Day 1 of its IPO, via bStocks, Ondo, and xStocks."UnverifiedThe contracts exist, but nothing was live when we checked. CoinGecko lists a BNB Chain address for Ondo's ANTON and for xStocks' ANTX, which uses the same address on nine EVM networks plus Solana and TON. At 6:48 a.m. ET on November 19, before Nasdaq's 9:30 a.m. open, both showed a circulating supply of 0 and no price. bStocks is a real tokenized-stock brand on BNB Chain, but we found no bStocks page naming an Anthropic token, so that part cannot be confirmed. The whole stablecoin category, about $282.4 billion, was down 0.3% in 24 hours. U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs lost a revised $306.2 million on Wednesday, CoinMarketCap data show, but their outflows started weeks before pricing. The only crypto pool clearly tied to the IPO that we found is Binance Wallet's sale, capped at 500 million USDC, which we covered on November 18. Source

Why we did this check

On November 18, the day Anthropic priced its IPO, a post from Binance founder Changpeng Zhao said tokenized ANT will be live on BNB Chain on the first day, through three issuers. Other posts claimed the IPO was pulling money out of crypto. We last checked Anthropic token claims on November 12, when no IPO date or price existed. Here is what is new, checked against Anthropic's own release, its SEC filing and CoinGecko's token records.

What is confirmed

The IPO itself is on the record. Anthropic sold 956,351,354 shares and selling stockholders 23,648,646 more, for 980 million in all at $83. Underwriters have a 30-day option to buy up to 147 million more. The price is $5 above the top of the range Anthropic filed one day earlier, and the share count is 155 million higher.

Token contracts are also on record. CoinGecko lists ANTON from Ondo with addresses on Ethereum, BNB Chain and Solana, and ANTX from xStocks on 11 networks, BNB Chain among them. Both are marked as preview listings.

What does not add up yet

"Live" is the weak word in the viral post. A token can have a contract address and still have nothing minted. Before Nasdaq opened, both BNB Chain listings showed zero tokens in circulation and no trading price.

We also found no issuer page for a "bStocks" Anthropic token. Until one exists, that part of the claim is unconfirmed. The tokens also limit who can hold them. Ondo's description says its stocks are for non-U.S. users.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the first real test comes after 9:30 a.m. ET: whether ANTON and ANTX supplies rise from zero and whether their prices track the Nasdaq share closely.
  • Pricing $5 above the range and selling 155 million more shares points to heavy demand for the stock itself. That demand is in dollars on Nasdaq; the crypto data we checked show only small stablecoin moves.
  • The viral post blurred a plan into a fact. Ondo's day-one plan is on record, but a token with zero supply is not yet something anyone can trade.

What's next

  • Circulating supply and price on CoinGecko for ANTON, ANTX and Robinhood's ANT after the Nasdaq open.
  • Any official page from a bStocks issuer.
  • Whether underwriters use the option for 147 million more shares.
