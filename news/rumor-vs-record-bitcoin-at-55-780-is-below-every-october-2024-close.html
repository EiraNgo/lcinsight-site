Rumor vs record: bitcoin at $55,780 is below every October 2024 close, back to September

As bitcoin slid below $56,000 early Saturday, posts and headlines said it hit its lowest since October 2024, that crypto lost $200 billion and that billions in bets were wiped out. We checked six claims against price, market and exchange data.

LC Insight Editorial Team4 min read

Key points

  • Bitcoin traded at $55,780 at 12:37 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 21, 2026, CoinGecko shows. Every daily close in October 2024 was higher; the last lower close was $54,842 on September 8, 2024, in CoinMarketCap's history.
  • The crypto market fell 9.9% in 24 hours to $1.97 trillion, about $216 billion, CoinGecko's global data show.
  • The claim of billions wiped out in liquidations could not be checked on primary data. Open bets on Hyperliquid's crypto markets fell about $480 million since Friday morning.

Why we did this check

Bitcoin fell 3.2% in one hour late Friday and traded at $55,780 at 12:37 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 21, 2026, down 9.4% in 24 hours, according to CoinGecko. Posts and headlines spread quickly, most with a date or a round number attached.

We checked six of the most repeated claims against CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap price data and Hyperliquid's exchange figures. We do not name the people who posted them. The table above gives each verdict and the record behind it.

How far back the price goes

The "lowest since October 2024" line undersells the drop. In CoinMarketCap's daily history, bitcoin never closed below $60,274 in October 2024. To find a lower close than Saturday's price you have to go back to September 8, 2024, when bitcoin ended the day at $54,842.

What the market data show

CoinGecko's global figures put the market at $1.97 trillion, down about $216 billion in a day. Bitcoin's share was 57.2%. Almost every large coin fell together: BNB 9.8%, XRP 9.5%, Solana 9.3% and HYPE 12.7%, in CoinGecko's market list.

The fund side was weak too. U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs lost $126.8 million on Friday and $1.30 billion over the week, CoinMarketCap's flow series shows.

What we could not check

Liquidation totals, the forced closing of bets that run out of collateral, are usually quoted from private data firms that gather exchange feeds. We use only exchange, on-chain and price data, and the exchange data we opened do not break out liquidations. So the "billions liquidated" figure stays unverified. None of the data we checked shows a cause for the drop, and we do not offer one.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the date in the viral line matters. Saying "since October 2024" makes the fall sound smaller than it is. Bitcoin is back below every daily close since early September 2024.
  • The $216 billion one-day loss is real on CoinGecko's figures, but market value is price times supply, so no one took $216 billion out in cash.
  • Big liquidation numbers often travel faster than the data behind them. Until an exchange publishes its own figure, we would treat them as estimates.

What's next

  • Whether bitcoin closes a day below $54,842, its September 8, 2024 close.
  • Monday's ETF flows and any revision to Friday's first reading.
  • Hyperliquid open interest over the weekend.
