Initia's INIT falls 71% from its day high to $0.113, below where its rally began

INIT touched $0.392 in the past 24 hours, then slid to about $0.113 by 10:05 p.m. ET on November 11. That is lower than its price before the November 9 surge, with traders swapping three times its market value in a day.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko showed INIT at about $0.113 at 10:05 p.m. ET on November 11, 2026, down 58% in 24 hours and 71% below the 24-hour high of $0.392.
  • The price is now under the roughly $0.123 to $0.130 where INIT traded on the morning of November 9, before a two-day rally.
  • Volume was about $78 million in 24 hours, about 3.2 times INIT's market value of $24.8 million. Binance's USDT and Turkish lira pairs carried about 47% of it.

The drop

Initia's token INIT traded at about $0.113 at 10:05 p.m. ET on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, according to CoinGecko. That is down 58% in 24 hours and 71% below the 24-hour high of $0.392. INIT was the number three trending coin on CoinGecko late Wednesday.

The fall wiped out a two-day run. On the morning of Monday, November 9, INIT traded between about $0.123 and $0.130. By early Tuesday it was above $0.21, a move we covered in our earlier story. New to these numbers? Our guide on market cap versus FDV explains them.

Where it traded

CoinGecko lists 32 INIT markets with about $89.5 million of combined 24-hour volume. Binance's INIT/USDT pair handled about $22.1 million (25%) and its INIT/TRY pair, priced in Turkish lira, about $19.7 million (22%). BTCC followed with $11.0 million (12%). HTX's last INIT/USDT price was about $0.079, roughly 30% under the other big venues near $0.113, a gap that suggests that market is not trading in line with the rest.

Supply and value

CoinGecko lists about 219 million INIT in circulation out of a total supply of 1 billion, about 22%. At Wednesday night's price the market value is about $24.8 million and the fully diluted value about $113 million. INIT is down 21% over seven days and about 92% below its record of $1.42 from May 13, 2025. We found no official explanation from Initia for the swing in the sources we checked, so we do not assign a cause.

  • We think the volume figure tells the story. When a token trades more than three times its market value in a day, a price can rise and fall this far in hours, and the round trip below the starting point shows how little of the rally was held.
