BlackRock's BUIDL shrank 24% in a month and now ranks fourth. Circle's USYC lost 15%

The two big institutional Treasury tokens lost roughly $900 million between them in 30 days, rwa.xyz data show, while Ondo's USDY grew.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • BlackRock's BUIDL fund token was worth $1.65 billion on rwa.xyz on November 16, 2026, down 24.4% from 30 days earlier, or about $530 million.
  • Circle's USYC was worth $2.02 billion as of November 15, down 15.5% in 30 days, or about $370 million. About 98% of its tokens sit on BNB Chain.
  • Ondo's USDY, now the largest at $2.40 billion, rose about 1.3% over the same month on CoinGecko. BUIDL now ranks fourth, behind Franklin Templeton's iBENJI.

What moved

Tokenized Treasury funds are money-market-style funds whose shares live on blockchains. Big holders use them to park cash on-chain and earn interest.

On rwa.xyz, BlackRock's USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund (BUIDL), issued through Securitize, had a total value of $1.654 billion, down 24.4% from 30 days earlier. That implies about $2.19 billion a month ago, so roughly $530 million left. The fund has only 102 holding addresses, and 204 transfers moved $894.9 million in 30 days.

Circle's USYC was worth $2.02 billion as of November 15, down 15.5% in 30 days, about $370 million. It has 29 holders and only six were active in the past month.

Where the tokens sit

The two funds live on different chains. USYC is almost a BNB Chain product: 1.732 billion of its 1.763 billion tokens are there, with 31.2 million on Ethereum. BUIDL is spread out: 483.8 million on Solana, 356.5 million on Avalanche, 277.6 million on BNB Chain and about 409.3 million across two Ethereum listings, with smaller amounts on Aptos, Optimism, Tempo, Arbitrum and Polygon.

BUIDL also sits behind other tokens. rwa.xyz says part of the reserve of Ethena's USDtb is held in BUIDL, and USDtb shrank 32.7% over the same 30 days.

Not everyone shrank

CoinGecko put Ondo's USDY at $2.387 billion at 6:51 a.m. ET on Monday, up from $2.356 billion in its October 18 reading. On the rwa.xyz league table, Franklin Templeton's iBENJI, at $1.715 billion, now sits above BUIDL. All stablecoins together fell 5.0% in 30 days to $277.6 billion, rwa.xyz shows, so BUIDL's drop was about five times the market's.

The data show totals only. They do not name which holders redeemed, and we found no statement from BlackRock, Securitize or Circle on the moves. Both funds kept their share prices. These are cash-like products, so their value falls only when holders take money out.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think a few large holders explain most of this. BUIDL has 102 addresses and USYC has 29, so one exit can move the total.
  • USYC's heavy BNB Chain footprint suggests it is used mostly as exchange collateral. If so, its shrinkage tracks trading desks cutting risk in a falling market.
  • Ondo's USDY growing while the institutional funds shrank hints that retail-style yield tokens and big-desk cash tools are moving on different cycles.

What's next

  • Whether BUIDL drops below $1.5 billion or moves back above iBENJI.
  • USYC's BNB Chain balance, the clearest sign of collateral demand.
  • Fund statements from BlackRock, Securitize or Circle on the outflows.
