Pearl climbed 53% in six days to $1.84 as bitcoin fell. Only 18% of its coins are out

The coin of Pearl, a blockchain that pays GPU miners for AI math, is back on CoinGecko's trending list. Its market value rose by about $184 million in a week, but all 2.1 billion coins would be worth $3.86 billion.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows PEARL at about $1.84 at 12:44 a.m. ET on November 18, 2026, up 8.6% in 24 hours. It ranks 85th by market value, at about $704 million.
  • The climb from the week's low of about $1.20 at 10 a.m. ET on November 12 is 53%. Over the same seven days bitcoin fell 12.7%, to about $64,650.
  • Only about 382.7 million of a maximum 2.1 billion PEARL circulate, about 18%. The fully diluted value is about $3.86 billion. PEARL is still 25% below its $2.46 record from October 21.

How PEARL climbed

PEARL rose on most days of the past week. It traded near $1.39 at 1 a.m. ET on November 11, dipped to about $1.20 on the morning of November 12, then climbed in steps to a high near $1.85 at 6 p.m. ET on November 17, according to CoinGecko. Just after midnight ET on November 18 it sat at about $1.84 and was on CoinGecko's trending list.

The rest of the market went the other way. Bitcoin lost 12.7% and ether 12.5% over the same seven days, CoinGecko data show.

What Pearl is

Pearl calls itself a proof-of-useful-work blockchain. In proof of work, miners compete to solve a math puzzle to add blocks. Pearl's design makes that puzzle part of matrix multiplication, the core math in AI training and inference, so a GPU doing AI jobs can mine at the same time, its project description on CoinGecko says. Its website is pearlresearch.ai.

Where the trading happens

CoinGecko counted about $24.2 million of PEARL trading in 24 hours. BigONE (28%), Gate (24%) and SafeTrade (17%) carried more than two-thirds of it. Coinbase's PEARL to dollar market took about 10.5%, or $2.5 million, and Kraken about $720,000. A small share runs through Rhea Finance on NEAR.

Daily volume roughly doubled during the week, from about $12.5 million on November 12 to over $24 million.

The supply picture

Pearl's maximum supply is 2.1 billion coins. CoinGecko counts about 382.7 million in circulation. That leaves about 82% still to come. In a mined coin, those new coins are paid out to miners over time, and miners often sell some to cover costs.

CoinGecko's figures also hint at that flow. On its seven-day chart, from 2 a.m. ET on November 11, market value rose about 34%, from about $524 million to $704 million, while the price rose about 32%. The gap equals roughly 7.4 million more PEARL counted as circulating in a week.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the move is a countertrend trade: buyers looking for coins with their own story while bitcoin falls. Rallies like this in a weak market can reverse fast, as the coin's 25% drop from its October record shows.
  • Supply is the long-term question. With 82% of coins not yet mined, steady new supply will meet any demand, so the low float matters more than the weekly gain.
  • Most PEARL trading runs on BigONE, Gate and SafeTrade, with Coinbase and Kraken a small share, so the price is set mainly on smaller venues.

What's next

  • Whether PEARL can retake its $2.46 record from October 21
  • How fast CoinGecko's circulating supply count keeps rising
  • Whether trading spreads beyond the three exchanges that carry most of it
