A shark with 81,000+ trades a month just bought $6 million of RLUSD, 99% of its basket

DeepSea's shark Hold91cc swapped into 18 tokens within one minute on Ethereum. Only one was big: Ripple's dollar stablecoin.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • DeepSea recorded a 6.00 million RLUSD buy at about 6:36 a.m. ET, worth $6.006 million.
  • The same minute held buys of 17 other tokens (EIGEN, ONDO, QNT, PAXG and more), priced at only about $69,000 in all.
  • The wallet made more than 81,000 trades and $1.62 billion of volume in 30 days, and DeepSea shows it down $1.64 million over 7 days.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea tracks "sharks", big wallets whose trades it follows. Hold91cc is one on Ethereum. Its owner is unknown. At about 6:36 a.m. ET, DeepSea logged a burst of buys, all within one minute.

The largest: 6.00 million RLUSD, a stablecoin issued by Ripple, priced at $6.006 million. Next came EIGEN (about $34,800), BARD (about $13,100), ONDO (about $6,600), USDS (about $4,600), QNT (about $4,200) and PAXG (about $2,600), plus smaller buys. DeepSea priced the other buys at about $69,000 in total.

The wallet is very busy: more than 81,000 trades (81,143 at 6:37 a.m. ET) and $1.62 billion of volume in 30 days, with a 34% win rate and a loss of $1.64 million over 7 days. Its top holdings on the page: LINK $34.61 million, RLUSD $16.32 million, XAUt (tokenized gold) $14.35 million, USD1 $11.56 million and QNT $9.17 million. DeepSea shows $109.42 million as total assets, but a $340.71 million holdings figure further down, so treat totals with care.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 17, 2026, 6:46 a.m. ET.

Checking the numbers

CoinGecko priced RLUSD at $1.00 at 6:40 a.m. ET, so 6.00 million tokens match DeepSea's $6.006 million. EigenLayer traded near $0.131: 267,800 EIGEN is about $35,000, close to DeepSea's figure. ONDO near $0.309 puts 21,600 ONDO at about $6,700, also a match.

The pattern, many small purchases plus one large stablecoin purchase, is common for wallets that rebalance. We cannot see the reason. Data from DeepSea and CoinGecko.

New to this?

RLUSD is a stablecoin, a token designed to stay near one US dollar, issued by Ripple. Buying a stablecoin moves a wallet from price-moving assets toward cash-like holdings, but it can also be a simple swap between tokens. A "shark" in DeepSea means a wallet with a large balance whose trades are tracked. Because this wallet trades tens of thousands of times a month, one batch tells us little on its own. The better clue is the holdings list: RLUSD at $16.32 million is its second-largest position after LINK, so the new $6 million buy is a large share of what it holds in that coin.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the burst of small buys alongside one large stablecoin buy looks like portfolio upkeep, but DeepSea cannot show the wallet owner's intent.
  • A 34% win rate and a weekly loss show that a big, active wallet is not automatically a winning one.

What's next

  • Whether RLUSD holdings keep growing from $16.32 million
  • Further buy or sell batches from this wallet
