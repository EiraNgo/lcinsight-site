Janction (JCT) gives back most of its rally: down 65% from Sunday's record

JCT hit an all-time high near $0.0185 on Sunday afternoon. By early Tuesday it traded near $0.0065, down 26% in a day, though still more than twice its price before the surge.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows JCT at about $0.00648 at 4:02 a.m. ET on November 10, 2026, down 26% in 24 hours and 65% below its all-time high of $0.01853, set at 3:10 p.m. ET on November 8.
  • In the past 24 hours JCT swung between $0.01157 and $0.00558. Trading volume was about $68 million, against a market cap of about $74 million.
  • The token is still up about 30% over 7 days and roughly 2.4 times its price of about $0.0027 late on November 7, before the surge began.

The drop

Janction's token JCT ran from about $0.0027 late on Saturday, November 7, to a new all-time high of $0.01853 at 3:10 p.m. ET on Sunday, November 8, according to CoinGecko. That is close to a 7x move in under a day.

Most of it has now been given back. At 4:02 a.m. ET on Tuesday, November 10, JCT traded at about $0.00648, down 26% in 24 hours and 65% below the record. The 24-hour range ran from $0.01157 down to $0.00558. JCT was the number three trending coin on CoinGecko on Tuesday morning.

Where it traded

CoinGecko's ticker data put about $69 million of JCT volume across all markets in 24 hours, well below the roughly $175 million recorded on Sunday morning. The PancakeSwap Infinity CLMM pool on BNB Chain handled about $26.9 million (39%), KuCoin about $19.0 million (28%) and Bitget about $15.4 million (22%). Uniswap V3 on BNB Chain, LBank and MEXC followed with small shares.

Supply and value

Janction describes itself on its CoinGecko listing as a platform for sharing computing power, storage and other resources for AI products. CoinGecko lists about 11.49 billion JCT in circulation out of a maximum supply of 50 billion, about 23%. At Tuesday's price the market cap is about $74.4 million and the fully diluted value about $324 million. We did not find an official statement from Janction about the rise or the drop in the data we checked, so we do not assign a cause.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • A run to a record followed by a 65% pullback within two days is the pattern of heavy short-term trading. Daily volume close to the full market cap means coins are still changing hands fast, so sharp swings in both directions remain likely.

What's next

  • Whether JCT holds above its pre-surge level near $0.0027
  • Any official statement from Janction
  • Changes in circulating supply
