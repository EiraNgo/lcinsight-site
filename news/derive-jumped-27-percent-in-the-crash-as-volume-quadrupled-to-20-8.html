Derive jumped 27% in the crash as volume quadrupled to $20.8 million, led by Upbit

Three large tokens rose while almost everything else fell: Derive (DRV), Bitway (BTW) and Venice (VVV). Exchange data show where the buying happened. We found no primary source that explains the moves.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Derive (DRV) rose 27.5% in 24 hours to $0.3117 at 6:35 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 21, 2026, CoinGecko shows. Its 24-hour volume rose from $4.8 million on Friday morning to $20.8 million.
  • South Korea's Upbit carried 36% of DRV's trading in its won market, and Korean won pairs on Upbit and Bithumb about 41%, in CoinGecko's exchange data.
  • Bitway (BTW) rose 15.5% to $0.7465 on almost unchanged volume, led by Bitget. Venice (VVV) rose 9.1% to $22.27 as volume grew about half, led by Coinbase. Bitcoin fell 5.2% over the same day.

Three tokens against the tide

At 6:35 a.m. ET on Saturday, bitcoin was down 5.2% in 24 hours at $58,424 and ether down 4.8%, CoinGecko's market list shows. Among tokens worth more than $100 million, three of the biggest gainers were Derive (DRV, up 27.5%), Bitway (BTW, up 15.5%) and Venice (VVV, up 9.1%).

Derive runs an on-chain market for options, perpetual futures and structured products, CoinGecko's profile says. Venice offers private AI chat and image tools. Bitway's BTW is the token of a yield, payment and lending network.

Derive: Korean won buyers and a fourfold volume jump

DRV traded near $0.248 at 8 p.m. ET on Friday and climbed through the night to about $0.296 by 6 a.m. ET, CoinGecko's hourly chart shows. Its rolling 24-hour volume went from $4.8 million at 2 a.m. ET on Friday to $20.8 million, more than four times as much. A week ago it was $7.8 million.

Upbit's DRV/KRW pair carried 36.4% of trading at 6:45 a.m. ET, in CoinGecko's ticker list, ahead of Gate (11.7%) and Coinbase (11.2%). With Bithumb, won pairs made up about 41%. DRV traded about 2.4% higher on Upbit than on Coinbase. Even after the jump, DRV is about 22% below its $0.399 of October 21 and about 45% below its October 9, 2026 record of $0.5693.

Bitway and Venice

BTW rose from about $0.661 at midnight ET to $0.743 by 4 a.m. ET, a 12% climb in four hours, per CoinGecko. Its 24-hour volume barely moved, about $10.2 million, against $10.7 million on Friday morning. Bitget carried 39.5%, HTX 26.9% and Gate 16.9%, in its tickers. BTW is still 73% below its October 14, 2026 record of $2.77, and only about 15% of its 10 billion tokens are in circulation.

VVV jumped from about $20.02 at midnight ET to $21.92 by 2 a.m. ET, per CoinGecko, and its daily volume rose from $44.1 million on Friday morning to $67.6 million. Coinbase led with 18.5%, then Bybit (11.3%) and the Aerodrome pool on Base (9.0%), its tickers show. VVV is up 20% in seven days.

What we could not find

We looked for a project post, listing notice or token event behind each move and could not open one on a primary source this morning. Exchange data show where tokens traded, not why people bought. We do not guess a cause.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think Derive's move is the one with the clearest footprint: volume more than quadrupled and Korean won markets did the most trading, a pattern we have seen in other sudden moves this month.
  • Bitway's rise came on flat volume, about half of Derive's in dollars. Thin trading can move a price quickly in both directions.
  • Rising on a day the whole market falls says something about these tokens' buyers, but it does not change the larger picture: all three are well below their records.

What's next

  • Any Derive, Bitway or Venice announcement that explains the buying.
  • Whether DRV's Upbit price premium closes.
  • Whether the three hold their gains if bitcoin falls back toward $55,000.
