Meme coin AudaCity jumped 260% in 90 minutes after an 85% slide, and it trades in one Uniswap pool

AudaCity (DACITYCOIN), a five-day-old Ethereum meme token, crashed 85% from its record over two days, then rose from $0.0042 to $0.0152 in 90 minutes on Saturday afternoon, November 14. It is up 248% in 24 hours and No. 2 on CoinGecko's trending list.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows AudaCity at about $0.0130 at 10:06 a.m. ET on November 15, 2026, up 248% in 24 hours. It is No. 2 on CoinGecko's trending list.
  • It hit a record $0.0236 at 7:20 p.m. ET on November 12, then fell 85% to about $0.0036 by 11:35 a.m. ET on November 14.
  • Between 1:45 and 3:15 p.m. ET on Saturday it climbed from about $0.0042 to $0.0152. All trading CoinGecko tracks runs through one Uniswap v4 pool on Ethereum, about $1.1 million in 24 hours.

What moved

AudaCity, traded under the ticker DACITYCOIN, is a meme token on Ethereum. CoinGecko's price history for it starts at noon ET on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, near $0.0060.

It rose for two days to a record $0.0236 at 7:20 p.m. ET on Thursday, November 12. Then it fell hard. By 11:35 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 14, it was near $0.0036, about 85% below that peak.

The rebound came fast. CoinGecko's five-minute data shows about $0.0042 at 1:45 p.m. ET on Saturday and about $0.0152 at 3:15 p.m. ET, a rise of roughly 260% in 90 minutes. The price touched about $0.0156 at 9:35 p.m. ET and then drifted lower overnight.

At 10:06 a.m. ET on Sunday, November 15, AudaCity stood at about $0.0130, up 248% in 24 hours and still about 45% below its record. Over the same 24 hours CoinGecko shows bitcoin down 1.9% and ether down 2.6%.

Where it traded

The only market CoinGecko tracks for DACITYCOIN is a Uniswap v4 pool on Ethereum, where the token is swapped for ETH. Roughly $1.1 million went through that pool over the past day, less than a tenth of the token's market value. No centralized exchange appears on its CoinGecko markets list.

Thin, single-pool trading cuts both ways: a few big sells can sink the price, and a few big buys can lift it just as quickly. AudaCity has now shown both in the same week.

It shares the top of CoinGecko's trending list with Gato The Cat (GATO), another new Ethereum meme coin with a fixed 1 billion supply that also trades on a single Uniswap v4 pool. We found no tie between the two teams.

Supply and value

The supply is fixed at 1 billion tokens, and CoinGecko counts all of them as circulating. At about $0.013 each, that makes AudaCity worth roughly $13 million, No. 979 by market value on CoinGecko.

Its CoinGecko profile calls the project a joke city "where audacity is the local culture", run on humor and memes rather than financial products. The profile names no team and says nothing about an audit, a token split or a roadmap. It links to audacityeth.lol and to @audacityeth_x on X.

What we could not check

Holder data for DACITYCOIN was not something we could confirm, so we cannot say how concentrated the supply is or who sold into the two-day slide. The Ethereum contract address is 0xc4700a...89e98e2. We found no official post that explains either the crash or the rebound. We will update this story if the project says more.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a more than threefold day while bitcoin and ether both slipped looks like buying aimed at this one token, not a market-wide lift. The price data does not show who did the buying. This is not investment advice.
  • Just before the jump the same coin had lost 85% in under two days. With five days of history, one pool and about $1 million of daily volume, AudaCity can move that far again in either direction.
  • Both of CoinGecko's top trending coins this weekend are new single-pool Ethereum meme tokens.
