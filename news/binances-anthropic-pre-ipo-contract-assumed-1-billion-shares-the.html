Binance's Anthropic pre-IPO contract assumed 1 billion shares. The filing points to 13.1 billion

Binance priced its ANTHROPICUSDT contract on an estimate of 1 billion Anthropic shares. Citing Anthropic's latest IPO filing, it now expects about 13.1 billion and will resize and rename the contract before the stock lists.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Binance's ANTHROPICUSDT Pre-IPO Perp has used an estimate of 1,000,000,000 Anthropic shares since its contract specs were published on June 2, 2026.
  • In a notice dated November 13, Binance says Anthropic's amended S-1 filing of November 10 points to a revised estimate of 13,116,075,288 shares, about 13.1 times as many.
  • Binance will adjust the contract size before the official IPO listing and rename the contract ANTUSDT. It says open positions are not affected.

What Binance announced

Binance runs a futures contract called the ANTHROPICUSDT Pre-IPO Perp. It is a perpetual contract (a futures bet with no expiry date) settled in stablecoins, and it tracks the price of one common share of Anthropic, the AI company, before the shares trade on a stock market.

In a notice dated November 13, 2026, Binance explained which shares it counts. Since its contract specifications of June 2, 2026, the exchange has used an estimate of 1,000,000,000 shares. Based on Anthropic's amended registration filing, the S-1/A-2 published November 10, Binance now expects a revised estimate of 13,116,075,288 shares.

Binance stresses that the contract does not give any ownership of Anthropic and that the product is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Anthropic.

Where 13.1 billion comes from

Binance counts only common shares issued and outstanding. It leaves out unexercised options, unsettled restricted stock units, unconverted convertibles, the underwriters' option shares and shares reserved under equity plans. Its four building blocks, all taken from the filing as Binance reads it:

What changes for traders

For now, nothing. Binance says "No change will be made to the current Contract Size" until further notice. The contract size will be adjusted before Anthropic's official IPO listing date, with timing and details in a separate exchange notice.

During that adjustment, the contract will be renamed ANTUSDT Pre-IPO Perp. Binance says the rename will not impact open positions and that users do not need to close or settle anything because of it. It also warns that the estimate can move again if Anthropic updates its filing before listing: "The actual share count may be different."

The notice does not give the contract's current price, its leverage limits or a listing date.

Why the share count matters

A pre-IPO contract has no live stock price to follow, so its value per share depends on how many shares the company is split into. The same company value spread over 13.1 billion shares works out to a much smaller number per share than over 1 billion. Binance did not say in the notice how the size adjustment will be calculated.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think this notice is the most concrete sign yet that a crypto exchange is preparing its Anthropic product for a real listing date, not just trading on hype. The rename to ANTUSDT points to the ticker Binance expects.
  • A 13-fold change in the share base is large. If the contract size is not adjusted with care, the per-share price could look very different from the stock's first trade, and traders who skip the fine print may misread the gap.
  • The estimate rests on a filing that can still change. Until the stock lists, the share count is a moving target, as Binance itself says.

What's next

  • Binance's exchange notice with the exact contract size change and date
  • Any further amendment to Anthropic's S-1 that changes the share count
  • Anthropic's official listing date and ticker
