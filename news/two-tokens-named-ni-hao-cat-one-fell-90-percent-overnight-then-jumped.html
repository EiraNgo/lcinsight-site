Two tokens named ni hao cat: one fell 90% overnight, then jumped 9x in a day

CoinGecko lists two "ni hao cat" tokens with the same MIHAO ticker, one on Ethereum and one on Solana. The Ethereum one went from a record to a 90% loss and back up 9x in 24 hours, all in a single Uniswap pool.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko lists two tokens called ni hao cat with the ticker MIHAO: one on Ethereum and one on Solana, with different contracts, websites and X accounts.
  • The Ethereum MIHAO hit a record $0.0270 at 5:26 p.m. ET on November 12, fell to about $0.0026 by 1 a.m. ET (about 90% lower), climbed back to about $0.023 by 5 p.m. ET on November 13, then halved to $0.011 by 7 p.m. ET.
  • All of its tracked trading, about $3.5 million in 24 hours, ran through one Uniswap V4 pool. Another Ethereum memecoin on the trending list, Seppuku, fell about 31% in five hours.

Same name, two tokens

The coin at the top of CoinGecko's trending list on Friday evening is ni hao cat (MIHAO). There are two of them. One is a Solana token launched through Pump.fun, the Solana memecoin launchpad, with the website mihao.fun and the X account @iamnihaocat. The other is an Ethereum token with the website mihao.site and the X account @MiHaoCat. Both use the same name and ticker on CoinGecko.

The Solana token is the one ranked first on the trending list. At 7:10 p.m. ET it traded at about $0.0145, down 5.7% in a day, with a market value near $13.6 million and $10.9 million in daily volume, mostly on PumpSwap and Meteora pools. The Ethereum token had the wild day.

The Ethereum MIHAO's 24 hours

CoinGecko data shows the Ethereum MIHAO at an all-time low of $0.0015 at 4:38 a.m. ET on Thursday, November 12. It set a record of $0.0270 at 5:26 p.m. ET the same day, about 18 times higher.

Then it reversed. By 1 a.m. ET on Friday it was near $0.0026, about 90% below the record. By 5 p.m. ET it was back near $0.023, about 9 times the overnight low. Two hours later, at 7:02 p.m. ET, it was at $0.0110, down 33% in 24 hours and 59% below its record. With a fixed supply of 1 billion tokens, that is a market value of about $11.0 million.

One pool, big swings

CoinGecko's market list shows almost all of the Ethereum MIHAO's trading in one place: a Uniswap V4 pool on Ethereum that carried about $3.5 million in 24 hours. The only other listed market had $83 of volume and is marked stale. When all trading runs through one pool, a few large buys or sales can move the price by multiples in hours, which is what the chart shows.

We could not open an Ethereum block explorer or the projects' sites from our system for this story, so we cannot say who bought or sold, how the pool's liquidity changed, or whether the two tokens' teams are related. We found no statement from either X account in the data we could check.

Seppuku, the other Ethereum memecoin on the list

Seppuku (SEPPUKU), another Ethereum memecoin that was on CoinGecko's trending list on Friday, followed a calmer but similar path. It roughly tripled from an all-time low of $0.0103 at 3:02 p.m. ET on November 10 to a record $0.0307 at 3:52 p.m. ET on November 12. On Friday it slid from about $0.0228 at 2 p.m. ET to $0.0156 at 7:02 p.m. ET, about 31% in five hours. It is down 25% in 24 hours and 49% below its record, with a market value near $15.6 million. Its $1.6 million of daily volume also ran through a single Uniswap V4 pool.

What to watch

Readers looking up MIHAO should check which chain and contract they are viewing: the Ethereum token's contract is 0xb3a6add33f777ac6ea0ec88bdf533573c8bbbc03, and the Solana token's mint is 4CxjZyMNoF1p6eXHXHv1TWpS2X47CuUmLwtGe18spump, as listed on CoinGecko. We will watch whether the Ethereum MIHAO holds above Friday's overnight low near $0.0026 and whether either project's X account comments.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the Ethereum MIHAO's 90% drop and 9x rebound inside one day is what thin, single-pool trading looks like: the price measures a few large orders, not broad demand. This is not investment advice.
  • Two tokens sharing one name and ticker is a trap for anyone who searches by ticker. The trending rank belongs to the Solana token; the wild chart belongs to the Ethereum one.
  • Seppuku's slide shows the same pattern at a smaller scale: a fast run to a record, then a sharp drop, with all trading in one pool.

  • Volume in the single Uniswap V4 pools of MIHAO and SEPPUKU
