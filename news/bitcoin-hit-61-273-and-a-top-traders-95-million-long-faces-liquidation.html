Bitcoin hit $61,273, and a top trader's $95 million long faces liquidation at $60,183

A single 40x bet on a rise took the long share of DeepSea's top Hyperliquid traders from 7% on Thursday to 18%. Without it, the long side is about the same size as a day ago.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Bitcoin traded at $61,568 at 12:35 a.m. ET on Friday, down 2.9% in 24 hours, after a low of $61,273, CoinGecko shows.
  • DeepSea's BTC-PERP page counts 94 top Hyperliquid traders: $666.32 million short and $147.57 million long. On Thursday it was $693.71 million short and $55.60 million long.
  • The biggest position is a new $95.45 million long at 40x, entered at $61,702, with a liquidation price of $60,183.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's BTC-PERP page lists the open bitcoin bets of the biggest traders it tracks on Hyperliquid, a futures exchange that runs on its own blockchain. At 12:39 a.m. ET on Friday it counted 94 tracked wallets: 82% short (bets that the price drops) with $666.32 million at an average entry of $71,031, and 18% long with $147.57 million at an average of $65,844.

A day earlier the same page showed 97 wallets, 93% short, with $693.71 million short and $55.60 million long. Most of the jump on the long side is one position.

Smart trader #72 now holds the largest bet on the list: a $95.45 million long at 40x leverage, entered at $61,702, with a liquidation price of $60,183 and a paper loss of $186,500. It was not among the top positions in our Thursday check. Without it, the other longs add up to about $52 million, close to Thursday's total.

Another big long, Smart trader #219, holds $10.92 million at 40x from $81,221 and is $3.48 million down on paper.

The shorts still sit on paper gains. John Wick, the largest short at 12:39 a.m. ET, showed $62.57 million at 25x from $69,363, up $7.87 million.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 20, 2026, 12:39 a.m. ET.

The price

Bitcoin slid overnight to a 24-hour low of $61,273, according to CoinGecko. At 12:35 a.m. ET on Friday it traded at $61,568, down 2.9% in a day and 10.8% in a week.

Perps, short for perpetual futures, never expire. Leverage lets a trader bet with borrowed money: at 40x, $1 of margin controls $40 of bitcoin. A liquidation price is where the exchange closes the bet because the margin is used up.

How close the long is to liquidation

The new long's liquidation price is about 2.2% below Friday's 12:35 a.m. price. A drop of that size would close the $95.45 million bet. A move back above $61,702, its entry, would put it in profit.

In our screenshot at 12:59 a.m. ET it was $95.94 million and showed a paper gain of $301,800. The whole long side read $146.69 million and the short side $669.35 million.

The wider market

CoinGecko's Hyperliquid page, read through its data feed at 12:58 a.m. ET, put open bets on Hyperliquid's BTC-USD contract at $1.24 billion, counted one side only, with $4.95 billion traded in 24 hours.

On CoinGecko's derivatives list at the same time, funding on Binance's BTCUSDT contract, the largest at $6.05 billion of open bets, was 0.0003%, down from 0.0044% on Thursday. Funding is a small fee one side pays the other; near zero means longs pay almost nothing.

What we could not check

Hyperliquid's own data feed and block explorers were out of reach for us this morning, so we could not see the wallet behind Smart trader #72 or exactly when it opened the bet. The list changes by the minute, so the figures above are for the times shown.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think one wallet is driving the change in mood on this board. As a group, these traders are still heavily short.
  • A 40x long this close to its liquidation price is a short-term bet. If bitcoin dips about 2%, the exchange would have to sell a large position into a falling market.
  • Funding near zero on Binance suggests the wider market is now neutral, not eager to buy.

What's next

  • Whether bitcoin holds above $60,183, the big long's liquidation price.
  • Whether Smart trader #72 adds to the long or cuts it.
  • Whether Binance funding turns negative.
