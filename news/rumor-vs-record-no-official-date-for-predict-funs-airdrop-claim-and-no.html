Rumor vs record: no official date for Predict.fun's airdrop claim, and no MetaMask token

Posts say PREDICT's delayed Season 1 claim opens November 18 and that a MetaMask token claim closes November 26. We checked four claims against official pages and CoinGecko. Three could not be confirmed on any official page we could open, and one is wrong.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • We found no official Predict.fun or Binance page we could open that confirms a November 18 claim date. At 12:40 a.m. ET, CoinGecko still showed no price, no trading markets and no circulating supply for PREDICT.
  • CoinGecko lists a total supply of 1 billion PREDICT. We could not reopen Predict.fun's own posts today, so the 70 million airdrop size, the 35% or 100% choice and a November 26 deadline stay unverified.
  • MetaMask's own support page says: "At this time, there are no plans for a $MASK token." Any page offering a MetaMask token claim should be treated as fake.

Why we did this check

Delayed airdrops attract a flood of posts with exact dates and times. This week, two kinds circulated: claims that Predict.fun's PREDICT airdrop finally opens on November 18, and countdowns for a MetaMask token claim. We split them into four claims and checked each one against the projects' own pages and CoinGecko. We did not use news articles as evidence.

What the PREDICT record shows

Predict.fun is a prediction market on BNB Chain. On October 30, we reported that its token launch and Season 1 claim were delayed. We could not reopen the official X posts behind that report from our system today, and no newer official date appeared on any page we could open.

What we could check is CoinGecko. It lists the PREDICT contract on BNB Chain with a total supply of 1 billion, but shows no price, no trading volume, no markets and no circulating supply. Until that changes, no claim date has turned into trading.

What MetaMask says

MetaMask's support page is direct: MetaMask is not planning a $MASK token. MetaMask Rewards is a sponsor program, and its Season 1 has ended. Past activity "may or may not" count for future rewards, the page says. Nothing there mentions an airdrop or a November 26 date.

How to stay safe

  • Use only official domains you type yourself: predict.fun and metamask.io.
  • Never connect a wallet from a link in a DM, a reply or an ad.
  • A claim with a countdown and no post from the project's verified account is a red flag.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the PREDICT date may well turn out right, but until Predict.fun or Binance posts it, treating it as fact helps scammers more than claimers. Fake claim pages work best in the gap between a rumor and the real announcement.
  • The MetaMask claim is the more dangerous of the two. A token the company says it has no plans for cannot have a claim deadline.
  • CoinGecko is the simplest public check. Until PREDICT shows a price and markets there, no claim has turned into trading.

What's next

  • An official PREDICT claim date from Predict.fun's verified X account or Binance
  • The first PREDICT price on CoinGecko, which would show trading has started
  • Any change to MetaMask's support page on a token
