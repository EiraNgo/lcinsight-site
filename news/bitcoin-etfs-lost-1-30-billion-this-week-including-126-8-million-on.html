Bitcoin ETFs lost $1.30 billion this week, including $126.8 million on Friday

U.S. spot bitcoin funds had a fourth straight outflow day as bitcoin broke below $60,000. Ether funds lost money every day of the week, and November's outflows now top $3.4 billion for bitcoin funds.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Investors pulled a net $126.8 million from U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs on Friday, November 20, 2026, CoinMarketCap's first reading shows. Spot ether ETFs lost $13.2 million.
  • For the week of November 16 to 20, bitcoin funds lost $1.30 billion, against $880.6 million the week before. Ether funds lost $211.7 million, with outflows on all five days.
  • Bitcoin funds have now had four outflow days in a row, worth $1.31 billion. November stands at minus $3.46 billion over 15 sessions.

Friday's numbers

U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs lost a net $126.8 million on Friday, November 20, 2026, according to CoinMarketCap's flow series read at 12:40 a.m. ET on Saturday. Spot ether funds lost $13.2 million, CoinMarketCap's ether fund data show.

A spot ETF buys and holds the coin itself, and its shares trade like a stock. When more money leaves a fund than enters it in a day, the fund usually has fewer coins to hold. Our ETF explainer covers the basics.

Both are first readings. Thursday's first figure, $90.8 million out, was later revised to $631.9 million out, as we reported in our Friday story. That revised number is unchanged in Saturday's data. We could not open Farside's per-fund tables, so the funds behind Friday's outflow are not yet known.

The week in one chart

Bitcoin funds took in money on Monday, $9.6 million, and lost it on every other day of the week. Thursday was the worst day of the month so far. The week's total was minus $1.30 billion, about 47% more than the $880.6 million that left in the week of November 9 to 13.

Since Tuesday, four outflow days in a row have taken out $1.31 billion: $241.9 million, $306.2 million, $631.9 million and $126.8 million.

Ether funds: five red days

Ether funds lost money on all five days of the week: $8.4 million, $26.3 million, $34.4 million, $129.4 million and $13.2 million, in CoinMarketCap's data. That is $211.7 million for the week, less than the $311.8 million of the week before. Their last inflow day was Friday, November 13, at $2.3 million.

For November, ether funds are at minus $844.1 million over 15 sessions.

The price on the day

On Friday bitcoin slid from about $61,464 at midnight ET to about $58,300 when U.S. markets closed at 4 p.m. ET, a 5.1% fall, CoinGecko's hourly chart shows. Most of that came in one hour, between 10 a.m. and 11 a.m. ET. Ether lost 7.3% over the same hours, from about $1,725 to about $1,599, in CoinGecko's ether chart.

Against that drop, Friday's $126.8 million first reading is small. The slide went on after the close: by 12:37 a.m. ET on Saturday bitcoin was at $55,780 and ether at $1,559. ETFs do not trade on weekends, so the next flow figure is for Monday, November 23.

November so far

Bitcoin funds have lost $3.46 billion in November's 15 sessions, CoinMarketCap's series shows. They had inflows on only two days, November 4 and November 16.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think Friday's figure is likely to grow. This week's first readings have been revised up sharply, Thursday's almost sevenfold, so a $126.8 million first print on a 5% down day looks incomplete.
  • Four outflow days in a row at $1.31 billion show steady selling by fund investors, but the funds are closed on weekends, so the slide below $56,000 late Friday and early Saturday came without them.
  • Ether funds' losses are smaller in dollars, but with outflows on all five days they have had almost no buyers this month.

What's next

  • Revisions to Friday's first reading on Monday.
  • Monday's flows, the first since the drop below $56,000.
  • Whether November's bitcoin fund outflow passes $4 billion.
