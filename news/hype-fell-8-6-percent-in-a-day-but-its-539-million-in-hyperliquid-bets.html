HYPE fell 8.6% in a day, but its $539 million in Hyperliquid bets tops ether's

Hyperliquid's own token slid all Tuesday to $58.79, three times bitcoin's drop. The money in open HYPE futures on its home exchange barely moved, and longs were still paying shorts.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • HYPE was at $58.79 at 12:40 a.m. ET on Wednesday, November 25, down 8.6% in 24 hours and 40% below its September 23 record of $97.96, CoinGecko shows. Bitcoin fell 2.8% and ether 3.2%.
  • Open bets on Hyperliquid's HYPE-USD contract stood at $539.3 million, second only to bitcoin's $1.05 billion and ahead of ether's $515.1 million, in CoinGecko's derivatives data.
  • Counted in tokens, those bets are about 9.2 million HYPE, roughly 1% fewer than on Saturday. Funding stayed positive on Hyperliquid and Binance.

A slow slide all day Tuesday

HYPE is the token of Hyperliquid, an exchange for perpetual futures, price bets with no expiry date (how Hyperliquid works). At 12:40 a.m. ET on Wednesday, November 25, it traded at $58.79, down 8.6% in 24 hours, 14.1% in a week and 22.5% in 30 days, according to CoinGecko. Its market value was $14.0 billion, the 10th largest coin, and the worst 24-hour result in the top 10.

The drop came in small steps. CoinGecko's five-minute prices show $64.36 at 12:45 a.m. ET on Tuesday, $62.49 at 8:05 a.m., $60.02 at 2:45 p.m. and $58.72 at 9:25 p.m. There was no single plunge. Over the same 24 hours bitcoin lost 2.8% and ether 3.2%, so HYPE fell about three times as hard.

HYPE is the second-biggest bet on its own exchange

CoinGecko's Hyperliquid derivatives page lists 353 open contracts with $4.80 billion in open interest, the money in bets not yet closed. Bitcoin's contract leads with $1.05 billion. HYPE-USD is next with $539.3 million, about 11% of the exchange's total, and ether's contract is third with $515.1 million. HYPE-USD traded about 8.66 million tokens in 24 hours.

When we last checked, at 12:41 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 21, the HYPE contract held $527 million at a price of $56.79 (our story). That was about 9.28 million HYPE. Today's figure works out to about 9.18 million HYPE, roughly 1% fewer. So the dollar total rose a little only because the price was higher than on Saturday, while traders closed few bets during Tuesday's 8.6% fall.

Binance and Gate add $418 million more

CoinGecko's cross-exchange derivatives list shows two more large HYPE contracts. Binance's HYPEUSDT holds $264.5 million in open bets and traded $553.4 million in 24 hours, a bit more than Hyperliquid's $531.3 million. Gate's HYPE_USDT holds $153.4 million. Across these three venues that is about $957 million, and Hyperliquid's share is 56%.

The funding rate, the regular payment that keeps a perpetual near the spot price, was positive on Hyperliquid and Binance. That means traders betting on a rise were still paying those betting on a fall, even after the slide. On Gate it was slightly negative.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the steady token count is the key detail. A fall of 8.6% with almost no bets closed means neither side gave up on Tuesday, so the positions that remain could make the next move sharper in either direction.
  • Positive funding after a 40% slide from the record suggests buyers are still the crowded side on the two largest venues.
  • Binance traded more HYPE futures in 24 hours than Hyperliquid did, $553.4 million to $531.3 million. We think that means price-setting for Hyperliquid's own token no longer happens mainly on its home exchange.

What's next

  • Whether HYPE's open interest in tokens starts to fall, a sign that longs are closing
  • Funding on Hyperliquid and Binance turning negative
  • The $58.54 24-hour low
