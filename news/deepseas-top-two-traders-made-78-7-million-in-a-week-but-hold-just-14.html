DeepSea's top two traders made $78.7 million in a week but hold just $14.5 million

SMc060 is back at No. 1 on DeepSea's smart money board, with SM6e36 right behind. SM6e36's assets roughly halved in a day while its profit rose, and Arbiscan shows neither wallet keeps any Aave USDC on Arbitrum.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • SMc060 leads DeepSea's 7-day smart money board at +$40.55 million with $6.85 million in total assets. SM6e36 is No. 2 at +$38.11 million with $7.63 million.
  • Over 30 days the pair are up $224.63 million together, and each traded more than $850 million.
  • SM6e36 showed $14.87 million in assets a day earlier. Arbiscan shows both wallets hold 0 of Aave's Arbitrum USDC token.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's smart money board ranks wallets by profit. Read at 12:35 a.m. ET on November 19, its 7-day list is led by two futures traders on Hyperliquid, a crypto derivatives exchange:

  • SMc060: +$40.55 million in 7 days, +$120.78 million in 30 days, $855.48 million traded in 30 days, and $6.85 million in total assets.
  • SM6e36: +$38.11 million in 7 days, +$103.85 million in 30 days, $884.44 million traded, and $7.63 million in total assets.

Together that is $78.66 million in a week against $14.48 million now in their accounts. No. 3, SMad53, is far behind at +$21.49 million, though it holds $110.25 million.

A day earlier, on November 18, DeepSea showed SM6e36 at No. 2 with +$35.19 million and $14.87 million in assets. Its 7-day profit has grown since, while its assets fell by about half.

Both pages also list Aave sales on Arbitrum: 9.50 million aArbUSDCn for SMc060 three days ago, and 9.50 million plus 551,800 for SM6e36. Many other rows are fake lookalike "USDC" tokens with no dollar value, a common spam trick.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 19, 2026, 12:35 a.m. ET.

What Arbiscan shows

Data from DeepSea and Arbiscan. The Arbitrum block explorer lists 0 aArbUSDCn, the receipt for USDC deposited in Aave, for both SMc060 and SM6e36 at about 12:50 a.m. ET. So both wallets have taken out the Aave cash they once kept on Arbitrum.

We could not open Hyperliquid's own account data in this run, so the positions behind the profits are not shown here.

How profit can beat assets

Profit and assets measure different things. Profit counts gains over the period, including money that has already been taken out. Total assets is what the account holds now. A trader who wins big and then withdraws can show a profit several times larger than the balance left behind. Smart money is DeepSea's label for profitable wallets; the owners are unknown.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the gap between $78.66 million in weekly profit and $14.48 million in assets means both traders are taking money off the table rather than letting it ride.
  • SM6e36's assets halving in a day while its profit rose points to a withdrawal, not a loss. The data does not show where the money went.
  • The two have swapped the top spots several times this month, so the ranking says more about a hot streak in a falling market than about any single trade.

What's next

  • Whether SM6e36's withdrawn funds show up on another chain or exchange.
  • Whether either trader opens large new positions.
  • Whether SMad53 closes the gap.
