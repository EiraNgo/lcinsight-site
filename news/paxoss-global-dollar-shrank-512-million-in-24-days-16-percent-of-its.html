Paxos's Global Dollar shrank $512 million in 24 days, 16% of its supply

USDG fell from $3.16 billion on October 22 to $2.65 billion on Sunday, CoinGecko data show. That is 25% below its May peak, while its price held at $1.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Global Dollar (USDG) had a market value of $2.649 billion at 6:42 a.m. ET on November 15, 2026, down $512 million or 16.2% from CoinGecko's October 22 reading of $3.161 billion.
  • Its biggest one-day drop in that stretch, $129.2 million in the October 31 reading, was the largest since May 16 in CoinGecko's one-year daily data.
  • USDG is 25% below its May 13 reading of $3.552 billion, the highest in the past year. Its price stayed at $0.9999.

What moved

USDG is a dollar token issued by Paxos Digital Singapore, a payments firm supervised by the Monetary Authority of Singapore, according to its CoinGecko listing, which says holders can redeem it one for one with Paxos. Because each token is meant to be worth $1, its market value is close to its supply. When that number falls, tokens were redeemed and destroyed.

At 6:42 a.m. ET on November 15, 2026, CoinGecko put USDG at $2.649 billion. Its October 22 daily reading was $3.161 billion. That is $512 million gone in 24 days, or 16.2% of the supply. Over 30 days it lost $437 million, or 14.2%. For comparison, USDC shrank 4.9% over the same 30 days on CoinGecko.

Steps, not a single exit

The fall came in steps. The October 31 reading showed a drop of $129.2 million, the largest one-day fall since May 16 in CoinGecko's one-year data. More one-day drops followed: $78 million in the November 1 reading, $53 million and $52 million in the November 3 and 4 readings, and $58 million in the November 7 reading. After that the daily drops were smaller, the biggest being $33 million in the November 13 reading.

The price did not move: USDG traded at $0.9999, and about $612 million changed hands in 24 hours. It is now 25% below its May 13 reading of $3.552 billion. One year ago it was about $1.11 billion, so it is still more than twice as big.

CoinGecko's data show only the total. They do not show who redeemed or why, and we found no statement from Paxos on the decline.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think a 16% cut in three weeks is large for a fully backed dollar token, even though the price held. It suggests a few big holders redeemed, not a broad loss of trust, but the data cannot prove that.
  • USDG's drop started before the November crypto selloff, so the crash alone does not explain it.
  • A stable $1 price during heavy redemptions is what a fully backed token is supposed to show.

What's next

  • Whether USDG holds near $2.65 billion or keeps falling.
  • Any statement or reserve report from Paxos on the decline.
  • Whether other Paxos-issued tokens shrink at the same time.
