MetaDAO's META jumped 40% in under three hours as bitcoin fell. Upbit carried 83% of trading

The decision-market token went from about $3.32 to $4.66 on Thursday night ET while most coins slid. Daily volume, under $1 million a week ago, hit $21 million, mostly in Korean won.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • META rose from about $3.32 at 7 p.m. ET on Thursday, November 19, 2026, to a high of about $4.66 in the 9 p.m. ET hour, about 40%, CoinMarketCap's hourly prices show.
  • At 12:35 a.m. ET on Friday, CoinGecko showed META at $4.47, up 34.9% in 24 hours, with a market value of about $102.9 million. Bitcoin was down 2.9%.
  • Upbit's Korean won market carried 83.2% of META's $21.2 million in 24-hour volume. From November 10 to 17, daily volume was $0.4 million to $0.9 million.

The move

META is the token of MetaDAO, a Solana project that CoinMarketCap's project profile calls a decision-market platform: traders bet on how a proposal would affect a measure such as the token price, and those market prices decide the outcome.

On Thursday evening it moved against the market. CoinMarketCap's hourly prices show META at about $3.32 at 7 p.m. ET, then about $3.90 an hour later, and a high of about $4.66 in the 9 p.m. ET hour. It eased to about $4.30 by midnight ET.

At 12:35 a.m. ET on Friday, CoinGecko showed $4.47, up 34.9% in 24 hours and 18.6% in seven days. Over the same 24 hours, bitcoin fell 2.9% to $61,568.

Where it traded

META's 24-hour volume was $21.2 million at 12:49 a.m. ET, according to CoinMarketCap. Upbit, a South Korean exchange, carried 83.2% of it on its won market, which CoinMarketCap lists as META2/KRW. Bithumb, another Korean exchange, carried 7.6%. MetaDAO's own futarchy pool on Solana carried 5.7%, CoinMarketCap's market list shows.

That is a big change. From November 10 to 17, META's daily volume ran between about $0.4 million and $0.9 million, per CoinMarketCap's daily data.

A second spike in two days

Thursday's move was not the first this week. On Wednesday, November 18 (UTC day), META rose from $3.13 to a high of $4.28 on $9.05 million of volume, then gave back about half of the gain to close at $3.73. It opened Thursday's UTC day there and fell to about $3.26 before the evening jump, CoinMarketCap's data show.

META is still far below its record of $10.84 from October 21, 2025, according to CoinGecko, about 59% lower. CoinMarketCap lists about 20.9 million META in total supply.

What we could not confirm

We found no MetaDAO announcement tied to the jump, and we could not open MetaDAO's or Upbit's websites from our system this morning. The volume pattern points to Korean won trading as the main driver, but we cannot say why buyers came in.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the Upbit share is the story. When one won market carries more than four-fifths of a small token's trading, its price can swing hard on local demand alone.
  • META has spiked and faded once already this week. Wednesday's high did not hold, which is a reason to watch whether Thursday's does.

What's next

  • Whether META holds above Wednesday's $4.28 high.
  • Whether Upbit's share of volume stays above 80%.
  • Any MetaDAO or Upbit notice that explains the move.
