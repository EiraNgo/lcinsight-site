Robinhood's $14.9 billion ranks third on DeepSea. One cold wallet holds $11.27 billion of it

DeepSea's exchanges list puts Robinhood's labeled wallets behind only Binance and Bitfinex. A single Bitcoin cold wallet holds about three-quarters of the total.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • DeepSea's wallets labeled Robinhood held $14.90 billion at 12:44 a.m. ET on November 5, 2026, third behind Binance and Bitfinex on its exchanges list.
  • A single wallet labeled Robinhood-coldwallet on Bitcoin holds $11.27 billion, about three-quarters of the total.
  • OKX ($7.99 billion), Bybit ($4.03 billion) and Kraken ($2.58 billion) add up to $14.60 billion, but DeepSea also lists a separate "Okex" entry with $6.38 billion.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's Robinhood page groups 21 wallets on 8 chains that it labels as Robinhood. At 12:44 a.m. ET on November 5, 2026, they held $14.90 billion: $14.32 billion in native coins such as bitcoin and ether, and only $572.49 million in other tokens.

On DeepSea's exchanges list, read at 12:41 a.m. ET, that puts Robinhood third, behind Binance ($146.34 billion) and Bitfinex ($31.07 billion). It is ahead of the OKX ($7.99 billion), Bybit ($4.03 billion) and Kraken ($2.58 billion) entries, even if those three are added together ($14.60 billion). DeepSea also lists a separate "Okex" entry, covered below, that is not in the OKX figure.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 5, 2026, 12:44 a.m. ET.

One wallet holds most of it

The biggest piece is a single address labeled Robinhood-coldwallet on Bitcoin, worth $11.27 billion. A cold wallet is one kept offline for safety, used to store coins rather than pay out withdrawals.

The next largest is a Robinhood wallet on Ethereum with $3.27 billion, of which $2.95 billion is ether. A second Ethereum wallet holds $254.59 million, mostly in tokens. Everything else is small: $30.05 million on Robinhood's own chain, $16.03 million on BNB Chain and $13.55 million on Arbitrum.

What is moving

DeepSea shows no seven-day inflow or outflow total for Robinhood, so we cannot say whether money is arriving or leaving overall. The page shows only a dash in those fields. Its token list shows small moves: VVV with $4.46 million in and $1.87 million out, ZORA with $4.27 million in and $3.90 million out, and AERO roughly even at about $2.3 million each way.

What to watch

These are labeled wallets, not a full audit. DeepSea's Coinbase entry shows only $316.98 million across 97 wallets, which suggests its labels do not cover every exchange equally. An exchange with fewer labeled wallets will look smaller here than it really is. DeepSea also lists a separate "Okex" entry with $6.38 billion that is not counted in the OKX figure; with it, the four entries would top Robinhood. Rankings like this one show what DeepSea can see on-chain. We will watch the Robinhood cold wallet for any large move.

  • We think Robinhood's high rank says more about how clearly its bitcoin is labeled than about its size against rivals, since some exchanges appear only in part.
