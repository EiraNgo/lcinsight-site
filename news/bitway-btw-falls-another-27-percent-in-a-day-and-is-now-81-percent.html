Bitway (BTW) falls another 27% in a day and is now 81% below its October record

BTW slid from about $0.72 to a low near $0.50 in 24 hours, its second steep daily drop this week, on only about $9.3 million of trading.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows BTW at $0.530 at 7:04 p.m. ET on November 15, 2026, down 26.8% in 24 hours, 53.5% in seven days and 72.5% in 30 days.
  • It touched about $0.500 at 1:45 p.m. ET, the low of the day, and is now about 81% below its $2.77 record from October 13.
  • About $9.3 million traded in 24 hours against a market value near $800 million. Bitget carried 43.6% of it and Gate 27.1%.

The move

Bitway's token BTW was at about $0.724 at 7:05 p.m. ET on Saturday, November 14, 2026, according to CoinGecko. It slid through Sunday and hit about $0.500 at 1:45 p.m. ET. At 7:04 p.m. ET on November 15 it was at $0.530, down 26.8% on the day and 4.3% in the last hour.

This is the second steep drop in three days. On November 13 we reported BTW down 29% in a day at $0.7116. Since then it has lost about another quarter of its value. Over seven days it is down 53.5%, and over 30 days 72.5%.

The token set its record of $2.77 at 11:49 p.m. ET on October 13, 2026. It is now about 81% below that level. Over the same 24 hours CoinGecko shows bitcoin up 1.9% and BNB up 2.5%, so BTW fell against a rising market.

Where it traded

CoinGecko lists 17 markets for BTW with about $9.3 million of combined volume in 24 hours. Bitget handled about $4.1 million (43.6%), Gate about $2.5 million (27.1%), HTX about $0.9 million (9.3%) and a Uniswap V3 pool on BNB Chain about $0.6 million (6.4%). Toobit and MEXC followed with smaller shares.

That volume is a little over 1% of the token's market value, so a small amount of selling can move the price a long way.

Supply to keep in mind

CoinGecko counts about 1.51 billion BTW in circulation out of a fixed 10 billion, about 15%. At $0.530 that is a market value of about $798 million (No. 78 on CoinGecko) and a fully diluted value of about $5.30 billion. Bitway has not given a reason for the slide in any source we checked, and this story does not suggest one.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, two drops of more than 25% in three days on single-digit millions of volume show how little buying there is under the price. With only 15% of supply circulating, any future unlocks would add to that pressure. This is not investment advice.

What's next

  • Any official statement from Bitway about the slide
  • Changes in circulating supply or token unlocks
