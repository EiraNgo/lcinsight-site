Ether ETFs lost $2.19 billion in 34 trading days, erasing all their late-summer inflows

U.S. spot ether funds had outflows on 29 of 34 trading days from September 29 to November 13, CoinMarketCap data show. Over 90 days they are now net sellers.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • U.S. spot ether ETFs had net outflows of $2.19 billion over the 34 trading days from September 29 to November 13, 2026, CoinMarketCap data show.
  • Money left on 29 of those days. The five inflow days added just $119.6 million in total.
  • That wiped out the $2.04 billion the funds took in from August 19 to September 28. Over the full 90-day window they are now down $152.7 million.

Two months of outflows

A spot ether ETF is a fund that trades on a U.S. stock exchange and holds ether itself. When investors pull out more money than they put in (an outflow), the fund sells ether.

According to CoinMarketCap's ETF flow data, U.S. spot ether ETFs had a combined net outflow of $2.19 billion over the 34 trading days from Tuesday, September 29, to Friday, November 13, 2026. Money left on 29 of those days. The five inflow days brought in only $119.6 million together; the largest was $75.3 million on November 4.

The biggest single outflow in the run was $201.9 million on Tuesday, October 6. Over the same stretch, the ether price CoinMarketCap lists beside the flows fell from about $2,688 to $2,017, a drop of about 25%.

The summer money is gone

The run erased an earlier buying wave. From Wednesday, August 19, to Monday, September 28, the funds took in a net $2.04 billion over 28 trading days, with eight days above $170 million each.

Added together, the 90-day window that CoinMarketCap shows (August 19 to November 13) turned negative on Monday, November 9, after a $163.4 million outflow. It stood at minus $152.7 million after Friday's session.

By month, the funds lost $1.50 billion in October and another $632.4 million in the first 10 trading days of November.

Where ether trades now

The selling continued after the funds closed on Friday. CoinGecko showed ether at $1,807.61 at 6:33 a.m. ET on Monday, November 16, down 1.4% in 24 hours and about 63% below its record of $4,946.05 set in August 2025. Flows for the weekend drop will first show up in Monday's ETF data.

What we could not confirm

CoinMarketCap's daily figures are totals for all U.S. spot ether funds. They do not split each day by fund, so we cannot say which fund lost the most. CoinMarketCap's fund list names BlackRock's iShares Ethereum Trust (ETHA) as the largest, but its asset figures did not match its market value when we checked, so we leave them out.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the reversal shows the late-summer buying was short-term money. It took 28 days to come in and 34 days to leave.
  • The selling in ether funds has been steadier than in bitcoin funds. Over the last 20 trading days, bitcoin funds had 8 inflow days and ether funds 4, CoinMarketCap data show.
  • With ether near $1,800 after the weekend, Monday's flows will show whether fund holders keep selling into the drop.

What's next

  • Monday, November 16: whether ether funds post a second straight inflow day or return to outflows.
  • Whether the 90-day total keeps falling below zero.
  • Fund-by-fund flow data once CoinMarketCap lists them.
