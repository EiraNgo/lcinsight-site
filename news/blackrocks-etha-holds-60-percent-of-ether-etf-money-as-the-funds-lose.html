BlackRock's ETHA holds 60% of ether ETF money as the funds lose $403 million in 8 days

U.S. spot ether funds lost $191.7 million in the three trading days before Thanksgiving and have had only two inflow days in November. One BlackRock fund now holds most of what is left.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • BlackRock's iShares Ethereum Trust (ETHA) held $8.61 billion, 59.8% of the $14.39 billion in U.S. spot ether ETFs, in CoinMarketCap's fund list, read at 6:42 a.m. ET on November 27, 2026.
  • Spot ether ETFs lost $84.9 million Monday, $38.3 million Tuesday and $68.5 million Wednesday, a total of $191.7 million before the Thanksgiving break, CoinMarketCap data shows.
  • That makes eight straight outflow days worth $403.4 million. In November's 18 trading days the funds lost $1.04 billion, with inflows on only two of them.

One fund holds most of the money

U.S. spot ether ETFs held $14.39 billion across nine funds when we read CoinMarketCap's ETF list at 6:42 a.m. ET on Friday, November 27, 2026. BlackRock's ETHA alone held $8.61 billion, or 59.8%. A spot ETF holds the coin itself, so its assets rise and fall with both the ether price and money moving in or out. Grayscale's two funds come next, with $4.03 billion combined, followed by Fidelity's FETH at $1.37 billion.

The week so far

The group lost a net $84.9 million on Monday, November 23, $38.3 million on Tuesday and $68.5 million on Wednesday, according to CoinMarketCap's daily flow data. That is $191.7 million in three days. U.S. markets were closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving, and Friday is a shortened session, so Friday's figure was not out when we checked.

The last inflow day was Friday, November 13, at $2.3 million. Since then the funds have had eight outflow days in a row, worth $403.4 million in all.

November so far

Across November's 18 trading days through Wednesday, spot ether ETFs lost a net $1.04 billion in CoinMarketCap's 90-day series. Only two days had inflows: $75.3 million on November 4 and $2.3 million on November 13. The biggest single outflow was $163.4 million on November 9.

Ether's price, as logged next to each day in the same data, slid from about $2,017 on November 13 to about $1,678 on November 25. CoinGecko showed $1,728 at 6:35 a.m. ET on Friday, up 1.8% in 24 hours.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think ETHA's 60% share is the number to watch, not the daily totals. When most of the money sits in one fund, that fund's flows decide the group's day, and its 0.25% fee is far below Grayscale ETHE's 2.50%.
  • The outflows have been steady, with no day this week above $85 million. That looks more like a slow drain than a rush for the exit.
  • Ether's 7-day move on CoinGecko was close to flat on Friday even as money kept leaving the funds, which suggests ETF selling is only one part of the ether market.

What's next

  • Friday's shortened session, the first data after Thanksgiving.
  • Whether a single inflow day breaks the eight-day run.
  • Changes in ETHA's share of the group's assets.
