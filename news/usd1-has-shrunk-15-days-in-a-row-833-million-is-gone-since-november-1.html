USD1 has shrunk 15 days in a row. $833 million is gone since November 1

World Liberty's dollar token fell from $3.87 billion to $3.04 billion on CoinGecko, now below where it started 2026, while it still trades about $1 billion a day.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • USD1's market value fell in every CoinGecko daily reading from November 2 through November 16, 2026, 15 in a row, and dropped again to $3.038 billion at 6:42 a.m. ET.
  • That is $833 million, or 21.5%, below the November 1 reading of $3.871 billion. The biggest single drop was $103 million in the November 7 reading.
  • USD1 is 43.5% below its one-year high of $5.378 billion on February 12 and below its January 1 level of $3.312 billion. Etherscan shows 893 million USD1 on Ethereum.

Fifteen straight drops

USD1 is the dollar token of World Liberty Financial. Because it is meant to trade at $1, its market value tracks how many tokens exist. A falling number means tokens were redeemed and destroyed.

CoinGecko's daily data show USD1 at $3.871 billion in the November 1 reading. It then fell in each of the next 15 readings, to $3.077 billion on November 16. The live figure at 6:42 a.m. ET was lower still: $3.038 billion. That is $833 million gone, or 21.5%.

The heaviest days came early. Four readings from November 4 to November 7 each showed drops of $71.6 million to $103 million. Smaller drops followed, with $84 million and $78.8 million in the November 10 and 11 readings.

Busy trading, steady price

While supply shrank, trading stayed heavy. CoinGecko counted about $1.02 billion of USD1 traded in the 24 hours to the November 16 reading, a third of its value. Eight of the 16 daily readings this month topped $1 billion. Over the past 10 days the price held between $0.9976 and $0.9987, a touch under $1.

Most of the token lives outside Ethereum. Etherscan showed a supply of 893.3 million USD1 on Ethereum at 7:05 a.m. ET, held by 42,904 addresses, about 29% of the total. rwa.xyz put all USD1 at $3.073 billion, sixth among dollar tokens.

Back below January

USD1 started 2026 at $3.312 billion, peaked at $5.378 billion on February 12, and was still $4.2 billion on September 1. It is now 43.5% below that February high and smaller than on New Year's Day. One year ago it was $2.81 billion.

USD1 shrank far faster than dollar tokens as a group: rwa.xyz shows all stablecoins down 5.0% in 30 days, to $277.6 billion. CoinGecko's figures show only totals, and we found no statement from World Liberty Financial on the decline.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think a 15-day streak with this much daily trading points to large holders redeeming steadily, not a sudden loss of trust. The price stayed within a quarter of a cent of $1.
  • About 71% of USD1 sits outside Ethereum, much of it on chains where it is used on exchanges. We think the drop likely reflects trading balances shrinking as the market fell, though the data do not show who redeemed.
  • Shrinking dollar tokens mean less cash waiting on-chain to buy crypto. That is a slow headwind, not a crash signal.

What's next

  • Whether USD1 posts a 16th straight drop in the November 17 reading.
  • Whether it falls below $3 billion.
  • Any statement from World Liberty Financial on redemptions.
