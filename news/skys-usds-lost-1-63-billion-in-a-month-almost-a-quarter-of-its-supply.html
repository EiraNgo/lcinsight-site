Sky's USDS lost $1.63 billion in a month, almost a quarter of its supply

The 12 biggest dollar stablecoins shrank $850 million in a day and $3.61 billion in a week, DefiLlama data show. USDS fell $121 million in a day, and USDC lost ground on Hyperliquid, Solana and Base.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • The 12 largest dollar stablecoins held $274.36 billion at about 12:36 a.m. ET on November 18, 2026, down $850 million in a day and $3.61 billion in a week, DefiLlama data show.
  • Sky's USDS fell $121.2 million (2.3%) in a day to $5.24 billion and is down $1.63 billion, or 23.7%, from a month earlier.
  • World Liberty Financial's USD1 fell $151.5 million in a day to $2.94 billion, lower than the $3.04 billion we reported on November 16.
  • USDC fell $194.3 million on Hyperliquid's chain, $108.8 million on Solana and $93.9 million on Base, but rose $92.2 million on Arbitrum.

What moved

Stablecoins are tokens pegged to the US dollar. Supply grows when people deposit dollars with an issuer and shrinks when tokens are redeemed, so the total is a rough gauge of cash entering or leaving crypto.

DefiLlama's stablecoin data, read at about 12:36 a.m. ET on November 18, 2026, put the 12 largest dollar stablecoins at $274.36 billion. That is $850 million less than a day earlier, $3.61 billion less than a week earlier and $15.31 billion less than a month earlier. Ether fell 1.4% in a day and 12.5% in a week, CoinGecko showed.

USDS shrinks fastest among the big coins

Sky's USDS, the dollar token of the project formerly called Maker, fell $121.2 million in a day to $5.24 billion. A month ago DefiLlama counted $6.86 billion, so $1.63 billion, or 23.7%, is gone. Over the same month USDT shrank 4.4% and USDC 4.7%.

USD1 fell $151.5 million in a day to $2.94 billion, 27.3% below a month earlier. On November 16 we reported it at $3.04 billion on CoinGecko after 15 straight daily drops. DefiLlama also now shows PayPal's PYUSD at $2.37 billion, which appears to reflect the drop we reported on November 17 rather than a new one.

Ripple's RLUSD bucked the trend over the week, up $110.9 million, though it slipped $10.7 million in the last day.

Where USDC and USDT moved

DefiLlama splits each coin by blockchain. USDC on Hyperliquid's own chain fell $194.3 million in a day to $5.82 billion. USDC on Solana fell $108.8 million and on Base $93.9 million. Arbitrum went the other way: USDC there rose $92.2 million and USDT $36.4 million. Circle's Arc chain added $85.7 million of USDC.

USDT on Tron, its biggest home, fell $110.9 million in a day to $91.77 billion. USDT on Ethereum fell $44.6 million.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the USDS drop matters more than its size suggests, because a fall of nearly a quarter in a month is far steeper than for USDT or USDC.
  • The chain moves look like a reshuffle on top of a slow drain: Arbitrum and Arc gained, but not enough to offset Hyperliquid, Solana and Base.
  • The likely result is that the total keeps sliding while prices stay weak, since redemptions have outpaced new issuance for weeks.

What's next

  • USD1 against $2.94 billion
  • USDC on Hyperliquid's chain against $5.82 billion
  • USDT on Tron after a $110.9 million daily drop
