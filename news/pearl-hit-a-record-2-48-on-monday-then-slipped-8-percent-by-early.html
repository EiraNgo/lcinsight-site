Pearl hit a record $2.48 on Monday, then slipped 8% by early evening

The AI mining coin climbed about 21% from midnight ET to a new high at 4:55 p.m. ET, just above its October record, while bitcoin went nowhere. By 7:10 p.m. ET it was back at $2.29.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko records a new all-time high for PEARL of $2.48 at 4:55 p.m. ET on November 23, 2026, edging past the $2.46 record from October 21.
  • PEARL traded near $2.05 at midnight ET, so the climb to the record was about 21% in 17 hours. By 7:10 p.m. ET it was at about $2.29, 7.8% below the new high.
  • PEARL is up 12.8% in 24 hours and 44.4% in seven days, ranked 74th at about $889 million. Bitcoin is flat over 24 hours, near $61,090, and down 6.2% on the week.

How the run unfolded

PEARL started Monday near $2.05 and rose through the early hours, reaching about $2.38 by 7 a.m. ET, according to hourly prices on CoinGecko. It held between $2.28 and $2.40 for most of the day, then pushed higher in the afternoon. CoinGecko logs the new record of $2.48 at 4:55 p.m. ET.

The high did not last. PEARL fell to about $2.31 by 6 p.m. ET, a drop of about 7% in one hour, and traded near $2.29 at 7:10 p.m. ET.

A gain against a flat market

The rest of the market did not join in. Bitcoin traded near $61,090, about unchanged over 24 hours and down 6.2% over seven days, and ether near $1,705, down 1% in a day and 6.4% on the week, CoinGecko data show. PEARL has now risen about 44% in seven days, extending the run our November 20 story covered, when it fell to $1.85 before bouncing.

CoinGecko's rolling 24-hour trading volume for PEARL was about $50 million at 7:10 p.m. ET.

The supply picture

CoinGecko describes Pearl as a proof-of-useful-work network whose GPU miners are rewarded for the matrix math behind AI models. Only about 388.4 million PEARL circulate today, CoinGecko data show, against a hard cap of 2.1 billion, or roughly 18.5%. Valued at $2.29 each, all 2.1 billion coins would be worth about $4.81 billion.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the record matters less for its size, a few cents above October's high, than for what it shows: PEARL has recovered all of its November 20 drop and more while bitcoin fell for the week.
  • The quick 7% hourly fall after the high suggests sellers were waiting near the old record. A new high that gives back gains this fast is not yet a breakout.
  • Supply is the long-term question: with about 81% of coins still to be mined, new coins keep reaching miners, who often sell some to cover costs.

What's next

  • Whether PEARL closes a day above the old $2.46 record
  • Whether it holds above Monday's 8 a.m. ET dip near $2.28
  • Whether 24-hour volume rises above $50 million on any new push
