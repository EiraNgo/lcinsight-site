Unichain sharks sold $2.12 million of USD₮0, three days after sharks bought $3.74 million

The flow on Unichain's dollar token flipped. A wallet that was not among Friday's buyers did almost all the selling.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • In the 24 hours to 8:49 p.m. ET on November 9, 2026, two shark wallets sold $2.12 million of USD₮0 on Unichain and bought $355,375, in 47 trades, DeepSea shows.
  • One wallet, 0x65…15d8, sold $1.97 million in 7 trades. It was not one of the two buyers in our November 6 story.
  • On November 6 DeepSea showed the reverse: $3.74 million bought and $75,308 sold.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's live feed puts USD₮0 on Unichain on its 24-hour selling list at 8:49 p.m. ET on November 9: $2,120,062 sold and $355,375 bought, by 2 wallets in 47 trades. Net: about minus $1.76 million. USD₮0 is a version of Tether's USDT that moves between chains. The 47 is a trade count, not a count of wallets.

The wallet DeepSea shortens to 0x65…15d8 sold $1,967,654 and bought $112,051 in 7 trades. The wallet 0xdc…8b87 made 40 trades, $243,324 bought and $152,408 sold. DeepSea gives no name for either. USD₮0 was the only Unichain token in the shark lists, and the feed does not show what the sellers received.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 9, 2026, 8:49 p.m. ET.

How it compares with Friday

On November 6 we reported that two sharks bought $3.74 million of USD₮0 on Unichain in a day. The bigger buyer, 0x61…b09d (that story called it 611f), bought $2.45 million in 2 trades. The other buyer was 0xdc…8b87, the same smaller wallet that is still trading now. 0x61…b09d does not appear in Monday's Unichain list, and the main seller, 0x65…15d8, did not appear on Friday. So the selling does not look like the same buyer reversing its trade, at least among the wallets DeepSea tracks.

In the last hour before 8:49 p.m. ET, DeepSea showed no Unichain shark trades.

Small track records

DeepSea's profit table shows only a short history for the seller: 4 closed trades in 7 days, 1 winner, for minus $108.48. 0xdc…8b87 is at plus $96.14 over 30 days from 111 closed trades. Neither number says much about whether the big sale was a good call. Swapping a dollar token rarely makes or loses much by itself.

DeepSea's token page still showed a 7-day whale net flow of plus $6.18 million for USD₮0 on Unichain after the sale, so over the week big wallets remain net buyers.

Checked against CoinGecko

CoinGecko showed USDT0 at $0.9988 at 8:54 p.m. ET, with a market value of $3.36 billion and $83.7 million of 24-hour volume across all chains. The price barely moved, so the sale was about moving dollars, not a bet on USD₮0's price.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the Unichain flow is one or two wallets moving money in and out, not a broad trend. The buyers on Friday and the main seller on Monday are different addresses.
  • Selling a stablecoin usually means swapping into something else or moving it away. Until DeepSea shows the other side, the destination is unknown.

What's next

  • Whether 0x65…15d8 shows up buying another token on Unichain
  • Whether DeepSea's 7-day whale flow for USD₮0 on Unichain (plus $6.18 million) starts to shrink
