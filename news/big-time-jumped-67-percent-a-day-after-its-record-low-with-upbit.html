Big Time jumped 67% a day after its record low, with Upbit taking 60% of the trading

The gaming token rose from $0.0041 to about $0.0068 late Tuesday, most of it in a 47% jump in one quarter hour after 11 p.m. ET. Volume hit roughly ten times a normal day, and one Korean exchange, Upbit, carried 60% of it.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • BIGTIME was at $0.0068 at 12:39 a.m. ET on Wednesday, November 25, up 67.2% in 24 hours, CoinGecko shows. It set a record low of $0.003827 at 5:52 p.m. ET on Monday.
  • The big step came at 11:15 p.m. ET Tuesday: $0.00449 to $0.00659 in 15 minutes, a 47% jump in CoinGecko's five-minute prices.
  • Upbit's BIGTIME/KRW pair traded $45.9 million of the $76.3 million on CoinGecko's ticker list, about 60%. The token is still 99.3% below its 2023 record.

From record low to top of the trending list

Big Time is the token of a free-to-play action game of the same name, issued on Ethereum. On Monday, November 23, at 5:52 p.m. ET it hit $0.003827, its lowest price ever, according to CoinGecko. New to Korea's crypto market, where most of this trading happened? Read how South Korea's crypto rules work.

A day later it took off. At 12:39 a.m. ET on Wednesday, November 25, BIGTIME traded at $0.0068, up 67.2% in 24 hours, with a 24-hour high of $0.00704. Its market value was $19.9 million, rank 732 on CoinGecko. At the same hour it sat first on CoinGecko's trending list, ahead of bitcoin, Monad and Pudgy Penguins. Bitcoin itself was down 2.8% on the day.

Most of it came in 15 minutes

CoinGecko's five-minute prices show the rise came in two steps. The token sat near $0.0041 all day Tuesday. After 7 p.m. ET it climbed to $0.00455 by 7:45 p.m. and held between $0.0044 and $0.0047 until 11 p.m.

Then, between 11 p.m. and 11:15 p.m. ET, it went from $0.00449 to $0.00659, up 47% in one quarter hour. It dipped to $0.00607 by 11:45 p.m. and reached $0.00688 at 12:15 a.m. ET Wednesday.

Volume and who traded it

In the 13 days from November 12 to 24, BIGTIME traded between $7.2 million and $8.3 million a day in CoinGecko's daily data. The rolling 24-hour count stood at $12.3 million at 7 p.m. ET Tuesday, $27.9 million at 11 p.m. and $73.9 million by 12:30 a.m. ET, about ten times a normal day.

CoinGecko's ticker list adds up to $76.3 million across all markets. Upbit's BIGTIME/KRW pair, priced in Korean won, took $45.9 million of it, or 60%. LBank's USDT pair had $11.2 million, Binance $5.5 million and OKX $2.4 million. Upbit's price, $0.006854, was about 1.7% above Binance's $0.006742 at the same minute.

Still a long way down

Even after the jump, BIGTIME is 99.3% below its record of $0.977 from December 5, 2023, per CoinGecko. About 2.93 billion of its 5 billion maximum tokens are in circulation. Over 30 days the token is up just 2.4%, so the move only takes it back to where it was in late October.

The price and volume data do not show why buyers arrived. This story reports the move, not a cause.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think a move this size in a $20 million token says more about where it trades than about the game. When 60% of volume sits on one won market, a burst of local buying can lift the global price within minutes.
  • The likely test is whether volume stays near $70 million once the first day passes. If the volume fades, the price has little to hold it up.
  • A 67% day still leaves BIGTIME 99.3% below its 2023 record. Over 30 days it is up only 2.4%, so the jump only takes it back to where it stood a month ago.

What's next

  • Whether Upbit's share of volume falls back as other venues catch up
  • Any statement from the Big Time team that explains the move
  • Whether the price holds above Tuesday's $0.0044 to $0.0047 range
