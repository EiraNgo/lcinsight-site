Lighter's LIT rose 28% from Monday's low, above its price before the $10.4 million loss

LIT traded at $3.00 early Tuesday, CoinGecko data show, about 9% above where it stood before Lighter froze withdrawals. Daily volume is about four times its pre-incident level.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • LIT, the token of the Lighter exchange, traded at $3.00 at 12:42 a.m. ET on November 17, 2026, up 11.2% in 24 hours, CoinGecko data show.
  • That is about 28% above its 24-hour low of $2.35 and about 9% above the $2.75 it showed at 5 a.m. ET on Monday, an hour before Lighter paused withdrawals.
  • 24-hour volume reached $184.9 million, about four times the level before the incident. OKX, DigiFinex and Bybit handled 47% of it.

Back above the pre-incident price

Lighter is an exchange for perpetual futures (bets on prices with no expiry date). On Monday, November 16, it paused all withdrawals after a leaked API key let someone drain about $10.4 million from one trader's account, as we reported.

CoinGecko's hourly prices show LIT at $2.75 at 5 a.m. ET on Monday and $2.61 an hour later, after the pause. The 24-hour low on CoinGecko is $2.35.

LIT then climbed most of the day. It was near $2.90 by 6 p.m. ET and read $3.00 at 12:42 a.m. ET on Tuesday, with a 24-hour high of $3.08. That puts it about 28% above the low and about 9% above its price before the incident. LIT was also on CoinGecko's trending list.

Where it traded

CoinGecko counted $184.9 million of LIT trading in 24 hours, against a market value of $750.6 million. Before the incident, its 24-hour volume reading was about $44 million to $46 million.

By CoinGecko's exchange list, OKX handled 19.1% of the volume, DigiFinex 15.9% and Bybit 11.8%. Coinbase had 5.2% and Lighter's own spot market 3.8%.

Still far below its record

LIT's record is $7.86, set on December 29, 2025 (ET). At $3.00 it is about 62% below that. Over seven days it is still down 4.7%.

What we could not confirm

We could not confirm whether Lighter has reopened withdrawals. The rise in LIT's price does not tell us that either way. We also did not check Lighter's deposits on chain for this piece.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the speed of the rebound stands out: within a day LIT was back above its pre-incident price, even though we could not confirm that withdrawals had reopened.
  • Most of the trading ran on large exchanges, not on Lighter itself, so the rebound says more about outside traders than about Lighter's own users.
  • The real test is withdrawals. Until they reopen, users cannot move funds out, whatever LIT's price does.

What's next

  • A Lighter post saying withdrawals are back.
  • Whether the stolen funds move again on chain.
  • Whether LIT holds above $2.75, its price before the incident.
