Influencer slingoor turned a $686,000 week into $9.38 million as bitcoin fell

DeepSea's 7-day KOL board changed fast in the November 5 sell-off. The top two influencers now hold 91% of the profit shown in the top 100.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • slingoor's 7-day PnL on DeepSea rose from +$685,600 on November 5 to +$9.38 million on November 6, lifting it from third to second.
  • Leader 2 freres 2 fauves rose from +$8.84 million to +$12.72 million. Its 30-day figure is now +$15.87 million.
  • Together the two show $22.10 million, about 91% of the $24.27 million made by the 100 KOLs listed.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's KOLs & Sharks leaderboard ranks the known wallets of crypto influencers (KOLs, or key opinion leaders) by profit and loss (PnL). LC Insight read the 7-day view at 12:35 a.m. ET on November 6.

2 freres 2 fauves leads at +$12.72 million, up from +$8.84 million at 12:30 a.m. ET on November 5. slingoor is second at +$9.38 million, up from +$685,600. bizyugo.hl, second yesterday at +$2.86 million, is no longer in the top 10. Frank (+$332,500) and Chairman ² (+$283,700) are unchanged in third and fourth.

DeepSea's 30-day figures: 2 freres 2 fauves +$15.87 million, slingoor +$12.28 million, with a 23% win rate on DeepSea. Of the 100 KOLs shown, 99 are in profit, together about $24.27 million.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 6, 2026, 12:35 a.m. ET.

A board that rests on two wallets

The gap below the top two is wide. Third place made $332,500, less than 4% of slingoor's week. From tenth place down, every KOL shown made under $65,000. The figures cover only influencer wallets DeepSea knows, and the board ranks winners, so it says little about how influencers did on average.

What an explorer shows

DeepSea's live feed has tagged one Hyperliquid wallet as 2 freres 2 fauves. Hyperliquid is a perpetual futures exchange (bets on prices with no expiry date). Etherscan's multichain view puts that wallet at about $11.05 million, $10.43 million of it on HyperCore, with 17,157 transactions. DeepSea's Smart Money board lists the account at $32.58 million in total assets. The tools may count open perp positions, margin or linked wallets differently, so the gap does not by itself show a withdrawal. LC Insight does not publish the full wallet address.

DeepSea's page text did not give slingoor's wallet, so LC Insight could not check it on an explorer.

The market behind it

CoinGecko showed bitcoin at $75,552 at 12:46 a.m. ET on November 6, down 6.0% in 24 hours, after trading above $80,000 on November 4 and 5. DeepSea shows the profit, not the trades behind it.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the board shows how much one volatile day can reshuffle a weekly ranking: slingoor's week grew about 14 times in less than 24 hours.
  • A 23% win rate on DeepSea next to a $12.28 million month suggests a few large winning trades, not steady small gains. That pattern can reverse quickly.
  • Copying a trader from a leaderboard means entering after the move. This is not a signal to follow anyone.

What's next

  • Whether slingoor keeps second place when the November 5 swing rolls out of the 7-day window
  • The 30-day board, where 2 freres 2 fauves leads at +$15.87 million
  • Whether the 2 freres 2 fauves wallet balance on Etherscan's multichain view moves
