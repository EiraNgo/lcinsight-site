DeepSea's top 10 smart money traders: $256 million in weekly profit, then $110 million

The 7-day profit shown for the crash-week winners on DeepSea's boards fell by more than half in 24 hours. The top two influencers' figures halved, and sharks are still down $3.15 million on closed trades.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • The top 10 on DeepSea's 7-day Smart Money board show $110.08 million of profit, down from $255.77 million at 12:37 a.m. ET on November 6.
  • Former leader SMc060 fell from +$59.67 million to +$9.49 million. The top two KOLs, slingoor and 2 freres 2 fauves, now show $5.95 million and $5.60 million, about half of a day earlier.
  • In DeepSea's realized profit table, 67 of 96 shark wallets with a 7-day figure lost money, a net $3.15 million.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's Smart Money leaderboard ranks wallets with a strong trading record by profit and loss (PnL). On the 7-day view, all chains, read at 12:36 a.m. ET on November 7, the top 10 show $110.08 million of profit on $990.33 million of total assets. A day earlier the same ten slots showed $255.77 million.

All ten are still Hyperliquid perp traders. Perps (perpetual futures) are bets on a price with no expiry date, often with borrowed money. SM6e36 now leads at +$20.53 million, down from +$53.98 million. SMc060, first yesterday at +$59.67 million, is sixth at +$9.49 million, while its assets barely moved ($221.14 million to $214.10 million). The first 48 rows are all Hyperliquid wallets; the first other name, DeeZe on Robinhood, is No. 49.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 7, 2026, 12:36 a.m. ET.

Influencers: the top two halved

On DeepSea's KOLs & Sharks board, 7-day view, slingoor now leads at +$5.95 million, down from +$9.38 million. 2 freres 2 fauves, first yesterday at +$12.72 million, is second at +$5.60 million. Below them, little changed: Frank (+$332,500) and Chairman ² (+$283,700) are where they were. The top 10 KOLs (crypto influencers, or key opinion leaders) together show about $12.89 million.

Sharks: still behind on closed trades

DeepSea's realized PnL table counts closed trades only. In the version made at 12:34 a.m. ET, 96 shark wallets (large, active traders) have a 7-day figure: 67 lost money, 27 made money and 2 were flat, for a net minus $3.15 million. A day earlier, 100 sharks showed minus $5.85 million. Their 7,229 closed trades won 44% of the time.

The best was a BSC wallet ending in 8771, up $1.49 million on 3,109 trades with a 47% win rate. The worst is the same Optimism wallet ending in 4dea that led the losses yesterday: minus $1.60 million, with 3 winning trades out of 59.

Why it matters

Prices barely moved in the same day. CoinGecko showed bitcoin at $75,947 at 12:43 a.m. ET on November 7, up 0.5% in 24 hours, and ether at $2,246, up 1.1%. So a big price swing does not explain the $146 million drop in 7-day profit. DeepSea's page does not say how the 7-day figure is updated, so the change may reflect closed positions, price marks or the window rolling forward. We checked prices on CoinGecko; LC Insight could not reach the Hyperliquid API in this run to check single wallets.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the lesson is that 7-day leaderboards move fast. A wallet that tops the board one day can drop to sixth the next while its assets barely change.
  • The KOL board shows the same pattern: a few big Hyperliquid accounts drive most of the total, so the totals swing with them.
  • Sharks are a different story: their losses are realized, so they stay. Fewer of them lost money than a day ago, but most are still behind.

What's next

  • Whether SM6e36 and SMffa1 hold the top spots
  • Whether slingoor and 2 freres 2 fauves keep falling on the 7-day view
  • Whether shark realized losses shrink below $3 million
