WLFI lost half its price in 75 minutes and hit a new record low of $0.0167

World Liberty Financial's WLFI token fell from about 3.5 cents to 1.67 cents between 11:30 p.m. ET Friday and 12:46 a.m. ET Saturday. That is 58% below the record low it set just four days earlier. We found no official explanation.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows WLFI fell from about $0.0345 at 11:30 p.m. ET on November 27 to a record low of $0.01668 at 12:46 a.m. ET on November 28, a drop of about 52% in 75 minutes.
  • At 1:02 a.m. ET WLFI traded near $0.0194, down 53.7% in 24 hours. Its market value fell to about $615 million, rank No. 90, and it was No. 1 on CoinGecko's trending list.
  • The new low is 58% below the previous record of $0.03991 from November 24. WLFI is now about 94% below its high of $0.331 from September 1, 2025.

What moved

WLFI is the governance token of World Liberty Financial, the project that also issues the USD1 dollar stablecoin. On Friday, November 27, it was already slipping: CoinGecko data put it near $0.0419 at 1 a.m. ET and near $0.0345 by 11:30 p.m. ET, a loss of about 18% over the day.

Then it broke. In five-minute data the price went to about $0.0315 at 11:35 p.m. ET and about $0.0250 at 11:45 p.m. ET. After a short bounce it kept falling past midnight and reached about $0.0177 in the chart at 12:45 a.m. ET. CoinGecko records the all-time low as $0.01668 at 12:46 a.m. ET. At 1:02 a.m. ET WLFI was near $0.0194, down 53.7% in 24 hours and 55.4% in 7 days.

Trading jumped with the fall

CoinGecko's rolling 24-hour WLFI volume was about $85 million at 11:30 p.m. ET. By 1 a.m. ET it was about $243 million, so roughly $158 million of trading came in the 90 minutes of the drop. The latest 24-hour figure, about $246.5 million, equals about 40% of WLFI's market value of $615 million.

WLFI was No. 1 on CoinGecko's trending list at 1:04 a.m. ET. USD1, the project's stablecoin, was No. 7. USD1 traded at about $0.9969, with a market value of about $2.84 billion.

The market was falling too, but much less

Bitcoin was down 3.3% in 24 hours at about $59,698 and ether down 4.9% at about $1,654, per CoinGecko at 1:04 a.m. ET. A broad dip of a few percent does not explain a token losing half its price in about an hour, so this is mainly a WLFI story.

It is also the second record low in four days. On November 24 WLFI set a low of $0.03991, which we covered with on-chain data on shark wallets. Saturday's low is 58% under that level.

What we could not check

We found no statement from World Liberty Financial explaining the drop by publish time. Market data shows how far and how fast the price fell, but not who sold or why. We will update this story if the project, an exchange or on-chain data shows a clear cause.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a 52% fall in 75 minutes on a day when bitcoin lost about 3% points to selling aimed at WLFI itself, not a market-wide panic. This is not investment advice.
  • Moves this fast in thin overnight trading often come from a few large orders or forced selling, and they can partly reverse just as quickly. The quick bounce from $0.0167 to about $0.0194 shows how jumpy the price still is.
  • USD1 holding close to $1 while WLFI halved suggests the stablecoin and the governance token are being treated separately by traders for now. That is worth watching if the selling continues.

What's next

  • Whether WLFI holds above the $0.01668 record low
  • Any statement from World Liberty Financial on its official channels
  • Whether USD1 stays near $1 and whether its supply keeps shrinking
