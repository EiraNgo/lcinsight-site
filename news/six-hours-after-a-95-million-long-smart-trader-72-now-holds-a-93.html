Six hours after a $95 million long, Smart trader #72 now holds a $93 million bitcoin short

DeepSea's top bitcoin traders on Hyperliquid went from 82% short to 91% short overnight. The biggest new bet, a $92.76 million short at 40x, sits under the same label that held the biggest long at 12:39 a.m.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • Bitcoin traded at $61,722 at 6:48 a.m. ET on Friday, down 3.6% in a day and 10.2% in a week, CoinGecko shows. The 24-hour low was $61,273.
  • DeepSea's BTC-PERP page at 6:37 a.m. ET counted 93 top Hyperliquid wallets 91% short: $755.29 million short against $78.92 million long. At 12:39 a.m. it was 82% short.
  • Smart trader #72 now holds a $92.76 million short at 40x from $61,823. At 12:39 a.m. the same label showed a $95.45 million long from $61,702.

What DeepSea's data shows

DeepSea's BTC-PERP page lists the open bitcoin bets of the biggest traders it tracks on Hyperliquid, an on-chain futures exchange. At 6:37 a.m. ET on Friday it counted 93 wallets: 91% short (bets on a fall) with $755.29 million at an average entry of $69,766, and 9% long with $78.92 million at an average of $69,431.

At 12:39 a.m. ET, in our earlier check, the page showed 94 wallets, 82% short: $666.32 million short and $147.57 million long. In six hours the long side shrank by $68.65 million and the short side grew by $88.97 million.

The largest bet on the page is now Smart trader #72: a $92.76 million short at 40x leverage, entered at $61,823, with a liquidation price of $63,169 and a $29,300 paper loss. At 12:39 a.m. ET the label Smart trader #72 held the page's largest long, $95.45 million at 40x from $61,702.

Data: DeepSea (desea.io), checked November 20, 2026, 6:37 a.m. ET.

New bets at the low

Three other large bets on the page were opened within about $200 of the current price, so they look new. Smart trader #127 holds a $35.16 million short at 12x from $61,761. On the other side, a wallet DeepSea labels Auros holds a $21.35 million long at 40x from $61,934, with liquidation at $50,416, and Smart trader #10 holds a $15.17 million long at 20x from $61,835. Each of the three showed a paper gain or loss of less than $52,000.

The older shorts sit on gains. John Wick shows $56.08 million short from $69,335, up $6.84 million, and Smart trader #13 $53.72 million from $78,440, up $14.48 million.

In our screenshot at 6:52 a.m. ET the board had barely moved: $755.04 million short and $78.09 million long, with #72's short at $92.67 million.

Same label, same wallet?

DeepSea links today's Smart trader #72 to the Hyperliquid wallet 0x1f25…f925, which its public dataset lists as unlabeled. Our 12:39 a.m. check did not record the wallet behind the long. So we cannot confirm that one trader closed the long and opened the short, only that the label switched sides at almost the same price. Hyperliquid's data feed and block explorers were out of reach for us this morning.

The wider market

On CoinGecko's Hyperliquid page, read at 6:49 a.m. ET, the BTC-USD contract had $1.23 billion in open interest and $5.21 billion of 24-hour trading. DeepSea's page shows twice that, $2.45 billion, since the two tally open bets in different ways.

Across every Hyperliquid perpetual market, DefiLlama counted $7.712 billion of open interest and $13.709 billion of daily volume.

A liquidation price is where the exchange closes a bet because its margin is used up. At 40x, a 2.2% rise from $61,823 would reach #72's level.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the overnight shift matters more than any single bet: the tracked long side nearly halved while bitcoin barely moved, so these traders did not buy the dip as a group.
  • The newest bets on both sides were opened at almost the same price, which leaves them with very little room. A move of a few percent either way could force one side out.
  • Until the wallet history is visible, the #72 switch is best read as a change in the board, not as proof of one trader's change of mind.

What's next

  • Whether bitcoin moves toward $63,169, Smart trader #72's liquidation price.
  • Whether the long side on DeepSea's board keeps shrinking below $78.92 million.
  • Whether bitcoin breaks Friday's $61,273 low.
