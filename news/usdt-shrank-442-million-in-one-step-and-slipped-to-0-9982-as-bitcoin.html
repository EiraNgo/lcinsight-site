USDT shrank $442 million in one step and slipped to $0.9982 as bitcoin crashed

CoinGecko's data show USDT's supply flat for a day, then down $442 million Friday evening. USDC fell $649 million and won back $418 million. Tether's Ethereum treasury is down to $862 million.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • USDT's circulating supply on CoinGecko sat at $175.25 billion for more than a day, then fell to $174.80 billion between 7 p.m. and 8 p.m. ET Friday.
  • USDT traded as low as $0.9982 in the day to 12:45 a.m. ET Saturday, below the $0.9993 it showed on November 13.
  • Etherscan shows Tether's Ethereum treasury wallet at 861.98 million USDT, down from 4.65 billion on November 13.

What moved

Stablecoins are tokens built to stay at $1. Their supply grows when people hand dollars to the issuer and shrinks when they cash tokens back in, so the totals are a rough gauge of money entering or leaving crypto.

CoinGecko's hourly data show USDT's circulating supply at $175.25 billion through all of Thursday and until 7 p.m. ET Friday, November 20. By 8 p.m. ET it read $174.80 billion, about $442 million less, and it was still there at 12:43 a.m. ET Saturday. Bitcoin was near $58,800 at the time of the drop and about $55,900 by early Saturday.

USDT's price also slipped. CoinGecko put it at $0.9989 early Saturday, with a 24-hour low of $0.9982. On November 13 it had shown $0.9993.

USDC fell, then came back

Circle's USDC moved more but ended closer to where it started. In CoinGecko's hourly data its supply fell from $68.68 billion at 2 p.m. ET Thursday to $68.03 billion at 3 p.m. ET Friday, a drop of about $649 million, as bitcoin slid from about $62,800 to $58,500.

Then it rose about $418 million to $68.45 billion by 10 p.m. ET. At 12:43 a.m. ET Saturday it stood at $68.37 billion, about $305 million below Thursday afternoon. In the same hourly data, USDC's price stayed between $0.9992 and $0.9997.

Tether's treasury wallet keeps emptying

Tether keeps a stock of USDT in a wallet that Etherscan labels Tether: Treasury. At 12:45 a.m. ET Saturday it held 861,975,521 USDT, and DeepSea's wallet page showed the same $861.98 million at 12:57 a.m. On November 13 we reported 4.65 billion there, so about $3.79 billion has left in eight days.

CoinGecko lists USDT's total supply at $180.27 billion against $174.80 billion circulating, a $5.47 billion gap, the same size as on November 13.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • USDT's supply sat unchanged for more than a day and then moved in one step, so CoinGecko's figure appears to update in batches rather than hour by hour. The size of the drop is the solid part; the exact hour it happened is less certain.
  • USDC's drop and quick rebound suggests dollars left and came back within hours. The data do not show who moved them or why.
  • A price of $0.9982 is a small gap from $1, but it is wider than a week ago and worth watching while prices fall.

What's next

  • Whether USDT's supply moves again from $174.80 billion.
  • USDT's price against $0.998.
  • The Tether: Treasury balance against 862 million USDT.
