Bless (BLESS) more than doubled in a day, and it is still 91% below its 2025 record

BLESS climbed from about $0.0090 to a peak near $0.0194 in 15 hours on Monday, with a single PancakeSwap pool carrying about 41% of the trading.

LC Insight Editorial Team2 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko shows BLESS at $0.01888 at 8:02 p.m. ET on November 16, 2026, up 105.7% in 24 hours and No. 3 on its trending list.
  • It rose from a low of about $0.00901 at 8:50 p.m. ET on Sunday to a peak of about $0.01940 at 12:10 p.m. ET on Monday, a gain of about 115%.
  • About $20.7 million traded in 24 hours against a market value near $34.8 million. One PancakeSwap pool on BNB Chain carried about 41% of it.

The move

Bless Network's token BLESS sat near $0.0091 through Sunday evening, November 15, 2026, according to CoinGecko. It touched its low of the day, about $0.00901, at 8:50 p.m. ET. The climb started around 3 a.m. ET on Monday and ran steadily through the morning: about $0.0102 at 4 a.m., $0.0128 at 7 a.m., $0.0172 at 10 a.m. and a peak of about $0.01940 at 12:10 p.m. ET.

From the Sunday low to that peak is a rise of about 115%. At 8:02 p.m. ET BLESS was at $0.01888, up 105.7% in 24 hours, so it has held most of the gain for eight hours. Over the same 24 hours CoinGecko shows bitcoin down 0.5% and ether up 0.1%, so the jump was BLESS's own, not the market's.

The bigger picture is still rough. BLESS set its record of about $0.218 on October 15, 2025, and is still about 91% below it. It is up 139.9% over seven days and 153.9% over 30 days, from a low of about $0.00395 on June 5, 2026.

Where it traded

CoinGecko shows about $20.7 million of BLESS volume in 24 hours. Of the trading it lists by market, the BLESS/WBNB pool on PancakeSwap V3 (BNB Chain) handled about $9.3 million (41.3%), Gate about $4.2 million (18.5%) and Bitget about $3.5 million (15.6%). Kraken, MEXC, LBank and Toobit followed with smaller amounts.

That daily volume is about 60% of the token's market value, so the price is moving on heavy turnover in a small float, which can swing sharply in either direction.

What Bless is, and the supply

CoinGecko describes Bless Network as a decentralized computing platform that pools idle capacity from laptops, desktops and phones into a shared computer, founded in San Francisco in 2022 under the name Blockless. The token lives on Solana (A1t2...N49S) and BNB Chain (0x7c82...e11f).

CoinGecko counts about 1.84 billion BLESS in circulation out of a 10 billion maximum, about 18%. At $0.01888 that is a market value of about $34.8 million (No. 521 on CoinGecko) and a fully diluted value of about $189 million. Bless has not given a reason for the jump in any source we checked, and this story does not suggest one.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • In our view, a token with only about 18% of its supply in circulation that doubles in a day, with the biggest share of trading in one decentralized pool, is a fragile setup. The remaining 82% of supply is a long-term overhang, and fast rallies in small tokens often give back part of the gain. This is not investment advice.

What's next

  • Whether BLESS holds above $0.0172, where it traded before the last leg up at 10 a.m. ET
  • Whether trading spreads beyond the PancakeSwap pool
  • Any official statement from Bless Network about the move
