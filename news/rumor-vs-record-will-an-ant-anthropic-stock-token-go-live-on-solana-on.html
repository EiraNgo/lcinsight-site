Rumor vs record: will an "ANT" Anthropic stock token go live on Solana on IPO day?

A viral post says a tokenized Anthropic share called ANT will launch on Solana the day Anthropic starts trading in public. CoinGecko does list an Anthropic xStock with a Solana address, but under a different ticker, with zero supply and no price. We found no Anthropic or issuer statement that sets a date.

LC Insight Editorial Team3 min read

Key points

  • CoinGecko lists "Anthropic xStock" with the ticker ANTX, not ANT. It shows a Solana contract address plus 10 other networks, a circulating supply of 0 and no price, as of 12:43 a.m. ET on November 12.
  • A separate token, "Anthropic PreStocks" (ticker ANTHROPIC), already trades on Solana at about $879, CoinGecko data show. It is not a share issued by Anthropic.
  • We found no Anthropic statement or SEC filing in our checks that sets a first day of public trading, so any launch tied to that day is unverified.

Why we did this check

A claim has spread on social media this week: a tokenized Anthropic share, ticker "ANT," will go live on Solana on the same day Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI models, starts trading on a stock exchange. Tokenized stocks are blockchain tokens built to track the price of a share. Some are backed by real shares held by an issuer. Others only follow a price.

We checked each part against records we could open ourselves: CoinGecko's listings and what Anthropic and the token issuers have published. We did not use news reports. For an earlier edition of this series, see Rumor vs record: is a hacker really selling 335.9 million Binance user records?

What the record shows

CoinGecko has a page for Anthropic xStock. Its ticker is ANTX. The page lists contract addresses on 11 networks, including Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Arbitrum and X Layer, and links to xstocks.fi. CoinGecko marks it as a preview listing.

At 12:43 a.m. ET on November 12, the page showed a circulating supply of 0, no price, no market value and no trading volume. In plain terms, the token address exists in CoinGecko's data, but nothing on the record shows it trading yet, and nothing on the page gives a launch date.

The token that already trades

A different token, Anthropic PreStocks, ticker ANTHROPIC, does trade on Solana. CoinGecko put it at $878.96, down 0.9% in 24 hours, with about $646,000 in volume and a market value of about $7.1 million, at 12:44 a.m. ET on November 12. Its listed home page is on prestocks.com.

This kind of pre-IPO token tracks an estimated value of a private company. It is not a share that Anthropic has issued, and buyers do not become Anthropic shareholders through it on the record we checked.

What would settle it

A US listing starts with a registration statement that the SEC publishes on EDGAR, and a first trading day comes only after the offering is priced. An issuer like xStocks would then normally post its own launch notice. Until Anthropic, the SEC record or the issuer says so, the IPO-day launch remains a claim, not a fact.

LC Insight analysis

This section is our reading of the facts above, not a statement of fact.

  • We think the rumor took off because a real CoinGecko preview page exists. A placeholder with a Solana address is easy to read as a launch date, but it is not one.
  • The likely risk is lookalike tokens. When a big name trends, copycat tokens with the same name appear on Solana, so the contract address on the issuer's own page is the thing to check.
  • The pre-IPO token trading at about $879 shows there is demand, but it tracks an estimated value from a third party. It is not Anthropic stock.

What's next

  • Any Anthropic announcement or SEC filing about a public listing
  • An xStocks notice for ANTX, and its CoinGecko supply turning above zero
  • New Anthropic-named tokens on Solana with no issuer page behind them
